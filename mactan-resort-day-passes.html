Mactan Resort Day Passes: Rates, Inclusions and Tips

Ren Calago 11 November 20260

You can spend a resort day on Mactan without paying for a room. Several beachfront properties in Lapu-Lapu City sell day-use passes that cover a few hours of pool and beach time, and some add lunch, a dining credit, towels or a short list of activities. Prices run from about ₱1,700 to ₱2,500 per adult on the listed rates, so the real question is what each pass actually includes.


The resorts split into three groups. The big names around Punta Engaño (Shangri-La, Crimson, Dusit Thani) sell a polished pool-and-beach day. Bluewater in Maribago sells a simple beach-and-buffet package. Plantation Bay, farther south in Marigondon, is built around its lagoons and is the one to pick if you want to stay busy.


This guide compares eight options, shows what to add on top of the pass price, and lists the questions worth asking before you pay. Rates move with promotions and peak dates, so treat every price here as a planning figure and get the current one in writing from the resort.

The short answer


For a full day of things to do, book Plantation Bay: lunch, a drink, the lagoons and several activities sit inside one ₱2,500 price. For the easiest budget pick, Bluewater Maribago charges ₱1,700 on weekdays with a buffet included. If you want a calm, upscale pool day and will pay for it, choose among Shangri-La, Crimson and Dusit Thani depending on whether you care more about sports, dining credit or a scheduled meal.


Costabella, BE Resort and JPark are worth a message, but ask for the current package before you plan around them.

The resorts, one by one


Premium beach day

Shangri-La Mactan

Punta Engaño

The Beach Buddies Explorer Day Pass gets you onto the beach and into the pool, plus free badminton, table tennis, archery, water volleyball, water basketball and mini golf. Selected non-motorised water sports, tennis court rental and golf carry a 10% discount. Meals are not included, so budget lunch separately. The Adventure Zone and E-Zone are charged at regular rates.

Rate: ₱2,000 weekdays, ₱2,500 weekends per adult; ages 6 to 12 at ₱1,000; age 5 and under free with a paying adult (one child per adult). Open 9 AM to 6 PM; reserve at least 24 hours ahead and wait for confirmation.

Pool and dining credit

Crimson Resort & Spa Mactan

Seascapes Resort Town, Punta Engaño

Crimson's day-use vouchers cover the beach, the infinity pool, the Crimzone pool and a dining credit at Azure Beach Club. Access generally runs from 9 AM to 6 PM. Towels and lockers come with a refundable deposit, and the Saturday offer includes a foam party, which suits groups of friends more than anyone after a quiet afternoon.

Check: the value of the credit, whether it is per person, which outlets take it, whether unused credit expires, and any blackout dates. Advance reservation is required.

Two pools, set meal

Dusit Thani Mactan Cebu

Punta Engaño

The day-use offer opens the infinity pool and rainforest pool from 9 AM to 6 PM and the beach from 9 AM to 5 PM, with Wi-Fi, towels, showers and changing rooms. The meal depends on the day: food-and-beverage credit on selected weekdays, a Thai tasting menu on Tuesday, a lunch buffet on Saturday and a family-style Thai set on Sunday.

Check: the rate and the meal for your exact date, whether payment is needed in advance, and how children are priced.

Most to do

Plantation Bay Resort & Spa

Marigondon

The Daytripper Adventure Package is built around the saltwater lagoons and swimming pools rather than a single beach strip. The price includes lunch and a drink, wall climbing during set hours, 30 minutes of bicycle use, 15 minutes on a stand-up paddleboard or kayak, and showers and changing rooms. The resort has rules on noise, dress and behaviour, so read them before booking and confirm the activity schedule.

Rate: ₱2,500 net per person; ₱2,000 for groups of 20 or more booked in advance; children 0 to 12 pay half.

Beach and buffet

Bluewater Maribago Beach Resort

Buyong–Maribago

The Dali Na sa Bluewater package is the plainest deal on the list. Day use runs 9 AM to 5 PM, and a night-use slot runs 1 PM to 9 PM. Both include a buffet and access to the beach and main pool. Towels are available against a refundable deposit. Outside food and drinks are generally not allowed, except bottled water for personal use.

Rate: ₱1,700 weekdays, ₱1,900 weekends and holidays per person; children 5 to 11 pay half; age 4 and under free.

Quieter pick

Costabella Tropical Beach Hotel

Buyong

Costabella is a more traditional beachfront resort with a pool and a day-trip option, and it feels smaller than the big Punta Engaño properties. Day-trip packages change by date and promotion, so message the resort for the current written offer and ask what meal it covers, whether towels are included, and when day visitors can use the facilities.

Ask about: weekend, holiday and high-occupancy rates.

Casual beachfront

BE Resort Mactan

Punta Engaño

BE Resort promotes day use for non-overnight guests, with a beachfront area, main pool, kiddie pool, game room and on-site dining. Water sports and other activities may be sold separately. Since there is no fixed day-pass price to quote, ask for the current rate and for a list of what it covers.

Ask about: food, water sports and any area closed to day visitors.

Waterpark

JPark Island Resort & Waterpark

Maribago

JPark is the obvious name for families who want slides and themed pools instead of a quiet beach. Day access is not something to assume, though. Contact the resort first and ask whether it sells day-use for your date, which pools and slides it covers, whether Pororo Park is separate, and whether meals or activity fees are included.

Ask about: operating hours and the age or height limits on slides. Do not turn up expecting walk-in access.


What a resort day really costs


The pass price is only the start. A cheaper entry with every extra charged separately can end up costing more than a pricier package with lunch and activities built in, so compare the total for your whole group, children included.

What to ask before you pay


Before you book

  • Confirm the date and hoursWeekday and weekend rates differ, and some resorts split day and night use. Check opening and closing times, and whether arriving late shortens your stay.
  • Ask what is actually includedDay use does not always mean every facility. Ask about the beach, pools, slides, play areas, gym and water activities by name.
  • Know your meal typeA buffet, a set meal and a dining credit are different things. Ask which restaurant, which hours, and whether credit covers drinks.
  • Give the children's agesAge bands change from resort to resort, and some packages cap free children per paying adult.
  • Check the outside-food ruleMost resorts do not allow it, though bottled water is often fine. Ask before packing a cooler.
  • Keep it in writingSave the reply, voucher and receipt. For groups, ask about deposits and the rebooking or cancellation policy.

Where each resort is


Mactan is small, but the resorts sit in different corners, and traffic can turn a short distance into a long ride. Plan around the area, not just the price. If you are also working out the airport ride, see our guide to getting from Mactan-Cebu Airport to Cebu City.


North end

Punta Engaño

Shangri-La, Crimson, Dusit Thani and BE Resort. The easiest group if you are staying near Mactan Newtown.

Resort strip

Maribago and Buyong

Bluewater and Costabella in Buyong–Maribago, and JPark in Maribago. A good fit if you want two or three options close together.

South

Marigondon

Plantation Bay sits farthest south. Leave early and allow extra time from the airport or Punta Engaño.


Coming across the bridges from Cebu City, expect slow stretches around Mandaue and Lapu-Lapu. Avoid fitting a tight airport transfer right after your resort visit.


What to bring


  • Swimwear that fits the dress rulesSome resorts have attire rules, so check first.
  • A change of clothesPlus a small waterproof bag for wet things.
  • Sunscreen and a hatBeach hours here are 9 AM to 5 or 6 PM, mostly in full sun.
  • Cash or a cardFor towel deposits and anything the pass does not cover.
  • Your booking confirmationAnd an ID if the resort asks for one.
  • A towel, only if neededSome passes include one, others rent it, so skip carrying a spare until you know.

So, which one?


Pick the resort that matches your group, not the one with the biggest name. Plantation Bay makes sense for anyone who gets restless by the pool, and Bluewater is the safe, fairly priced choice for families who want lunch handled. Shangri-La suits couples and groups who will use the sports and pay for food separately, while Crimson and Dusit Thani fit a long, slow pool day with dining built in.

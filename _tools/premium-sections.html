The Davao table

Five things to taste

  • DurianThe fruit Davao is most famous for. First-timers should start with a small piece, or try it as candy or ice cream before taking on the fresh fruit.
  • PomeloBig, sweet-sour citrus sold in markets and fruit stands, and easy to carry home.

Before you fly

  • Know the local rulesDavao enforces a public smoking ban, a firecracker ban and speed limits, and the police act on them.
  • Carry cashMalls and larger restaurants take cards and e-wallets, but small vendors and drivers often want cash.

Before you go

Six things that make a December visit to Davao easier.

  • Tip 01Book rooms early for the second half of DecemberOrganisers expected around 100,000 visitors for last year's Pasko Fiesta, and families from nearby provinces fill hotels in the days before Christmas.
  • Tip 02Check road closures on event daysThe city posts closures for the parade and the dawn Mass food strip. Downtown traffic slows on these days, so walk or ride a taxi instead of driving into the centre.

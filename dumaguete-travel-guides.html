Dumaguete travel guides

Dumaguete is the capital of Negros Oriental, a coastal city on the southeastern side of Negros Island. The guides below cover getting there from Cebu, what a three-day trip costs, where to eat breakfast and what to try on a first food trip, how the city is changing, local events such as the Buglasan Festival, and day trips to Bais, Manjuyod, Mabinay and Siquijor.

Not sure whether to start here or in Cebu? Dumaguete vs Cebu compares the two. Costs, ferry schedules and event dates change, so check current details before you go. Looking beyond the city? See the Negros Oriental guides.

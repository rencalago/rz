Dumaguete travel guides

Dumaguete is the capital of Negros Oriental, a small, walkable city on the southeastern coast of Negros Island. It is a university town first: Silliman University, founded in 1901, sits right in the middle of it, and the students shape the cafes, the cheap eats and the slow pace of the place. The seafront Rizal Boulevard is where the city goes in the early morning and again at sunset.

Most people come for a mix of city time and day trips, and Dumaguete works well as a base for both. Within an hour or two you can reach Apo Island for snorkelling, the Twin Lakes in the hills of Sibulan, dolphin watching and the Manjuyod sandbar off Bais, or the ferry to Siquijor. Getting here is straightforward: the airport is in neighbouring Sibulan, and ferries and fast craft link the port with Cebu, Siquijor and Bohol. The sea or road guide compares the routes.

It is also one of the more affordable cities in the Visayas. A three-day trip on a budget is realistic, and food is a big part of the appeal, from breakfast spots to a first-timer's food trip. October is the busiest month for local events, with the Buglasan Festival bringing the towns of the province into the city.

The stories below cover the city itself, how it is changing, and its festivals and events. Ferry times, fares and event schedules change from season to season, so confirm them before you travel. Still choosing between this city and Cebu? Read Dumaguete vs Cebu first, and see the Negros Oriental guides for the rest of the province.

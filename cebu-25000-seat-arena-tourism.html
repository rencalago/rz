SM Seaside Cebu Arena: Upcoming Events, Capacity and How to Get There

Ren Calago 12 October 2026 Updated 16 October 2026

SM Seaside Cebu Arena is Cebu's newest big venue: an indoor arena in South Road Properties (SRP), beside SM Seaside City, that seats about 16,000 and holds up to 25,000 at full capacity. It opened in July 2026 and is the largest indoor arena in the Visayas. For visitors, the building matters less than what it brings to the city: concerts, ice shows and international sport that used to mean a trip to Manila.


This guide covers the events coming up, the sports tournaments set for 2027, how to get there from the airport and the city, and what to plan for on an event night.


Upcoming Events at the Arena


LANY brings its soft world tour to the arena on Friday, 6 November and Thursday, 12 November 2026. Disney On Ice follows with Let's Dance! from 28 November to 6 December 2026, with Cebu as the first stop before the show moves to Manila for the holidays. Check the official listing for each show for ticket prices and the seat map before you book flights or a room.


Since BINI opened the arena with a concert on 11 July, the calendar has mixed Filipino acts and international tours, including IV of Spades, KZ Tandingan and TJ Monterde, Hillsong Worship and Yeng Constantino. New dates are added through the year, so look at the arena's current listings on Live Nation Philippines and the organisers' pages before you plan a trip around a show.


Sport in 2027: Basketball and Volleyball


International sport arrives in 2027. The FIBA Women's Asia Cup is scheduled at SM Seaside Cebu Arena from 11 to 18 July 2027. Cebu is also lined up to host Women's Volleyball Nations League matches in 2027, after this year's Philippine leg moved to PhilSports Arena in Pasig because the Cebu venue was not finished in time. PBA and CESAFI games have been discussed for the arena as well, so keep an eye on those schedules if you follow local basketball.


What the Arena Is Like


SM Prime Holdings built the arena at a cost of about ₱7 billion. The architects, Arquitectonica of Miami, gave it a wave-shaped glass front that echoes SM Mall of Asia Arena. Inside, there is a centre-hung display, 30 premier suites above the VIP sections and a dedicated practice court.


How many people fit depends on the layout of each event, so the seat map matters more than the headline number. Before you pay, open the seat map for your show on the official ticket listing and check where your section sits in relation to the stage.



Where to Stay


SM's plan is a district in SRP that pairs the arena with the SMX Convention Center Seaside Cebu and two hotels, Park Inn by Radisson and Radisson, which are targeted to open in the first half of 2028. Until then, stay in Cebu City or along the South Road, and book early around big shows.


The idea behind the district is that concerts, sport and conventions give people a reason to stay longer instead of flying in and out. That works for visitors too: use an event as the anchor for a longer trip. Our three-day Cebu itinerary shows how to fill the days around a show, and the south Cebu waterfall stops make a good day out once the concert is over.


Getting There from the Airport and the City


The arena is about 15 kilometres from Mactan-Cebu International Airport. Off-peak, the drive takes 25 to 35 minutes. Between 7 and 10 in the morning and 4 and 7 in the evening it can take an hour or more, and event nights add their own traffic on the South Road.


Grab is the simplest option from the airport, at roughly ₱400 to ₱600, more when surge pricing kicks in. White metered taxis usually cost less, while the yellow airport taxis cost more. MyBus is the budget choice at about ₱50 a ride, but reaching SRP takes a transfer and the last buses run around 9 to 10 in the evening, so plan a car for the trip back after a show.


After a concert, give yourself plenty of time. The ride back is the part most likely to run long, and cars are harder to find once the crowd spills onto the road. Agreeing on a pickup point a short walk from the main entrance makes it easier for your driver to find you.


Event-Night Tips


Ticket prices depend on the show, so check each event's official listing, and the same goes for parking. Arrive early, since the South Road gets heavy well before big events start. SM Seaside City next door has plenty of places to eat before a show.


The rainy months run into the end of the year, so bring a light rain jacket for the walk from the road to the entrance. The district around the arena is still being built, so expect some unfinished areas, and more dining choices nearby once the hotels open.


Who This Suits


The arena is most useful for concert fans from across the Visayas and Mindanao, sports fans who want to watch a big match without travelling to Manila, and visitors already in Cebu for a conference. If you are looking for quiet beaches or a slow itinerary, it will not change much for you. You may still find it useful as a single evening out before you head to the coast.

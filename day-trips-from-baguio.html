Day Trips From Baguio: Where to Go and How Long It Takes

Ren Calago 8 November 20260

The honest answer to "where can I go for a day from Baguio?" depends on the clock, not the map. La Trinidad is a short ride away. Atok, on the Halsema Highway, takes about an hour and a half to two hours each way. The La Union coast is roughly two to three hours depending on how you travel, and Hundred Islands is a stretch at around three hours one way. Sagada, Mount Pulag and Banaue look close on a map of the Cordillera, but each one needs a night.


This guide sorts the options by door-to-door time instead of by how good the photos look, then covers what each trip is worth, how to reach it and where the day tends to go wrong. The times are typical figures that swing with traffic, fog and road works, so use them for planning rather than as promises.


Start early, carry a jacket and check road advisories the day before. In the mountains, weather and road conditions decide more day trips than distance does.

Every Trip Ranked by Travel Time


  • La Trinidad: a short jeepney or taxi ride, easy as a half day.
  • Atok, Benguet: about 48 km up Halsema Highway, 1.5 to 2 hours each way.
  • La Union coast: about 1 hour 50 minutes by car, up to three hours by bus.
  • Hundred Islands, Alaminos: about three hours each way.
  • Kabayan, the Mount Pulag gateway: two to three hours by van, and the climb needs a permit.
  • Sagada: roughly 5.5 to 6.5 hours each way.
  • Banaue: seven to eight hours by bus.

A workable rule: a day trip should leave you at least as many hours at the destination as on the road. Past three hours each way, that stops being true.


La Trinidad: The Easy Half Day


La Trinidad is the provincial capital just north of the city, and its strawberry farm, flower fields and Valley of Colors fit into a morning without a long bus ride at either end. Our Baguio and La Trinidad guide covers the stops and the jeepney route, so the rest of this article goes to trips that take real planning.


Atok on the Halsema Highway: The Best Full Day


Atok sits about 48 km north of Baguio, in the cool Benguet highlands. The Highest Point marker on Halsema Highway stands at 2,255 metres and was the loftiest spot on the Philippine highway system until 2019, when a road in Tinoc, Ifugao took the title. It is only a photo stop, so the reason to give Atok a day is everything around it:


  • Northern Blossom Flower Farm, open Monday to Saturday and closed on Sundays.
  • Sakura Park, which is free, though its cherry trees bloom for only a week or two.
  • Haight's Place, a flower garden with a café.
  • The Osocan Spanish Trail, a 2 to 3 km former salt trail with three man-made tunnels.
  • Lourdes Grotto and the Wagangan rock formations, reached by a short trek.

Entrance fees at the farms and gardens run from about ₱50 to a few hundred pesos, so bring cash. The tourism office inside the municipal hall handles registration and can arrange guides.


By public transport, take a van or GL Trans bus bound for Sagada or Bontoc from the Dangwa terminal area and ask to get off at Sayangan, near the municipal hall and market. The fare is around ₱150 to ₱180. If you drive, leave between 5:30 and 6:30 a.m. so you climb before the fog settles, and use low beams or fog lights on a road with sharp curves and few signs.


November to May is the better window, and temperatures can sit anywhere from about 7°C to 18°C. June to October brings fog and slippery roads. Ask about return rides before you wander far from the highway, and do not plan on a late-evening van.


La Union: Sea Level in Two Hours, or Three


The coast is about 80 km from Baguio. Naguilian Road, the 39 km route officially known as the Baguio–Bauang road, drops to Bauang, and the whole drive takes about 1 hour 50 minutes. Buses are slower. Partas and other operators with Abra, Vigan or Laoag signboards pick up along Gov. Pack Road, pass Urbiztondo in San Juan and take up to about three hours, with fares of roughly ₱100 to ₱150.


Choose one stop. San Juan is the surf option: a one-hour lesson with a board is listed at about ₱400 along Urbiztondo, and our La Union surf season guide explains when the waves suit beginners. Tangadan Falls in San Gabriel is the waterfall option: the entrance is ₱30, a guide is mandatory at ₱700 per group of up to seven, and the walk from the trailhead takes 15 to 20 minutes. A jeepney from San Fernando to San Gabriel takes about 45 minutes, so allow half a day. Fees change, so confirm at the municipal hall.


Doing both pushes the ride home into the dark, which is when mountain roads are least pleasant.


Hundred Islands: Possible, but Tight


Alaminos is about three hours from Baguio by van or bus. Six hours on the road leaves only a few hours on the water, and a typical island-hopping day covers four or five of the islands. If Hundred Islands is the point of the trip, sleep in Alaminos and take the boat the next morning. Our Hundred Islands guide lists the islands, fees and boat options.


The Trips That Need a Night


Sagada is about 150 km along Halsema Highway, and buses such as GL Trans take 5.5 to 6.5 hours each way. A same-day return means 11 to 13 hours of road for a few hours of caving or coffin viewing. Mount Pulag starts with a van to Kabayan, two to three hours away, where the DENR office registers climbers, and missing the last van can leave you stranded. Banaue is seven to eight hours by bus, with Ohayami departing near Burnham Park. All three work well with one night or more, not as a squeezed return trip.


Road Checks Before You Leave


Kennon Road, the old route down from the city, has had on-and-off closures from rockfalls and landslides. At the last advisory it was limited to light vehicles up to five tons, with buses and trucks sent along Marcos Highway, and rules like that can change quickly. Halsema Highway is landslide-prone in the rainy season, when paved sections turn slippery. Ask your hotel or the city tourism office about road status the day before, and set out before 6:30 a.m. wherever you are headed.


A Baguio day trip works when the road takes up less than a third of your waking hours. Atok clears that bar, La Union barely does, and everything else is better with a pillow at the end.

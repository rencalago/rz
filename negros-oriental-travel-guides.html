Negros Oriental travel guides

Negros Oriental covers the eastern half of Negros Island, with Dumaguete as its capital. This page follows the province beyond the city: weekend trips for different budgets, places within driving distance of Dumaguete, Mabinay's caves and nature, dolphin watching from Bais, mountain cafes in Pamplona, a beach resort in La Libertad and views in Tanjay.

For the city itself, including food, budgets and events, see the Dumaguete guides. Distances, ferry schedules and opening hours change, so confirm before you travel.

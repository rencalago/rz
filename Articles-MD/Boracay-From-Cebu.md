# Boracay from Cebu

Boracay is a straightforward trip from Cebu because there are direct flights to both Caticlan and Kalibo, the two main gateways in Aklan. Caticlan Airport (MPH) is the closest — seven kilometers from the jetty — while Kalibo Airport (KLO) is farther and cheaper, requiring a 1.5 to 2-hour van ride to Caticlan.

Once you land, the process is the same: van or tricycle to Caticlan Jetty Port, pay terminal and environmental fees, take a 10 to 15-minute boat to Cagban Port on Boracay, then an e-trike to your hotel. Total transfer from Caticlan Airport to a White Beach hotel is usually 45 to 60 minutes if you move quickly.

Here is how the two flight routes compare, what the Caticlan transfer actually costs right now, and when to go for the best beach conditions.

## 1. Cebu to Caticlan — The Fastest Route

This is the route most first-time visitors choose.

**Flights:** Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, and AirAsia Philippines operate Cebu (CEB) to Caticlan (MPH) daily. Flight time is about 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes. One-way fares range from PHP 1,200 on promo to PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,500 on regular dates. Bags and seat selection are extra on low-cost carriers.

**Caticlan Airport to Caticlan Jetty:** Exit the airport and take a tricycle or van to Caticlan Jetty Port. Travel time 7 to 10 minutes. Tricycle PHP 50 to PHP 150 per vehicle, shared van often included if you pre-book a door-to-door transfer.

**Caticlan Jetty to Boracay:** At the jetty terminal, you register and pay fees, then board a pump boat to Cagban Port. Boat ticket PHP 50 to PHP 100, terminal fee PHP 100 to PHP 150 at Caticlan Jetty (PHP 100 at Tabon Port), environmental fee PHP 150 for local tourists and PHP 300 for foreign tourists. Boats run roughly 5 AM to 9 PM. Crossing takes 10 to 15 minutes.

**Cagban Port to hotel:** E-trike from Cagban to White Beach Station 1, 2, or 3 costs PHP 150 to PHP 300 per vehicle depending on distance and luggage. Station 2 is the middle and most common drop-off.

**Total DIY cost one-way from Caticlan Airport to hotel:** Around PHP 350 to PHP 700 per person if you share a tricycle and boat, excluding flight. Pre-booked joiner door-to-door transfers that bundle van, fees, boat, and e-trike cost PHP 950 to PHP 1,050 per person.

**Best for:** Travelers who want the shortest travel time and are willing to pay a bit more for convenience.

## 2. Cebu to Kalibo — The Cheaper Flight, Longer Land Trip

Kalibo is the capital of Aklan and handles larger aircraft, so fares are often lower.

**Flights:** Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines fly Cebu to Kalibo (KLO). Flight time similar, about 1 hour. Promo one-way fares can be PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,000, often cheaper than Caticlan on the same dates.

**Kalibo Airport to Caticlan Jetty:** Take an air-conditioned van or bus from the airport to Caticlan. Travel time 1.5 to 2 hours, sometimes longer with traffic. Cost PHP 200 to PHP 500 per person for a shared van, PHP 2,000 to PHP 3,500 for a private van. Some buses go direct to Caticlan Jetty Port, others to the bus terminal with a short tricycle connection.

**Then same boat process as Caticlan route.**

**Total time from Kalibo landing to Boracay hotel:** About 2.5 to 3.5 hours including van and boat. Total from Cebu City to Boracay hotel via Kalibo: 5 to 6 hours including airport time.

**Best for:** Budget travelers and groups who can split a private van, or when Caticlan flights are sold out during peak season.

**What to do:** If you choose Kalibo, book a van that goes straight to Caticlan Jetty Port, not just Kalibo town. Confirm if terminal and environmental fees are included — most shared vans exclude them.

## 3. Costs to Budget Right Now

Bring cash in small bills. The jetty ports prefer cash, and ATMs on Boracay charge fees.

- **Cebu to Caticlan flight:** PHP 1,200 to PHP 4,500 one-way per person.
- **Cebu to Kalibo flight:** PHP 1,000 to PHP 3,500 one-way per person.
- **Tricycle/van Caticlan Airport to Jetty:** PHP 50 to PHP 150.
- **Terminal fee:** PHP 100 to PHP 150 at Caticlan Jetty.
- **Environmental fee:** PHP 150 local, PHP 300 foreign (one-time per entry, valid for length of stay).
- **Boat Caticlan to Cagban:** PHP 50 to PHP 100.
- **E-trike Cagban to hotel:** PHP 150 to PHP 300 per vehicle.
- **Roundtrip fees:** Some desks quote roundtrip terminal and environmental as PHP 520 local / PHP 670 foreign, but most pay per leg. Check before paying for a roundtrip bundle you may not use if you exit via a different port.

For a joiner package from Caticlan Airport to hotel inclusive of all fees and boat, budget PHP 950 to PHP 1,050 per person one-way.

Hotels on White Beach range from PHP 1,500 for basic fan rooms inland to PHP 5,000 to PHP 12,000 for beachfront mid-range. Station 1 is quieter and more upscale, Station 2 is central and busiest, Station 3 is more budget-friendly.

## 4. Best Months to Visit Boracay

Boracay has two main seasons shaped by monsoons.

**Dry season — November to May:** This is the best overall window. Northeast monsoon (Amihan) brings sunny days, lower humidity, and calm seas on White Beach. Temperatures average 25 to 32°C. December to February is coolest and driest, with calm water ideal for swimming. March to May is hotter and sunniest — April is often cited as the sunniest month, but also the most crowded around Holy Week.

**Wet season — June to October:** Southwest monsoon (Habagat) brings more rain, higher humidity, and rougher seas. White Beach can get seaweed and onshore winds shift to Bulabog Beach, which is why kitesurfing moves to Bulabog from June onward. July to September is the peak typhoon window, with higher chance of trip disruptions. Prices drop and crowds thin, but boat cancellations are more likely.

**For Cebu travelers:** November to January offers dry weather with fewer crowds than December holidays. February to April gives the most reliable beach days if you do not mind higher prices. If you want to avoid the biggest crowds, avoid Christmas to New Year and Holy Week.

## 5. White Beach and What to Actually Do

Boracay is small — you can walk White Beach end to end in about 45 minutes at low tide.

- **Swimming:** White Beach faces west, so afternoons have sunset but also stronger sun. Swim in the morning for calmer water. Follow the swimming buoys — boats are not allowed inside.
- **Bulabog Beach:** On the east side, 5 to 10 minutes by e-trike from White Beach. Best for kitesurfing and wind sports from June to September.
- **Puka Shell Beach:** North end, quieter, coarser sand with puka shells. Good for a beach day away from Station 2.
- **Activities:** Island hopping to Crocodile Island, Magic Island cliff diving, and snorkeling at Coral Garden. Standard joiner island hopping costs PHP 800 to PHP 1,500 per person including lunch.

## Tips Before You Go

### Pre-book your Caticlan transfer if you arrive late

Boats run until about 9 PM, but transfers and e-trikes thin out after 7 PM. A pre-booked door-to-door transfer guarantees a van and boat, and includes fee handling.

### Keep your QR code and ID ready

Caticlan Jetty requires registration. Have your hotel name, return date, and ID ready. Foreign passports need to show entry stamp for local vs foreign fee classification.

### Choose your station wisely

Station 1 is quieter, with higher-end resorts and less boat traffic. Station 2 is central for restaurants and nightlife. Station 3 has more budget rooms and local eateries. All three share the same white sand.

### Do not overpack

E-trikes have limited space. A backpack or medium suitcase is easier than a large hard case, especially for the boat transfer where luggage gets stacked.

### Respect Boracay's rules

Boracay enforces no smoking and no drinking on White Beach itself, no single-use plastic in some areas, and no bonfires. Environmental fees fund beach rehabilitation — follow lifeguard flags and swim only in designated zones.

## So, Caticlan or Kalibo from Cebu?

If time matters, fly Cebu to Caticlan and do the 45 to 60-minute jetty transfer. If price matters or Caticlan is full, fly Cebu to Kalibo and add the 1.5 to 2-hour van — the savings can be significant for groups.

Either way, aim for November to April for the best beach weather, budget PHP 350 to PHP 700 for the Caticlan to hotel transfer on top of your flight, and keep cash ready for terminal and environmental fees at the jetty.

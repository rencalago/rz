# Gigantes Islands, Iloilo Guide

Gigantes Islands sits off the northeastern tip of Panay in the municipality of Carles, Iloilo. Locals call it Islas de Gigantes, and it is made of two main islands — Gigantes Norte and Gigantes Sur — plus small islets like Cabugao Gamay, Pulupandan, and the shifting Bantigue Sandbar. Norte is where most people live and where homestays and resorts are. Sur is where most of the postcard spots are clustered.

The whole group is famous for three things: limestone cliffs that look like a mini El Nido, white-sand pockets that stay relatively uncrowded, and scallops. Wasay-wasay oysters and fresh scallops are harvested here daily, so most tours include unli-scallops and unli-rice at lunch. It is still more rustic than Boracay or El Nido, which is exactly why people go.

From Cebu, Gigantes is not a day trip you can wing. You need to fly or ferry to Iloilo City first, then travel 3 to 4 hours overland to Bancal Port in Carles, then take a 1 to 2-hour boat to the islands. Done right, it is a 2 to 3-day side trip that pairs well with Iloilo City and Guimaras.

## 1. Gigantes Norte vs Sur — Where to Base

**Gigantes Norte** is the more developed island. Barangays Gabi, Granada, and Asluman have homestays, a few aircon guesthouses, sari-sari stores, and the 1895 Spanish-era Gigantes Lighthouse in Barangay Gabi. If you stay overnight, you will likely stay here. Accommodation is simple: PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500 for an aircon room for two, fan rooms from PHP 800. Expect basic Wi-Fi and limited power at night in some homestays.

**Gigantes Sur** is where the classic tour stops are — Tangke Lagoon, Antonia Beach, Cabugao Gamay, Bantigue Sandbar, and Pulupandan. There are fewer places to stay here, but day tours from Bancal or from Norte cover it easily.

**Best for:** Stay in Gigantes Norte for overnight island feel and early lighthouse walk. Stay in Iloilo City or Carles if you only want a joiner day tour without packing overnight bags.

**What to do:** Walk to Gigantes Norte Lighthouse for sunset, ask your host for a habal-habal tour around the fishing villages, and sort your boat for the next day before 6 PM — boats are easier to secure early.

## 2. The Four Must-Visit Spots in Gigantes Sur

### Cabugao Gamay Island — The Selfie Island

This tiny island southeast of Sur is the most photographed spot in Gigantes. Two small white beaches connected by a sandbar, plus a short scramble up limestone rocks to a viewpoint overlooking the whole group. You only need 30 to 45 minutes here. Entrance fee is around PHP 50 per head, usually included if you are on a packaged tour.

**Best for:** Classic viewpoint, drone shots if wind allows, quick swim on both sides of the sandbar.
**What to do:** Go up the left trail early morning for softer light. Bring aqua shoes — rocks are sharp.

### Tangke Lagoon — The Saltwater Pool

Tangke means tank in Hiligaynon. It is a brackish saltwater lagoon framed by high limestone walls on Gigantes Sur, hidden behind a narrow gap. Water level depends on tide — at low tide it is a shallow emerald pool you can swim in; at high tide or rough seas, guides will cancel the stop for safety. Local legend says water inside rises every June 24 for St. John the Baptist day. 

**Best for:** Shelter from waves, calm swim inside cliffs, interesting geology.
**What to do:** Listen to your guide on tide conditions. If Tangke is closed, tours usually replace it with Pulupandan or Tinagong Dagat.

### Antonia Beach — Lunch and Snorkel

Long white sand, clear water, and where most joiner tours serve lunch. It is also the hub for optional extras: banana boat PHP 200 per pax minimum 5, jet ski around PHP 2,000 per 30 minutes. Snorkeling by the rocky ends is decent but bring your own set if you are particular — rental gear is shared.

**Best for:** Unhurried swim, lunch stop, snorkeling for beginners.
**What to do:** After lunch, walk to the far end away from the boats for quieter water.

### Bantigue Sandbar and Pulupandan Islet

Bantigue is a long, shifting white sandbar off Sur — best at low tide when more sand is exposed. Pulupandan is the lone palm tree island you see on itineraries, great for a first photo stop from Bancal. Both are often included as 2 of the 5 to 7 stops in a full day tour.

## 3. Scallops and What to Eat

Gigantes is the scallops capital of Western Visayas. Fishers sell them for as low as PHP 1 per piece at Bancal Market, which is why joiner tours can offer unlimited steamed scallops, unlimited wasay-wasay oysters, plus grilled fish, adobong pusit, and rice. It is not fine dining — it is fresh, salty, grilled buffet food on a beach table.

If you stay overnight, order sinigang na tangigue, pinatamis na adobong scallops, and baked scallops from your homestay. Budget PHP 300 to PHP 600 per cooked dish for 2 people. Bring cash — no card terminals on the islands, and GCash signal is patchy.

## 4. How to Get to Gigantes Islands from Cebu

This is the practical route everyone uses.

### Step 1: Cebu to Iloilo City

**Fly:** Cebu Pacific and Philippines AirAsia fly Cebu (CEB) to Iloilo (ILO) daily. Flight time 1 hour. One-way fares PHP 1,200 promo to PHP 2,800 regular. First flights around 5:30 AM.

**Ferry:** OceanJet and 2GO/Cokaliong have Cebu to Iloilo via ferry. Cokaliong/Trans-Asia overnight routes leave Cebu around 7 PM and arrive Iloilo 8 AM next day. Economy fare PHP 900 to PHP 1,400, tourist PHP 1,600 to PHP 2,200. Good if you have big luggage and want to save a flight.

### Step 2: Iloilo City to Bancal Port, Carles

Go to **Tagbak Terminal** in Jaro, Iloilo City — the northern Iloilo van and Ceres terminal. 

- **Ceres bus to Carles/Estancia/Balasan:** Leaves every hour from around 4 AM to 5 PM. Iloilo to Carles by bus takes 3.5 to 4.5 hours, fare PHP 250 to PHP 350.
- **Public van to Carles:** Faster, 2.5 to 3.5 hours, fare PHP 300 to PHP 400. Vans leave when full, last van around 4 PM. If you are in a group, a private van rental from Iloilo City to Bancal is PHP 4,000 to PHP 6,500 one-way, driver can pick you up at the airport.

Tell the driver Bancal Port, not just Carles town — port is about 15 to 20 minutes more by tricycle. Tricycle from Carles terminal to Bancal Port PHP 50 to PHP 100 per person.

**Timing tip:** To catch the regular passenger boat to Gigantes at around 10:30 AM, you need to leave Iloilo City by 5 AM to 6 AM latest. Most joiner day tours leaving from Iloilo City pick up at 4 AM to 4:45 AM.

### Step 3: Bancal Port to Gigantes Islands

- **Public passenger boat Bancal to Gigantes Norte (Langub or Lantangan):** One trip per day around 10:30 AM to 11 AM from Bancal, returns early morning around 8 AM from Gigantes. Fare PHP 100 to PHP 150 per person, travel 1 to 1.5 hours. If you miss it, you charter.
- **Charter pump boat for island hopping:** Private boat that is also your island hopping boat for the day. Price PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,000 for a small boat good for 5 to 10 people depending on season and boat registration. This is the usual option if you booked a joiner tour — the tour operator provides the boat.
- **From Roxas City alternative:** If you fly Cebu to Roxas (RXS), van to Carles is only 2 to 2.5 hours, then same boat. Useful when Roxas flights are cheaper.

Total Cebu to Gigantes door-to-port time if you fly morning: Cebu airport to Iloilo arrival 8 AM, van to Bancal by 11:30 AM to 12:30 PM, boat to islands by 1:30 PM. Many travelers therefore overnight in Carles first, then start island hopping next morning.

## 5. Island Hopping Tours and Real Costs

There are two ways to do it.

**Joiner day tour from Bancal Port:** The budget standard. Operators at Bancal Building 1 advertise all-in joiner packages.

- Tour A Beach Lovers style: PHP 1,199 to PHP 1,399 per head for 5 to 7 islands: Pulupandan, Cabugao Gamay, Antonia Beach, Bantigue Sandbar, Tangke Lagoon. Includes boat, environmental fee, entrance fees, tour guide, life vest, and seafood lunch with unli-scallops.
- Private day tour from Iloilo City or Roxas City with transfers: PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,500 per person on Klook/private operators, US$ 45 to US$ 195 per person depending on inclusions and pickup. Often more expensive because it bundles Iloilo-Carles roundtrip van.

**Private charter if you have a group:** Boat alone PHP 3,000 to PHP 5,000 for the day, plus guide PHP 500, environmental fee PHP 75 per person, entrance fees PHP 50 per island stop (Cabugao Gamay, Tangke). Lunch if not included PHP 400 to PHP 600 per head for seafood.

Budget sample for 1 person joiner from Iloilo City:
- Cebu-Iloilo flight one-way PHP 1,500
- Van Iloilo to Bancal roundtrip PHP 600 to PHP 800
- Joiner all-in tour PHP 1,299
- Tricycle and terminal fees PHP 200
Total transport + tour around PHP 3,600 one-way before accommodation.

Accommodation in Gigantes Norte: PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500 aircon room, sometimes with free breakfast. Tent or nipa hut PHP 800 to PHP 1,200.

## 6. Best Months to Visit

Dry season November to May is best. Amihan northeast monsoon brings calmer seas and better visibility for boats.

- **November to February:** Cooler, 26 to 30°C, calm mornings, best for photos. Less chance of tour cancellation.
- **March to May:** Hottest, sunniest, best for sandbars. Bantigue and Cabugao look whitest. Midday heat is intense — boat rides need hats and rash guards.
- **June to October:** Habagat southwest monsoon, higher chance of big waves and Tangke closure. Passenger boats are more likely to be cancelled. Some tours stop operating in August to September. Avoid if you have a tight schedule.

If you are coming from Cebu for a weekend, target December to April. Book tours a day ahead during Holy Week and December holidays — Bancal boats fill up.

## Tips Before You Go

### Pack like you are going off-grid

Bring dry bag, reef-safe sunscreen, aqua shoes, cap, power bank, and cash in small bills. There are no ATMs on Gigantes Norte and Sur — last ATM is in Carles or Estancia. Charge everything in Iloilo City.

### Respect the tide and guide calls

Guides will skip Tangke Lagoon or Cabugao if waves are unsafe. That is normal. Do not pressure boatmen — they know the channel. Life jackets are required by Marina-registered boats; wear it between islands.

### Do not buy everything in Bancal

Water and snacks are 2x price on the islands. Bring 2 liters water per person for a day tour. Buy scallops at PHP 1 per piece at Bancal market only if you have a place to cook — otherwise just enjoy the unli lunch.

### Plan your return boat early

Public boat from Gigantes to Bancal leaves early morning. If you have a flight from Iloilo the same day, you cannot make it if you overnight on Gigantes and take the public boat — you need a private charter that leaves Gigantes by 7 AM to reach Iloilo by noon. Most people stay an extra night in Iloilo City before flying back to Cebu.

## So, Is Gigantes Worth the Van and Boat Hours?

Yes, if you like raw islands without big resorts. Gigantes Norte gives you fishing village life and a lighthouse walk, while Gigantes Sur gives you the hits — Cabugao Gamay for that drone-like viewpoint you can climb to, Tangke Lagoon for a swim inside limestone walls, Antonia Beach for lunch and snorkel, and Bantigue for a barefoot sandbar walk.

From Cebu, treat it as a 3-day trip: Day 1 Cebu to Iloilo City then van to Carles and overnight, Day 2 full island hopping with unli-scallops and overnight on Gigantes Norte, Day 3 boat back to Bancal and van to Iloilo for flight back to Cebu. November to May for calm seas, cash for fees, and a 4 AM pickup mindset — that is the real Gigantes formula.

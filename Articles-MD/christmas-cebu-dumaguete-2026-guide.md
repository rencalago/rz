# Christmas in Cebu and Dumaguete 2026: Lights, Simbang Gabi and Markets

Christmas starts early in the Philippines, and Cebu and Dumaguete show it better than most. By late October, malls light their trees. By December 1, Fuente Osmeña Circle in Cebu City is glowing, Quezon Park and Rizal Boulevard in Dumaguete are lined with lights, and the nine-day novena of Masses fills churches before dawn and again at night. If you are planning a December trip in 2026, you can see both cities in one loop and get two different feels for the season.

This guide gathers where the lights go up, which churches hold Simbang Gabi and Misa de Gallo, and which markets are worth your evenings.

## Christmas Lights: Where Both Cities Glow

### Cebu City: Fuente, Plaza Sugbo, and the malls

The city’s official season often opens at Fuente Osmeña Circle. In recent years, Cebu City has lit a Tree of Hope there in early December with a program of choir performances and a short fireworks show. The Cebu Provincial Capitol facade across the street lights up at the same time, so you get two photo spots within one walk. Plaza Sugbo, beside the City Hall, usually has its own tree. In 2024 and 2025 it leaned into a nature and resilience theme after a tough typhoon and earthquake year, with capay and native materials.

Mall trees start much earlier. SM Seaside City Cebu typically lights up in mid-October at the Mountain Wing Atrium. SM J Mall in Mandaue held its first tree lighting in late October 2025 with Japanese-inspired lanterns, and Ayala Center Cebu runs its Symphony of Lights at The Terraces from 6 pm to 9 pm through December. NUSTAR Resort at the South Road Properties usually opens its Atrium tree in late October as well, themed around pearls and coastal light installations at the Boardwalk.

Practical tip: Go to Fuente after 7 pm for the full effect, but bring a ride-hailing app. Traffic around Jones Avenue and Osmeña Blvd backs up badly during December. If you want quieter photos, come on a weekday and aim for the early blue hour before the crowds.

### Dumaguete: A smaller, walkable Paskohan

Dumaguete does not compete on scale, it wins on walkability. The City Hall, Quezon Park in front of St. Catherine of Alexandria Cathedral, and the lampposts along Rizal Boulevard are decorated every year as part of Pasko sa Dumaguete. The Boulevard lights reflect on the water, which makes a simple evening walk feel festive without a program.

Filinvest Malls Dumaguete adds a mall-centric Christmas. Its tree lighting in 2024 was on November 14 at Central Plaza, followed by a calendar of Paskong Pinoy activities like weekend bazaars, Santa meet-and-greets, and chill acoustic nights on Fridays and Saturdays of December. Expect the same timing in 2026, around mid-November.

For Dumaguete, plan your lights walk between 6:30 pm and 8:30 pm, then cut to the boulevard for grilled seafood. Local vendors often set up small food stalls near the park, and parking is tight, so walking from your hotel in the downtown area is easier than driving.

## Simbang Gabi and Misa de Gallo: The Nine-Day Novena

Understanding the schedule helps you join respectfully. The Archdiocese of Cebu distinguishes two forms. Simbang Gabi is the evening anticipated Mass from December 15 to 23. Misa de Gallo is the dawn Mass from December 16 to 24. Both are votive Masses for nine days leading to Christmas, still part of Advent, not yet Christmas liturgy.

### Cebu churches to consider

**Basilica Minore del Santo Niño, Osmeña Blvd, Cebu City.** The oldest church in the country, always packed. Misa de Gallo is usually at 4:30 am from December 16 to 24. Simbang Gabi is at night from December 15 to 23. Arrive 30 to 45 minutes early if you want a seat inside; the Pilgrim Center courtyard fills fast.

**Cebu Metropolitan Cathedral.** Faces the plaza near the Basilica. In the last cycle, Misa de Gallo was at 4:30 am, and Simbang Gabi anticipated Mass was at 6:00 pm Monday to Friday, and 5:30 pm on weekends. The cathedral courtyard closes to vehicles during Mass, which keeps it quiet.

**Archdiocesan Shrine of San Nicolas de Tolentino, C. Padilla St.** A neighborhood alternative if downtown is too crowded. Also holds 4:30 am Misa de Gallo.

**St. Therese of the Child Jesus, Lahug.** Serves the uptown area. Past Misa de Gallo times were 4:30 am. Good if you are staying near IT Park.

Expect puto, bibingka, and sikwate vendors outside after Mass. Bring small bills. Dress code is enforced in most churches: no sleeveless tops or short shorts.

### Dumaguete churches

**St. Catherine of Alexandria Cathedral, Quezon Park.** The main cathedral for the Diocese of Dumaguete. Dawn Masses for Misa de Gallo usually start between 4:00 am and 5:00 am from December 16 to 24, with Simbang Gabi in the evening from December 15 to 23 at around 6:00 pm to 7:30 pm. Bishop Julito Cortes has in past years centered the novena on a specific pastoral theme, like the Jubilee Year of Mercy, with homilies in Cebuano and English.

**Redemptorist Church, Real Street.** Slightly less crowded than the cathedral and popular with students from Silliman University.

In Dumaguete, Mass times are posted on parish Facebook pages by early December. Confirm at the gate, because some parishes add a second dawn Mass if the first overflows.

## Night Markets and Christmas Shopping

### Cebu: Carbon and Colon

Two markets define a Cebuano Christmas.

**Carbon Public Market, downtown.** For years known as Carbon Pasko Na, it turns 20 years in 2025. Vendors sell noche buena goods: ham, quezo de bola, fruit, and affordable decorations. In recent years over 5,000 vendors work the complex including ambulant sellers. It opens daily, but December gets busiest after 4 pm. The city holds a parade from Magallanes to Carbon around December 10 to open the program.

**Colon Night Market, Colon Street.** This is seasonal and covers about 1.17 kilometers of the oldest street in the country. It runs from mid-November through December, then again during Sinulog in January. Hours are roughly 5:30 pm to 1:00 am, with jeepneys rerouted. You find grilled skewers, ukay-ukay clothes, toys, phone accessories, and last-minute gifts starting at around 100 pesos. It is more about street food and bargain hunting than curated crafts.

Safety and comfort: Keep bags in front, use cash for small purchases, and avoid bringing large backpacks. If you want a calmer food market version, Carbon’s weekend night market from Friday to Sunday, 5 pm to 11 pm, has regulated stalls with local street food.

### Dumaguete: Park bazaars and boulevard stalls

Dumaguete’s Christmas shopping is less sprawling. During Pasko sa Dumaguete, the City Hall grounds and Quezon Park often host a Christmas bazaar with LGU-vetted stalls for local products, puto Calasiao-style rice cakes, and light installations. Filinvest Malls Dumaguete runs a Sunday Christmas bazaar through December, plus night markets tied to its Holidaze Chill events.

For something more local, walk Rizal Boulevard after 6 pm. Pop-up stalls sell handcrafted parols made from capiz or bamboo, and students sell handmade cards. It is low-key, which many travelers prefer.

## Planning Your Route for December 2026

If you want both cities, start in Cebu for the official lightings. Most tree lightings now happen in October to early December, so by mid-December everything is lit. Do Simbang Gabi or Misa de Gallo once in Cebu City, then take a fast ferry or a short flight to Dumaguete. Ceres or Vallacar buses to the south plus a ferry from Santander to Amlan or a direct flight via Cebu makes the transfer doable in a day.

**One practical flow:**

* December 15-16: Evening Simbang Gabi in Cebu at the Basilica or Cathedral, then dawn Misa de Gallo the next morning.
* December 16-18: Night markets at Colon and Carbon, with a side trip to Fuente Osmeña for lights.
* December 19-20: Ferry to Dumaguete, evening lights at Quezon Park and Rizal Boulevard.
* December 21-23: Markets at Filinvest Dumaguete and evening Mass at St. Catherine.
* December 24: Misa de Gallo at dawn, then Christmas Eve dinner.

**What to pack and book:** White or light church clothes that cover shoulders and knees, a small flashlight for dark sidewalks after Mass, and coins for vendors. Hotels near downtown Cebu and Dumaguete fill up from December 20 to January 2. Book early. Confirm Mass times on parish pages by December 10, 2026, since times shift slightly each year.

Christmas in Cebu feels big, bright, and celebratory. In Dumaguete it feels neighborly and easy to walk. Together they give a full picture of how the season is lived in the Visayas: lights for gathering, Masses for reflection, and markets for sharing.

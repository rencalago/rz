# Laoag and Pagudpud: Ilocos Norte Road Trip

Laoag City is Ilocos Norte's capital and gateway — sinking bell tower, old cathedral, sand dunes, and easy access to Paoay Church. Pagudpud, 80 km north, is the beach end — Saud Beach, Blue Lagoon, Kapurpurawan Rock, Bangui windmills, and Patapat Viaduct hugging the cliff.

The two make a natural road trip: base in Laoag for heritage and food, drive north to Pagudpud for beaches and coastal scenery, then loop back. The coastal road is well-paved, with views of West Philippine Sea on one side and Cordillera foothills on the other.

Here is how to plan a 3-day DIY road trip from Cebu, what to see between Laoag and Pagudpud, and what it costs.

## 1. Laoag City — Heritage, Bell Tower, and Dunes

**Sinking Bell Tower:** Laoag's iconic belfry, sinking a few centimeters each year into sandy ground. Beside St. William Cathedral. No entrance fee, quick photo stop.

**Paoay Church (St. Augustine Church):** UNESCO World Heritage Site in Paoay, 30 minutes from Laoag. Known for massive buttresses built for earthquake protection. Free entry, donation box.

**La Paz Sand Dunes:** Coastal dunes 15 minutes from Laoag City — 4x4 rides, sandboarding, and sunset views. 4x4 tour PHP 500–800 per vehicle, sandboarding included.

**Museo Ilocos Norte and Provincial Capitol:** Small museums for Ilocano history and textiles.

**Best for:** First half-day in Ilocos Norte, especially if you arrive Laoag midday.

**What to do:** Visit Sinking Bell Tower and St. William Cathedral on foot, drive to Paoay Church, then do La Paz Dunes for sunset 4x4.

## 2. Bangui Windmills, Kapurpurawan Rock, and Cape Bojeador — The Coast to Pagudpud

This stretch between Laoag and Pagudpud is the scenic core of the road trip.

**Bangui Wind Farm:** 20 wind turbines along Bangui Bay, 60 km from Laoag. First wind farm in Southeast Asia. No entrance fee, you drive right beside them. Best light morning or late afternoon.

**Kapurpurawan Rock Formation:** White limestone rock sculpted by wind and sea in Burgos, Ilocos Norte. Short walk from parking, horse ride PHP 100–200 optional. Entrance fee PHP 20–50 per person, guide PHP 100–200.

**Cape Bojeador Lighthouse:** Highest-elevated still-active Spanish-era lighthouse in the Philippines, built 1892, in Burgos. Views of Luzon Strait. Free entry, steep climb.

**Patapat Viaduct:** 1.3 km coastal viaduct hugging cliffs in Pagudpud, with sea on one side and mountains on the other. No fee — you stop at designated parking areas for photos.

**Bantay Abot Cave:** Natural cave opening with window to sea, near Patapat. Short hike.

**Best for:** Day 2 of road trip, driving north from Laoag to Pagudpud.

**What to do:** Start early — Laoag to Bangui 1 to 1.5 hours, Bangui to Kapurpurawan 30 minutes, Kapurpurawan to Cape Bojeador 20 minutes, Cape Bojeador to Patapat 30 minutes, Patapat to Saud Beach 30 minutes. Total driving Laoag to Pagudpud 2 to 2.5 hours without stops, 5 to 6 hours with stops.

## 3. Pagudpud — Beaches and Slow Time

Pagudpud has two main beaches and a quiet town.

**Saud Beach:** The main beach — long white sand, clear water, resorts lined along shore. Calmer than Blue Lagoon for swimming. Best for sunset.

**Blue Lagoon (Maira-Ira Beach):** More secluded, with a small lagoon and clearer water for snorkeling. 15 minutes from Saud. Fewer resorts, more quiet.

**Where to stay:** Saud Beach has most accommodation — budget inns PHP 1,500–3,000, mid-range like Kapuluan Vista or Saud Beach Resort PHP 3,500–6,000. Book ahead December to February and Holy Week.

**Best for:** Beach day after heritage and windmills. You can swim, island hop to Dos Hermanos Islands (small islets), or just rest.

**What to do:** Stay at least one night in Pagudpud to catch both sunrise at Blue Lagoon and sunset at Saud. Dos Hermanos boat tour PHP 1,000–1,500 per boat.

## 4. Getting to Ilocos Norte from Cebu

Same as Vigan — fly to Laoag or Manila, then bus.

**Fastest — Cebu to Laoag, then rent:**
- **CEB to LAO:** Philippine Airlines and Cebu Pacific fly CEB to LAO, 1h40m–2h direct, 2–3 times a week, PHP 1,500–5,000 one-way. Laoag Airport to Laoag City 10 minutes tricycle PHP 50–100.
- **Rent:** Motorbike rental from 2Wheels or Floyd's in Laoag, PHP 500–800 per day for scooter, PHP 800–1,200 for larger bike. Car rental with driver PHP 3,000–5,000 per day including fuel, popular for groups. Self-drive car PHP 2,500–4,000 per day.

**Via Manila:**
- **CEB to MNL:** Hourly, 1h20m–1h35m, PHP 1,200–5,000. Bus Manila to Laoag: Partas, Florida, Dominion, 10–12 hours, PHP 800–1,200 deluxe, PHP 1,200–1,800 premium. Night buses leave Cubao/Pasay evening, arrive Laoag morning.

**Public transport Laoag to Pagudpud:** Van or bus Laoag to Pagudpud 2 to 2.5 hours, PHP 150–250 per person, frequent until 4 PM. Tricycle tours in Pagudpud: half-day covering Kapurpurawan, Bangui, Patapat PHP 1,200–1,800 per tricycle, full day PHP 2,000–3,000.

## 5. 3-Day DIY Itinerary and Costs

**Day 1 — Arrive Laoag, Paoay and Dunes:**
- Morning: Arrive Laoag, check into hotel near city (PHP 1,500–4,000).
- 9:00 AM: Sinking Bell Tower, St. William Cathedral, Museo Ilocos Norte.
- 11:00 AM: Drive to Paoay Church (30 mins), Marcos Museum in Batac (15 mins from Paoay).
- 3:30 PM: La Paz Sand Dunes 4x4 and sunset.
- Evening: Dinner in Laoag — try Ilocos empanada (different from Vigan — thicker, more orange, with mung beans) PHP 40–60, bagnet, pinakbet.

**Day 2 — Laoag to Pagudpud Coastal Drive:**
- 7:00 AM: Early drive Laoag to Bangui Windmills (1–1.5 hrs).
- 9:00 AM: Kapurpurawan Rock (30 mins drive + 30 mins walk).
- 10:30 AM: Cape Bojeador Lighthouse (20 mins drive).
- 11:30 AM: Patapat Viaduct and Bantay Abot Cave (30 mins drive).
- 1:00 PM: Arrive Saud Beach, check in, lunch.
- 3:30 PM: Blue Lagoon swimming, sunset at Saud Beach.

**Day 3 — Pagudpud Beach and Return:**
- Morning: Dos Hermanos boat or beach time.
- 11:00 AM: Drive back to Laoag (2–2.5 hrs) or take bus.
- Afternoon: Pasalubong shopping — Abel Iloco, corniks, longganisa — and flight/bus back.

**Costs per person (excluding Cebu flights, sharing van/bike):**
- Motorbike 2 days: PHP 1,000–1,600.
- Van with driver 3 days: PHP 9,000–15,000 split among 3–4 pax (PHP 2,250–5,000 per person).
- Accommodation: PHP 1,500–6,000 per night.
- Bangui/Kapurpurawan/Cape Bojeador: mostly free, guide/horse PHP 100–300.
- Food: PHP 200–400 per meal.
- Total 3D2N excluding flights: PHP 6,000–12,000 per person DIY with motorbike, PHP 10,000–18,000 with van and mid-range hotels.

## Tips Before You Go

### Start early for coastal spots

Bangui and Kapurpurawan are best before 10 AM for light and fewer tourists. Patapat Viaduct gets crowded midday.

### Bring windbreaker and hat with strap

Coastal wind is strong, especially at Bangui and Patapat. A hat with strap prevents it flying into sea.

### Fuel up in Laoag

Gas stations between Laoag and Pagudpud are sparse. Fill up in Laoag or Paoay before heading north.

### Cash in small bills

Tricycle tours, boat rides, and small eateries are cash-only. ATMs in Pagudpud are limited — withdraw in Laoag.

### Respect wind farm and lighthouse rules

Do not climb wind turbines, stay behind barriers at Cape Bojeador, and do not leave trash at Kapurpurawan.

## So, How Should You Do Laoag and Pagudpud from Cebu?

Fly Cebu to Laoag, rent a motorbike if you are confident or a van with driver for groups, do Laoag heritage and dunes Day 1, coastal drive to Pagudpud via Bangui, Kapurpurawan, Cape Bojeador, Patapat Day 2, beach Day 3, and return. The road is paved, scenic, and well-signposted.

For a weekend from Cebu, 3 days is tight but doable if you fly. For a relaxed trip, add a night in Pagudpud and combine with Vigan on the way back — Vigan is 1.5 to 2 hours south of Laoag.

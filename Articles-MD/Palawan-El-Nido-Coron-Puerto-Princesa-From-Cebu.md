# Palawan: El Nido vs Coron vs Puerto Princesa, and Getting There from Cebu

Palawan is often talked about as one destination, but El Nido, Coron, and Puerto Princesa feel like three different trips. El Nido is about towering limestone cliffs and lagoon-hopping, Coron is about lakes and WWII wrecks, and Puerto Princesa is about city convenience and the Underground River.

If you are starting from Cebu, all three are now reachable without going through Manila. Cebu has direct flights to Puerto Princesa, El Nido (Lio Airport), Busuanga for Coron, and San Vicente for Port Barton. That changes how you plan a Palawan loop — you can pick one base or stitch two together.

Here is how El Nido, Coron, and Puerto Princesa compare, and how to get there from Cebu with current options.

## 1. El Nido — Best for Lagoons and Classic Island Hopping

El Nido town sits on Bacuit Bay, facing a maze of limestone islands. The town beach itself is busy with boats and not ideal for swimming, but the island tours are the main event.

**Island tours:** Tour A covers Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon, Secret Lagoon, Shimizu Island, and Seven Commandos Beach. Tour C adds Hidden Beach, Matinloc Shrine, and Helicopter Island. Tour B and D cover caves and more remote beaches. Boats leave between 8:30 and 9:30 AM, return around 4 PM. Big Lagoon now has a timed entry and a separate fee to manage crowds.

**Beaches on land:** Corong Corong, a kilometer south of town, is calmer for swimming. Lio Beach is wider and hosts resorts with sunset views. Nacpan Beach, 14 km north, is long and less built up — better for a beach day without boats.

**Vibe:** Lively but compact. More backpacker bars, dive shops, and restaurants than Coron town, with higher beachfront prices.

**Best for:** First-time Palawan visitors, photographers, island-hopping focused trips, and those who want limestone scenery every day.

**What to do:** Do Tour A first if you have only one day, then Tour C. Book Big Lagoon entry early — it sells out before other stops. Stay in Corong Corong or Lio if you want to swim near your hotel.

## 2. Coron — Best for Lakes, Lagoons, and Wreck Diving

Coron town is on Busuanga Island, not on Coron Island itself. The lakes and lagoons everyone talks about — Kayangan Lake, Barracuda Lake, Twin Lagoon — are on Coron Island, a short boat ride away.

**Lakes and lagoons:** Kayangan Lake is often called the cleanest lake in the country, with clear water and a steep 300-step climb to the viewpoint. Twin Lagoon has warm and cold layers where fresh and seawater meet. Barracuda Lake has dramatic underwater cliffs and thermoclines. These are included in Coron Tour A and Tour B.

**Wreck diving:** Coron Bay holds a dozen WWII Japanese wrecks sunk in 1944, most at 10 to 40 meters. They are among the most accessible wreck dives in the world — some masts start at 3 meters, making them visible to snorkellers on calm days. Popular wrecks include Skeleton Wreck, East Tangat, and Irako.

**Town:** Quieter than El Nido, with a baywalk, hot springs (Maquinit, 20 to 30 minutes by tricycle), and easy access to dive shops. Fewer party bars, more dive-focused evenings.

**Best for:** Divers, snorkellers who want better coral than El Nido town, and travelers who prefer nature and adventure over nightlife.

**What to do:** If you do not dive, book an island tour that includes Skeleton Wreck snorkeling — you can see structure without a tank. Bring aqua shoes for Kayangan's steps.

## 3. Puerto Princesa — Best for Access, Families, and the Underground River

Puerto Princesa is Palawan's capital and transport hub. It has a proper city center, malls, hospitals, and the most accommodation choices at lower prices than El Nido or Coron.

**Underground River:** The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site about 80 km north of the city. You need a permit, a boat from Sabang Wharf, and a guided paddle inside the cave. Day trips from the city take 8 to 10 hours including transfers. Book permits at least a day ahead — same-day slots fill, especially December to May.

**Honda Bay:** Island hopping here is shorter and more family-friendly than El Nido's — islands like Starfish Island and Luli Island have shallow swimming areas.

**City itself:** Baywalk, markets, and museums, but not a beach town. If your priority is white sand every day, you will want to move on to El Nido, Port Barton, or Coron.

**Best for:** First-night arrivals, families who want easier logistics, and travelers focused on the Underground River without long van rides.

**What to do:** Use Puerto Princesa as an entry or exit point, not a long base, unless you are combining it with Port Barton (about 3 hours by van) or Sabang.

## El Nido vs Coron vs Puerto Princesa — Quick Comparison

- **Scenery:** El Nido wins for limestone cliffs and lagoons; Coron wins for lakes and clear inland water; Puerto Princesa wins for the underground river cave.
- **Beach swimming near town:** Coron town has limited swimming, El Nido town is crowded but nearby Lio and Nacpan are better, Puerto Princesa city has no real beach but Honda Bay islands do.
- **Diving/snorkeling:** Coron for wrecks and lakes; El Nido for reef and lagoons; Puerto Princesa for Honda Bay's easy reefs.
- **Accessibility from Cebu:** Puerto Princesa is cheapest and most frequent; El Nido Lio Airport is fastest to El Nido town but usually pricier; Busuanga for Coron is direct and about 1 hour from Cebu.
- **Costs:** Puerto Princesa generally cheapest for hotels and food; El Nido beachfront highest; Coron mid-range but dive costs add up.
- **Nightlife:** El Nido most active, Coron quiet and dive-oriented, Puerto Princesa city bars and restaurants.
- **Crowds:** El Nido Big Lagoon and Coron Kayangan Lake get crowded by mid-morning; go with early departures.

## Getting to Palawan from Cebu — Direct Options

Cebu now has direct flights to all three gateways, which is why Palawan's tourism tagline in Cebu is "Duol ra diay ang Palawan" — Palawan is nearer than you think.

**Cebu (CEB) to Puerto Princesa (PPS):** Daily flights by Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines. Flight time just over an hour, often the cheapest Palawan leg. Good if you plan Underground River and then a van to El Nido or Port Barton.

**Cebu (CEB) to El Nido (ENI - Lio Airport):** Direct flights by Cebgo/AirSWIFT and Cebu Pacific. Flight time around 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes. You land in El Nido itself, 5 km from Lio Beach and 15 minutes from town, saving the 5 to 6-hour van from Puerto Princesa. Fares are usually higher than PPS.

**Cebu (CEB) to Busuanga/Coron (USU):** Direct flights by Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, and Sunlight Air. Flight time around 1 hour. From Busuanga airport, a van to Coron town takes 30 to 45 minutes, PHP 150 to PHP 250 shared, PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500 private.

**Cebu (CEB) to San Vicente (SWL - Port Barton):** Direct flights by Cebu Pacific/AirSWIFT. San Vicente is about 2 hours from Port Barton and 4 to 5 hours from El Nido by van, useful for a quieter entry point.

**Practical routing:**
- For one destination: Fly Cebu to that gateway and back. No Manila needed.
- For two destinations: Common combos are Cebu → El Nido → ferry to Coron (El Nido to Coron ferry 4 to 5 hours, PHP 1,800 to PHP 3,500) → Coron → Cebu, or Cebu → Puerto Princesa → van to El Nido → fly El Nido → Cebu.
- Budget option: Cebu → Puerto Princesa → shared van to El Nido (5 to 6 hours, PHP 600 to PHP 1,000 per person) → van to Port Barton if you want.

**Ferry alternative:** There is no direct Cebu to Palawan ferry. You must fly.

## When to Go and How Many Days

**Best months:** November to May is dry season, with calmer seas for island hopping. March to May is hottest and most crowded. June to October is Habagat, with higher chance of boat cancellations. December to February is cooler and less humid, with good visibility.

**Suggested stays:**
- 1 destination: 4 to 5 days.
- El Nido + Coron: 7 to 10 days, with the ferry between them.
- Puerto Princesa + El Nido: 6 to 8 days, including Underground River and island hopping.
- All three: 10 to 14 days if you include travel days.

If you can only pick one from Cebu: Choose El Nido for classic Palawan limestone lagoons, Coron for diving and lakes, Puerto Princesa if you want the Underground River with easy, affordable flights and less boat time.

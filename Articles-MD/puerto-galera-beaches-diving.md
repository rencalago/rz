# Puerto Galera Beaches and Diving

Puerto Galera is the closest real beach-and-dive combo to Manila, and that is why it has stayed busy for decades. It sits on the northern tip of Oriental Mindoro, facing the Verde Island Passage that marine biologists call the center of the center of marine biodiversity. You can be on a white-sand beach in the morning and on a wall dive with sea fans by lunch.

The town is spread across several coves. White Beach in Barangay San Isidro is the long stretch of sand with bars, souvenir shops, and fire dancers at night. Sabang and Small Lalaguna, a 15-minute ride over the hill, is the dive center — where every second building is a dive shop, gear room, or compressor house, and the nightlife runs later.

Here is how White Beach and Sabang compare, which dive sites are worth your time and money, how to get there from Manila and from Cebu, what it costs right now, and when to go.

## 1. White Beach — The Classic Beach Strip

White Beach is a 1-kilometer curve of light sand facing southwest. The swimming area is wide and shallow at low tide, with outrigger boats parked at the ends. Inland is a row of restaurants serving grilled seafood, pancit, and San Mig, plus budget inns and mid-range resorts.

It is not a quiet escape in peak season. Expect music from beach bars from about 4 PM onward, island-hopping sellers offering boat tours in the morning, and a busy sunset crowd. If you want calm, walk to the western end near Aninuan or book a resort slightly set back from the beach road.

**Best for:** First-time visitors, beach-focused groups, and travelers who want easy swimming without hiring a dive boat. Good for families who stay in White Beach but want to day-trip to Sabang for one dive.

**What to do:** Swim early before boats create wake, book a half-day island hopping tour to see Coral Garden and Bayanan Beach (PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,800 per small boat split between 4-6 people), try paraw sailing at sunset (PHP 800 to PHP 1,200 per hour), and eat at the grill stalls. White Beach has no dive cleaning stations right off the beach — you will take a boat from Sabang or Muelle for serious dives.

**Location:** White Beach, San Isidro, Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro. 10 to 15 minutes by e-trike/habal-habal from Balatero Port if you are coming from Batangas.

## 2. Sabang and Small Lalaguna — The Dive Hub

Sabang is smaller, denser, and far more focused on diving. The beach itself is narrow and not ideal for lounging, but the reef starts close to shore. Every operator here does daily fun dives, night dives, and tech dives. Small Lalaguna Beach, just around the point, is slightly quieter and fronts many of the better dive resorts like Atlantis, El Galleon, and Scandi Divers.

This is where most of the 40+ named dive sites are accessed within 5 to 15 minutes by small outrigger dive boat.

**Best for:** Certified divers, underwater photographers, and night owls who do not mind loud bars after diving. Non-divers can still snorkel Small Lalaguna at high tide and book introductory Discover Scuba Diving.

**What to do:** Walk the strip to compare dive shops, book a night dive at Sabang Wrecks, and eat at the small bistros that cater to dive crews. If you are not diving, book a snorkel trip to Coral Garden and Giant Clams — PHP 600 to PHP 1,000 per person on a joiner boat.

## 3. Top Dive Sites Around Puerto Galera

Diving here covers every level, from 5-meter coral gardens to 35-meter drift dives.

**Canyons:** The signature advanced site. Three underwater canyons at 25 to 35 meters with strong currents, schools of bannerfish, jacks, and occasional white-tip reef sharks. Best with nitrox and at slack tide. Usually a second or third dive of the day.

**Verde Island Drop-off / Pinnacle:** A day trip east of Puerto Galera, 45 to 60 minutes by big boat. A wall that drops to 70+ meters with excellent visibility, often 20 to 30 meters, and dense fish life. Day trip includes 2 to 3 dives with lunch on Verde Island. Operators require at least 4 divers to run it.

**Sabang Wrecks:** Three small wrecks at 18 to 20 meters in front of Sabang Bay. Perfect for night dives — mandarin fish, seahorses, frogfish, and nudibranchs. Depth makes it accessible to Open Water divers.

**Coral Garden:** 5 to 12 meters, protected and calm. Good for check dives, beginners, and snorkelers. Brain coral, giant clams, turtles.

**Hole in the Wall, Washing Machine / The Hill, Sinandigan Wall, Ernie's Cave:** Intermediate drift and wall sites at 15 to 28 meters, rich in sea fans, soft corals, and macro critters.

**Best for:** Divers who want variety without long boat rides, and photographers who want both macro and wide-angle in one trip.

**What to do:** Plan at least 2 days of diving — one day around Puerto Galera (Canyons + Sabang Wrecks night dive), one day to Verde Island if conditions allow.

## 4. How to Get There From Manila — Batangas Ferry Route

This is the only practical public route. No flights go directly to Puerto Galera.

**Manila to Batangas Port:** From Cubao or PITX, take JAM Liner, JAC Liner, or Ceres to Batangas Grand Terminal (2 to 3 hours, PHP 250 to PHP 350), then a jeepney or tricycle to Batangas Pier / Batangas Port Passenger Terminal Building 3 (10 minutes, PHP 30 to PHP 50). SiKAT and other bus-ferry combos sell a joint ticket Manila to Puerto Galera for PHP 800 to PHP 1,200 that includes bus and ferry.

**Batangas Port to Puerto Galera:** Three main companies — Galerian, Island Water, and Montenegro Lines. Ferries now all land at Balatero Port or Muelle Port, NOT directly at White Beach or Sabang. Fastcraft travel time 1 to 1.5 hours, RORO 1.5 to 2 hours. First trip around 6:30 AM to 8:00 AM, last trip around 5:30 PM to 6:00 PM — afternoon seas are rougher, go in the morning.

Fares: Fastcraft PHP 500 to PHP 700 one-way, RORO PHP 400 to PHP 500. Batangas terminal fee PHP 30 to PHP 100. Roundtrip combo tickets around PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,400.

**Balatero Port to White Beach / Sabang:** Tricycle, e-trike, or habal-habal 10 to 20 minutes. PHP 150 to PHP 300 per vehicle to White Beach, PHP 250 to PHP 400 to Sabang. After 6 PM, expect PHP 50 to PHP 100 extra. Some resorts offer free pickup if you message your ETA.

## 5. How to Get There From Cebu

There are no direct ferries from Cebu to Puerto Galera. Go via Manila.

**Step 1 — Cebu to Manila:** Fly Cebu (CEB) to Manila (MNL) or Batangas via Manila. Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, AirAsia fly 12 to 18 times daily. Flight time 1 hour 30 minutes to 1 hour 45 minutes. One-way promo fares PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,000, regular PHP 2,500 to PHP 5,000 with bag.

**Step 2 — Manila to Batangas to Puerto Galera:** Same as Manila route above. From NAIA, take a Grab or PITX bus to Batangas Grand Terminal, then ferry. If you land in Manila before 10 AM, you can usually make an afternoon ferry same day. If you land after 2 PM, stay overnight in Manila or Batangas City — last ferry is around 5:30 PM to 6 PM.

**Time budget from Cebu:** Cebu City to Puerto Galera beach hotel is 8 to 11 hours door-to-door if you connect same day, longer if you overnight. Many travelers do Cebu-Manila flight day 1, stay in Manila or Batangas, then ferry to Puerto Galera early day 2 to avoid stress.

**Alternative Cebu-Pacific route:** Cebu to Batangas City is faster if you fly to Manila and take a bus immediately from PITX to Batangas. Total bus+ferry from PITX is about 4 to 5 hours.

## 6. Costs, Fees, and Diving Prices in PHP

**Permits / environmental fees:** 
- Muelle/Balatero terminal environmental user fee (EUF) for Puerto Galera: PHP 100 to PHP 120 per person for tourists, PHP 50 to PHP 80 for Mindoro residents / locals, sometimes charged as PHP 50 at the port and PHP 70 at the tourist desk. 
- Barangay fee / snorkeling fee at some beaches: PHP 20 to PHP 50.
- Apo Reef? Not here. But for diving, some shops collect a marine park fee PHP 100 to PHP 150 per day if they go to protected areas.

**Diving:**
- Single fun boat dive: PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,800 (USD 30 to 50) including guide, tank, weights, boat. 
- Package of 5 dives: PHP 7,500 to PHP 12,000 (per-dive rate drops).
- Package of 10 dives: PHP 14,000 to PHP 22,000.
- Full gear rental: PHP 500 to PHP 900 per day, often free if you book 5+ dives.
- Night dive supplement: PHP 300 to PHP 800 on top of fun dive.
- Nitrox: PHP 300 to PHP 500 per tank, or PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,500 unlimited per day.
- Verde Island day trip (2 dives + lunch + boat, min 4 pax): PHP 4,500 to PHP 7,500 per person.
- Open Water certification: PHP 18,000 to PHP 25,000 (3 days).
- Discover Scuba (intro): PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,500.

**Hotels:** 
- White Beach budget fan room: PHP 1,200 to PHP 2,500 per night.
- White Beach mid-range aircon beachfront: PHP 3,500 to PHP 7,000.
- Sabang / Small Lalaguna dive resort: PHP 3,000 to PHP 8,500 per night including breakfast; many have dive+room packages.

**Food:** Carinderia meal PHP 150 to PHP 300, beach restaurant PHP 350 to PHP 650 per dish.

## 7. Best Months to Visit Puerto Galera

**Dry season November to May is best overall.** Northeast monsoon (Amihan) dominates December to April with sunny days, calm seas, and best beach swimming. Visibility for diving is good year-round but peaks October to June.

- **December to February:** Coolest and driest, 24 to 30°C, calm mornings. Busiest around Christmas, New Year, Chinese New Year. Book ahead.
- **March to May:** Hottest and sunniest, 28 to 33°C, calmer seas for Verde Island day trips. Holy Week is extremely crowded — rates double and ferries fill up. April and May have fewer rain interruptions.
- **June to September (Habagat):** Rainier, more afternoon downpours, choppier seas, occasional ferry cancellations. Diving still runs, with fewer tourists and sometimes better visibility when plankton drops. Water warmer.
- **October to November:** Shoulder — mix of sunny days and early northeast winds, good deals.

For diving from Cebu, aim for March to May for the calmest Verde Island crossings, or December to February if you also want beach weather. Avoid late July to September if you are on a tight ferry schedule.

## Tips Before You Go

### Do not expect direct Batangas to White Beach ferries

Public ferries now all arrive at Balatero or Muelle. Anyone selling you a ticket that says direct to White Beach or Sabang is selling a private charter or a transfer add-on. Budget the last-mile tricycle ride and confirm drop-off with your hotel.

### Bring cash and small bills

ATMs in White Beach and Sabang exist but run out or charge PHP 250 per withdrawal. Dive shops, bars, and ports prefer cash. Pay environmental and terminal fees in cash even if you booked ferry online.

### Choose your base by priority

Stay in White Beach if your priority is sand, swimming, sunset, and cheaper restaurants. Stay in Sabang / Small Lalaguna if your priority is diving before 8 AM, night dives, and not commuting. You can visit the other cove in 15 to 25 minutes by tricycle (PHP 150 to PHP 300).

### Book Verde Island early in your stay

Verde trips need 4+ divers and calm weather. Put it on day 2, not day 5, so you have a buffer for weather cancellations. Confirm include: boat, guide, tanks, weights, lunch, fees.

### Protect your ears and manage currents

Canyons and many walls have current. Bring or rent a dive computer, a surface marker buoy, and reef hook if you have one. Listen to briefing on equalization and drift procedures. Avoid diving with a cold — boats are short and clinics are basic.

### Respect fees and beach rules

Puerto Galera charges environmental fees that fund reef protection and trash management. Keep your receipt — some checkpoints ask for it. No jetting in swimming zones, follow lifeguard flags at White Beach, and avoid touching coral or taking shells.

## So, White Beach or Sabang, and Is It Worth It From Cebu?

If you have three days and want beach time, stay in White Beach, day-trip to Sabang once for a fun dive or snorkel at Coral Garden, and keep one island-hopping morning free. If you have four to five days and diving is the point, base in Sabang or Small Lalaguna, do Canyons, Hole in the Wall, and Sabang Wrecks, and add Verde Island on the calmest day.

From Cebu, Puerto Galera is not a weekend hop like Moalboal or Malapascua — it requires a flight to Manila plus a bus and ferry. But if you are already in Manila or planning a longer Luzon loop, the mix of White Beach ease, Sabang nightlife, and cheap, varied diving within 10 minutes of shore makes it worth the journey. Go November to May, bring cash, book your first two dive days in advance, and aim for a morning ferry to avoid rough afternoon crossings.

# Valentine's Day Getaways in the Philippines: 8 Romantic Ideas

Valentine's Day falls right in the best part of dry season. Seas are calm, skies are mostly clear, and domestic flights are busy but still manageable if you book early. If you want more than a dinner reservation, the Philippines makes it easy to turn February 14 into a short trip.

This guide rounds up romantic getaways that actually work for a Valentine's weekend, from luxury island stays in Palawan to quick escapes near Manila, plus quieter picks like Siquijor and Dumaguete. Expect practical tips on where to base, what couples actually do there, and how to avoid the Valentine's price surge.

## 1. El Nido, Palawan: Classic Island Romance

El Nido is the Philippines' Valentine's default for a reason. Bacuit Bay gives you limestone cliffs, hidden lagoons, and that sunset light that makes even a simple beach dinner feel special.

Base yourself at Lio Beach for convenience. Lio is a planned beach strip with resorts, restaurants, and direct access to El Nido town by trike. Seda Lio and other Lio properties work well for couples who want beachfront without going completely off-grid. If you want more seclusion, look at private island resorts like Pangulasian Island or Lagen Island, both known for honeymoon setups, private beach dinners, and curated Valentine's programs in February.

What couples do: daytime island hopping to Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon, and Secret Lagoon, then sunset sailing or a low-key cocktail at Maremegmeg Beach. February waters are typically calm, which helps if one of you gets seasick.

Trade-off: El Nido is expensive in February and flights to El Nido Airport (Lio) sell out fast. Book at least 2 to 3 months ahead, and keep one buffer day in case of wind-related tour cancellations.

## 2. Coron, Palawan: Quieter Luxury

If El Nido feels too packed, Coron offers a different flavor of Palawan. The town itself is functional, but the resorts around Busuanga Island and on private islets lean quiet and romantic. Discovery Coron and similar Coron resorts often run February packages with spa treatments and island picnics.

Romantic angle here is nature immersion: Twin Lagoon, Kayangan Lake, hot springs at Maquinit, and fewer crowds than El Nido. For couples who prefer snorkeling over parties, Coron wins.

Tip: Check whether your tour includes Coron island entrance fees and environmental fees. Most do now, but confirm at booking so you are not surprised at the dock.

## 3. Tagaytay: The Closest Getaway from Manila

Tagaytay remains the most booked Valentine's spot near Metro Manila. Two hours from the city, cool air, Taal Volcano views, and a concentration of romantic hotels and restaurants make it easy to plan a one or two night escape.

**Where to stay:** Taal Vista Hotel is the long-time landmark with Valentine's dining packages, while newer options like Quest Hotel Tagaytay and Discovery Country Suites offer suite promos in February. Crosswinds Tagaytay, with its Swiss-inspired enclave and restaurants like Napa and Cafe Voila, is popular for couples who want cafes, flower fields, and viewpoints in one area.

**What to eat:** Balay Dako, Antonio's, Marcia Adams in Alfonso, and the restaurants along Tagaytay-Nasugbu Highway all fill up for Valentine's dinners. Reserve at least two weeks before February 14, and have a backup spot within walking distance.

Honest note: Traffic on Aguinaldo Highway on Valentine's weekend can be heavy from late afternoon. Leave early, stay overnight, and avoid driving back to Manila on Sunday evening.

## 4. Siquijor: Slow, Mystical, and Made for Two

Siquijor has quietly become a favorite for couples who want beach without the Boracay crowds. The island is small enough to circle in a day by scooter, with waterfalls, white-sand coves, and low-rise resorts that prioritize quiet.

**Where to stay:** San Juan area is the hub. Coco Grove Beach Resort is the established beachfront name with spa and pool, while smaller options like Salamangka Resort, Infinity Heights Resort, and Aquamare Beach Camp lean more intimate and couples-focused. Many resorts offer kawa hot baths and couples massage that get fully booked around Valentine's.

**What to do together:** Cambugahay Falls in the morning, a slow lunch in San Juan, sunset at Paliton Beach, and a rented scooter ride to Lazi Church and old convent. Several local operators offer private romantic kawa bath setups with dinner on the beach, which is exactly the kind of low-effort, high-romance add-on that works for Valentine's.

Getting there: Fly into Dumaguete, then take a fast ferry to Siquijor. OceanJet and Montenegro run multiple daily trips, about 45 minutes to an hour. Book ferry seats online in February.

## 5. Dumaguete and Dauin: City Comfort, Beach Calm

Dumaguete works if one of you likes city conveniences and the other wants beach. The city itself has Rizal Boulevard for sunset walks, good coffee shops, and straightforward access to nearby escapes.

Stay in Dumaguete for food and nightlife, or base in Dauin, 30 minutes south, for beachfront resorts and Apo Island diving. Dauin resorts often include dive packages, which makes a good shared activity even for beginners. Try a half-day discover dive course together rather than committing to full certification.

For Valentine's Day itself, many Dumaguete restaurants do set menus, and boulevard hotels offer bay-view rooms. It is less about a grand resort program and more about easy, affordable romance.

## 6. Boracay and Beyond: If You Want the Classic Beach Party

Boracay is not in your original four, but it deserves a quick mention because it consistently ranks as the most searched Valentine's beach. Henann and Discovery Shores properties usually curate private beach dinners, fire shows, and couples spa deals for Valentine's week.

If you want something in the same region but quieter, consider Lio Beach or nearby Iloilo and Antique beach towns. Same sunset, fewer sunbeds.

## 7. Budget to Luxury: How Much to Expect

**Budget (PHP 3,000 to 6,000 per night for two):** Guesthouses and boutique inns in Tagaytay ridge, Dumaguete city, and Siquijor interior. Expect fan or basic AC rooms, breakfast sometimes included. You will spend more on activities and dining out.

**Mid-range (PHP 7,000 to 15,000 per night):** This is the sweet spot for Valentine's. Three to four star hotels in Tagaytay, beachfront cottages in Siquijor, dive resorts in Dauin, and Lio Beach resorts in El Nido. Most February promos bundle dinner or spa.

**Luxury (PHP 18,000 and up):** Private island resorts in Palawan, premium villas in Tagaytay, and Boracay five-stars. Valentine's packages often include airport transfers, private dinners, and spa credits. These sell out first.

Instead of quoting a single package price that will be outdated by next year, check the resort's official site for February offers and compare with at least one booking platform. Prices shift quickly in the week before February 14.

## 8. Booking Tips That Actually Save You Hassle

**Book early, but not blindly.** For Palawan and Boracay, 2 to 3 months ahead is ideal. For Tagaytay, one month ahead is usually enough, but restaurants need earlier reservation. Confirm cancellation policies, especially for beach resorts where weather can delay boats.

**Use February to your advantage.** February is peak dry season. You get better odds of clear skies than in June, but it is also peak domestic travel. Weekday check-ins on February 12 or 13 are cheaper than Friday, February 14.

**Transport first, hotel second.** Flights from Manila to Palawan and Dumaguete jump fastest. If you lock flights, you have leverage to choose hotels around your arrival times. For Siquijor, buy ferry tickets online once flights are set.

**Have a plan B for dinner.** Even with reservations, Valentine's dinners run late. In Tagaytay and Dumaguete, pick a street or area with multiple options so you can walk to another spot if needed.

**Ask about fees up front.** Ask about environmental fees, island hopping government fees, and corkage if you plan to bring wine. The tourism office at the dock or the resort front desk can give you the current list.

**Keep expectations honest.** Island destinations involve boats, trikes, and occasional brownouts. Luxury resorts buffer that well; budget stays less so. Pack patience along with sunscreen.

### Quick Itinerary Ideas

**For Manila couples with 2 days:** Drive to Tagaytay early Friday, check into a ridge-view hotel, wine tasting at Crosswinds, dinner at Balay Dako or Marcia Adams, breakfast with Taal view, then late lunch before driving back late Saturday to avoid traffic.

**For beach without long haul:** Fly to Dumaguete Saturday morning, lunch at Rizal Boulevard, fast ferry to Siquijor, sunset kawa bath, overnight in San Juan, Cambugahay Falls Sunday, ferry back Monday morning.

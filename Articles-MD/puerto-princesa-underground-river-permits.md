# Puerto Princesa Underground River: Permits, Booking and Getting There from Cebu

The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, or PPUR, is one of those rare places that lives up to its UNESCO World Heritage title. It protects an 8.2-kilometer underground river — one of the longest navigable cave rivers in the world — that winds through limestone karst before emptying into the West Philippine Sea. You enter by motorized bangka from Sabang Beach, then switch to a paddle boat guided by a park ranger inside the cave. No lights, no commentary speakers, just headlamps, stalactites shaped like mushrooms and cathedrals, and thousands of bats.

The catch is access. PPUR has a strict daily cap of 900-1,000 visitors, and you cannot just show up in Sabang and board a boat. Every visitor needs a Visitor Entry Permit (VEP) issued by the Puerto Princesa government. This is the single most important thing to sort out before you book a van or hotel.

If you are coming from Cebu, the good news is you can fly direct to Puerto Princesa almost daily, making a long weekend for the Underground River doable without Manila layovers. Here is how permits work, what it really costs in PHP, how to get there from Cebu and Manila, and what to bring.

## 1. The PPUR UNESCO Site — What You Are Visiting

PPUR is located 81 km north of Puerto Princesa City in the village of Sabang, on the northwest coast of Palawan. The national park was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site on December 4, 1999, and later named one of the New7Wonders of Nature. It protects old-growth forest, limestone cliffs, and a full mountain-to-sea ecosystem, but the star is the cave itself.

Only the first 4.3 km of the 8.2 km river is allowed for mapping, and tourists see the first 1.5 km on the standard tour. That is enough: you glide through chambers named Holywell, Cathedral, and Devil's Hole, with an audio guide that explains formations. Photography with flash is not allowed, and talking loudly is discouraged because of wildlife.

The jump-off point is Sabang Wharf. All boats to the cave entrance leave from here, and the ride takes 20 minutes in good weather, longer when the sea is choppy. From the cave beach, a short forest trail leads to the cave mouth where you board the smaller paddle boat.

**Best for:** First-time Palawan visitors, families, and anyone who wants a UNESCO site without strenuous hiking.

**What to do:** Combine the river with nearby Sabang sights if you have time — Sabang X Zipline (800m over the sea, PHP 800-900), Ugong Rock cave with spelunking, and a mangrove paddle. Most day tours from the city already include one side trip.

## 2. Permits and Booking — How to Get Your VEP

### Option A — Join a Group Tour (Easiest for Cebu travelers)

Most visitors book a shared PPUR tour from Puerto Princesa City. The tour operator secures the permit for you, which is why solo booking can be harder in peak months. A standard joining tour costs PHP 2,200 to PHP 2,850 per person from the city and already includes:
- City Coliseum Visitor Entry Permit (VEP) — PHP 250 for foreign / PHP included for operator allotment
- Sabang boat transfers (motor banca roundtrip)
- Audio guide rental (PHP 85)
- Licensed guide and van transfer from city hotel (roundtrip 2-2.5 hours each way)
- Buffet lunch in Sabang and environmental fee (PHP 150)

You just provide a full name, birthdate, and a valid ID copy one to two days before. Operators book permits through the city tourism system daily. In peak season (mid-December to May, especially Holy Week), book 3-7 days ahead. Daily slots sell out, especially December and January.

### Option B — DIY Booking at City Coliseum

If you want to go DIY without a tour package, you must secure the permit yourself.

**Where:** Underground River Booking Office, City Coliseum, Barangay San Pedro, National Highway, Puerto Princesa City. Near the airport, reachable by tricycle PHP 50-100.
**Phone:** (048) 434-2509 / Visit Puerto Princesa Facebook page for hours — usually Monday-Saturday 8am-5pm.
**What to bring:** Valid government ID for each person.
**Process:**
1. Go to the Booking Office in person. Online booking through the official Puerto Princesa App or city tourism website (citytourism.puertoprincesa.gov.ph) has been rolled out but is intermittent — many travelers still report having to go in person. If the online portal is up, you can reserve a date and pay the PHP 250 permit fee there.
2. Pay PHP 250 VEP + PHP 85 audio device. You get a permit slip with date.
3. On your tour day, travel to Sabang, then at Sabang Information Center / Tourism Office at the wharf, pay the additional fees: Environmental Fee PHP 150 (PHP 120 for senior/PWD/minor), and motor boat fee about PHP 1,300-1,600 per boat divided by up to 6-8 pax (so PHP 200-270 per person). If the sea is rough, your slot can be moved or cancelled — they prioritize safety.

**Cost breakdown DIY (per person, 2024-2025 rates):**
- Permit VEP: PHP 250
- Audio guide: PHP 85
- Environmental fee: PHP 150
- Sabang motor boat (shared): PHP 200-300
- Van one-way San Jose Terminal to Sabang: PHP 300-400 (Lexxus Shuttle, Cherry Bus), private van PHP 3,500-6,000 split
- Tricycle in Sabang: PHP 50
**Total DIY from Puerto Princesa City without lunch:** ~PHP 1,085-1,470 per person if you share boat and van — similar to group tours but more logistics.

**Tips:** Book 2-3 days before in dry season. Bring ID photocopy — they encode it. If you are in Sabang the day before, you can try at Sabang Tourism Office but slots are fewer than city allocation.

**Best for:** DIY travelers staying overnight in Sabang who have time to go to Coliseum a day early.

**What to do:** If online portal is down, message a local operator on Facebook or Viber to hold a permit while you arrange transport. Many offer permit-only service for PHP 400-500.

## 3. How to Get to Sabang — From City and From Cebu

**Puerto Princesa City to Sabang (81 km, 1.5-2.5 hours):**
- **Public van:** From San Jose Terminal (New Market), vans leave when full, 5am-5pm, PHP 300-400 single, PHP 600-700 roundtrip. Travel time 2 hours paved but last 10 km is rough and winding.
- **Bus:** Lexxus Shuttle and Cherry Transport aircon buses, PHP 250-350, 2.5-3 hours, less frequent.
- **Private van:** PHP 3,500-5,500 one-way for 10-12 seater, PHP 6,000-8,000 roundtrip in one day including waiting. Good if you are 4+ pax.
- **Rented van with tour:** Included in joining tour cost.

Sabang has accommodations from PHP 1,200 guesthouses to Daluyon Beach Resort (PHP 6,000+). If you plan to go early morning for calmer seas and fewer crowds, sleeping in Sabang the night before helps — first boats leave 8am.

### How to Get There from Cebu

**Direct Option — Cebu (CEB) to Puerto Princesa (PPS):**
This is the most practical for Cebu-based travelers.

- **Airlines:** Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines have 1-2 direct flights daily, AirAsia flies same route on some days. Flight time 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes.
- **Schedule:** Morning and afternoon departures from Mactan. Return flights typically late afternoon from PPS.
- **Cost:** Seat sale PHP 899-1,800 one-way, regular PHP 1,500-3,500 one-way, roundtrip PHP 2,000-5,000 if booked 2-4 weeks ahead. Without sale, expect PHP 4,000-6,000 RT.
- **Airport to city:** PPS airport is 3 km from city center, tricycle PHP 50-100, van free if joining tour with pickup.
- **Same-day to Sabang?** If you land before 9am, you can make an afternoon boat slot, but you must already have a permit booked yesterday. Safer to arrive day before, get permit, then go next day.

**Via Manila:**

- **CEB to MNL:** Hourly flights 1h20m, PHP 1,200-4,000 OW. Then MNL to PPS 1h30m, multiple flights daily by Cebu Pacific/PAL/Philippine AirAsia, PHP 1,200-4,500 OW.
- **Why via Manila:** Useful if Cebu-PPS direct is full or expensive during peak, or you want more time options. Overnight in Manila if connection is tight.
- **Ferry not viable:** No direct passenger ferry Cebu to Palawan. 2GO Travel goes Cebu-Manila-Puerto Princesa via long RORO that takes 2 days, not recommended for a short trip.

**Sample cost from Cebu (3D2N, per person sharing):**
- Flights RT: PHP 2,500-5,000
- 2 nights hotel in Puerto Princesa: PHP 1,200-2,500 budget, PHP 3,500-6,000 mid-range
- PPUR joining tour with permit/lunch: PHP 2,200-2,850
- Terminal fee, tricycle, meals: PHP 800-1,200
**Total:** PHP 6,700-11,550 budget to mid-range, without island hopping.

**Best for:** Travelers who want the fastest route from Cebu — CEB-PPS direct is ideal.

**What to do:** Book flights early morning arrival and late afternoon return to maximize days. Use day 1 for city tour/Baywalk/market, day 2 PPUR full day (leave 6:30-7am, back 4-5pm), day 3 for Honda Bay or return flight.

## 4. Best Months, What to Bring, and What to Expect

**Best months — November to May is dry season:**
- **Peak best: March to May** — Calm seas, boats rarely cancelled, sunny cave photos. Hot (32-34°C), humid. Best visibility for limestone cliffs. But also most crowded — permit quota fills weeks ahead for Holy Week and Christmas week.
- **Good: November to February, October to early December** — Amihan northeast monsoon, cooler, less humid, some wind. Sea can be choppy but still workable. December-January are busiest due to holidays.
- **Avoid if you want certainty: June to October** — Habagat southwest monsoon brings rain, rough seas, and boat cancellations. Sabang Wharf may close for days after typhoon swells. City tourism office can suspend trips. Not impossible, but have backup plans. June and October shoulders are less risky than July-September.

**What to bring / wear:**
- Valid ID (required for permit check at Sabang and cave entrance)
- Light quick-dry clothes — you may get splashed on banca
- Aqua shoes or sandals with strap — trail from boat to cave can be muddy
- Dry bag for phone/wallet, small backpack
- Water, snacks — tour lunch is usually buffet in Sabang at 1-2pm after river, not before
- Sunscreen and hat for wharf waiting area, insect repellent
- Cash — PHP 150 environmental fee and boat fees if DIY, plus tips for guides (PHP 50-100)
- No drone, no loud speaker, no flash photography inside cave. Life vest mandatory on both boats, provided.

**Duration:** Standard cave boat inside is 45 minutes to 1 hour. Total day from city: 8-10 hours including transfers and lunch. From Sabang: 2.5-3.5 hours.

**Food in Sabang:** Lunch is at local carenderia-style buffet for groups (chicken, fish, veggies, rice). If DIY, eateries near wharf sell meals PHP 150-250. Bring water.

**Environmental rules:** Do not touch stalactites, do not shout, follow ranger instructions. Cave has low oxygen in parts — if you have severe claustrophobia or asthma, tell the operator. Children allowed, but small boats can be bumpy for toddlers.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book permit first, then flights and vans

Never do it reverse. The daily cap is real — flights are easier to get than permits during December to April. If you land without a VEP, you may spend your whole Palawan stay waiting.

### Stay in Puerto Princesa City the night before your tour

Shared tours pick up 6:30-7:30am from city hotels. Staying near San Jose or city center saves time. If you stay in Sabang overnight, confirm with operator that pickup covers Sabang — some charge extra or require you to go back to city.

### Wear aqua shoes and bring motion sickness meds if needed

The Sabang to cave banca ride is 20 minutes but can be choppy, especially November-February. Take bonamine or similar 30 minutes before boat if you get seasick. The paddle boat inside is calm.

### Bring small bills and valid ID

ID check happens twice — at booking office and wharf. Environmental and boat fees require cash if DIY. Group tour includes it but bring PHP 500 extra for tips, water, and small entrance fees.

### Do not expect to swim at Sabang Beach on PPUR day

The tour schedule is tight. Sabang Beach water visibility is average and currents can be strong. If you want beach, add a day in Honda Bay (Starfish Island) or Port Barton / El Nido after PPUR.

## So, Should You Do the Underground River from Cebu?

Absolutely — PPUR is the easiest UNESCO site to tick from Cebu with a direct 1 hour 15 minute flight. Budget PHP 2,200-2,850 for a worry-free joining tour that already handles your permit, plus PHP 2,500-5,000 for Cebu-PPS roundtrip flights on sale. Go November to May for calmer seas and less chance of cancellation, and book your permit at least 2-3 days ahead (a week in peak).

Book via a reputable operator who shows the VEP on your voucher, leave early to get the first boats when wildlife is more active, and keep Sabang as an overnight if you want a slower pace and sunrise over the cliffs.

From Cebu, Palawan is now nearer than you think — just get the permit sorted first.

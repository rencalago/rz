# Samar Caves: Sohoton and Calbiga

Samar is the Philippines' caving capital for a reason. The island sits on one of the largest karst landscapes in the country, and two places stand out for first-time visitors: Sohoton Caves and Natural Bridge Park in Basey, Western Samar, and the Calbiga caves — better known as the Langun-Gobingob cave system — in Calbiga, Samar.

Sohoton is the easier, half-day river and cave tour built around a massive stone arch carved by the Sohoton River, with a short but impressive cave chamber inside. Calbiga's Langun-Gobingob is on another level — 12 chambers over roughly seven kilometers, 2,968 hectares of protected landscape, reputed as the largest cave system in the Philippines and the second largest in Southeast Asia. It was discovered by Italian Guido Rossi in 1987 and opened in 1990, protected under the National Cave Act.

Here is how Sohoton and Calbiga compare, what permits and guides you actually need right now, how to get there from Cebu via Tacloban or Calbayog, and when to go for safe boat rides and dry cave floors.

## 1. Sohoton Natural Bridge and Cave — Basey, Samar

Sohoton is managed by the Basey Municipal Tourism Office and the Samar Island Natural Park. You register in Basey town, then take a boat up the Sohoton River to the bridge.

**What to expect:** Motorized boat from Basey Visitor Center to Panhulugan area — about 30 to 45 minutes of nipa, limestone cliffs, and gold-tinted shallows on the Kadak-an River. At the park you transfer to a kayak or walk to the Natural Bridge, a huge limestone arch with the river flowing under it. The cave entrance is a short wade from the bridge. Inside, about 15 to 20 minutes one-way, with stalactites, bats, cave spiders. Life vests and helmets provided, headlamps required. Kayaking back through the gorge is often the highlight.

**Current fees (pay at Tourism Office or Visitor Center):**
- Entrance fee: PHP 25 local / PHP 200 foreign — some listings still show PHP 20 local / PHP 50 local for cave, confirm on arrival
- Return boat transfer Visitor Center to Panhulugan for 1-5 pax: PHP 500
- Long river cruise boat Basey to Sohoton and back: PHP 2,500 per boat
- Kadak-an / Golden River Environmental Fee: PHP 50
- Community/Tourist Fee: PHP 150
- Cave guide: PHP 300 per group
- Lighting fee / headlamp: PHP 300
- Kayak rental: PHP 50
- Kayak guide: PHP 150
- Guitarist / cultural performer: PHP 200 optional
- SSA Operational Expense: 15% surcharge on subtotal

All-in for a group of 1-5 on the standard boat is roughly PHP 2,400 to PHP 3,000 sharing, or PHP 500 to PHP 700 per person if you join others. If you take the full river cruise, budget PHP 4,000 to PHP 5,000 per boat.

**Permits:** No DENR permit needed for the main Sohoton short tour. Tourism office registration, environmental and community fees act as your permit. For extended caving beyond the main chamber, DENR-PENRO clearance may be required — ask Basey Tourism in advance.

**Best for:** First-time cavers, families with older kids, photographers, and day-trippers from Tacloban. Easy to combine with San Juanico Bridge.

**What to do:** Go early — tourism office opens 8 AM to 4 PM. Settle payment in town before going to the jump-off. Wear aqua shoes you can wade in, bring dry bag, headlamp if you have one, and cash in small bills.

## 2. Calbiga Caves — Langun-Gobingob Cave System

This is serious caving. Trexplore the Adventures (based in Catbalogan) is the accredited operator. They hold the mayor's permit, DENR / Samar Island Natural Park permit, and local guides. Independent entry without a guide is not allowed.

**The system:** Upper Gobingob, Langun, Lower Langun — total explored length around 6 to 7 kilometers. Chambers include Gobingob Campsite and Langun Campsite where you can actually camp overnight. There is no installed lighting, no walkways. You walk, crawl, wade through rivers inside.

**Tour types:**
- **Discovery Day Tour:** About 4-6 hours caving, entrance chambers only. Headlamp, helmet, gloves included. Good if you want a taste of Langun-Gobingob without camping.
- **Overnight 2D/1N:** Camp inside Gobingob. You need caving gear, food, porters.
- **Extreme 3D/2N:** Full traverse with 2 nights camping inside cave. You enter in Calbiga and exit via different barangay. Requires decent fitness.

**Current costs via Trexplore:**
- 1-day Discovery: around PHP 3,500 per person (min 4-5 pax), includes permits, caving gear, transport within Calbiga, food (breakfast/lunch), local porters, Trexplore cave master guide, photo documentation, caving certificate.
- 2-day and 3-day events: PHP 3,000 to PHP 3,500 per person quoted for organized spelunking festivals (includes certificate, shirt, souvenir kits, mayor's permit, DENR permit, guides). Private 3D/2N can run PHP 8,000 to PHP 12,000 per person all-in depending on group size and food.
- Habal-habal from Calbiga town to jump-off: PHP 150 to PHP 250 one-way.
- Add porters for overnight: often included, but tip PHP 300 to PHP 500 per porter per day.

**Permits:** Trexplore secures Mayor's Permit, Barangay clearance, DENR/Samar Island Natural Park permit, and insurance waiver. You need to send full name, age, emergency contact, and ID days ahead. Group size is controlled — max 12 to 15 per batch inside.

**Best for:** Experienced hikers, adventure travelers, and anyone who wants to camp inside one of the biggest cave systems in Asia. Not for claustrophobic or kids under 12.

**What to do:** Bring quick-dry clothes (2 sets), knee pads, sturdy shoes with grip (not flip-flops), headlamp plus backup, dry bag, 2L water, personal snacks. No phones outside dry bags — humidity is 90%+ inside. Listen to cave master — no touching formations beyond trail, no taking anything.

## 3. How to Get There from Cebu

Samar has no commercial airport inside the cave towns. You fly or sail to a gateway, then van/bus.

**Option A — Cebu to Tacloban (fastest for both Sohoton and Calbiga):**
- **Flight:** Cebu Pacific, PAL, AirAsia Cebu (CEB) to Tacloban (TAC) — 1h to 1h15m, daily. Promo one-way PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,000, regular PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,500.
- **Tacloban Airport to New Bus Terminal:** Jeep/tricycle 15 to 20 minutes, PHP 50 to PHP 150.
- **Tacloban to Basey for Sohoton:** Van or jeep at New Transport Terminal bound for Basey, 45 mins to 1 hour across San Juanico Bridge, PHP 70 to PHP 150. Tell driver Basey Tourism Office.
- **Tacloban to Calbiga for Langun-Gobingob:** Van to Calbiga or Catbalogan via Maharlika Highway, 1.5 to 2 hours, PHP 150 to PHP 250. Habal-habal to Brgy. Panayuran jump-off about 30 mins after that. Trexplore van pickup can be arranged in Tacloban if you book ahead — PHP 2,000 to PHP 2,500 per van.

**Option B — Cebu to Calbayog (better for northern Samar + Calbiga):**
- **Flight:** CebGo DG6546 Cebu to Calbayog (CYP) — 1h, 07:55 departure typical. One-way PHP 2,500 to PHP 5,000.
- **Ferry:** Cokaliong Cebu Pier 1 to Calbayog Maguinoo Port — Mon, Wed, Sat 7:00 PM, 10 to 12 hours overnight, Tourist class PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,800, Economy PHP 900 to PHP 1,200. Free shuttle from Maguinoo to Calbayog City. Roble Shipping Cebu to Catbalogan — Tue, Thu 7:00 PM, similar time and price.
- **Calbayog to Calbiga:** GrandTours van Calbayog to Catbalogan passes Calbiga, 1.5 hours, PHP 150 to PHP 200. From Catbalogan to Calbiga is 40 minutes reverse.
- **Calbayog to Basey for Sohoton:** Van Calbayog to Tacloban 3.5 to 4 hours, drop at Basey intersection, PHP 300 to PHP 400.

**Option C — Fast craft via Ormoc:** Cebu Pier 1 to Ormoc by OceanJet 2.5 to 3 hours, PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,600. Ormoc terminal to Tacloban van 2 hours, PHP 250 to PHP 350. Then Basey or Calbiga as above. Good if Tacloban flights are full or expensive.

**Best for:** Tacloban for speed and frequency. Calbayog flight/ferry if you want to see northern Samar rock formations (Marabut, Biri) on same trip.

## 5. Best Months to Visit

**November to May — Dry season, best for both:**
Sohoton boat rides are safest, water levels lower, cave floor less muddy. Calbiga chambers have less river flow inside, making camping feasible. March to May has least rain but hottest — cave stays cool at 22 to 26°C inside.

**December to February:** Cooler, still dry, good for trekking to Calbiga entrance. Sea for ferry crossings calmer.

**June to October — Avoid if possible:** Habagat brings heavy rain, Sohoton River can rise and park suspends boat tours. Langun River inside Calbiga floods quickly — Trexplore cancels trips when water level high. Also typhoon season in Eastern Visayas — August to October highest risk of ferry/flight cancellations.

If you must go wet season, book Sohoton early morning when river is lowest, and have 2 buffer days for Calbiga.

## Tips Before You Go

### Permits are handled differently for the two parks

For Sohoton, walk-in works — register at Basey Municipal Tourism Operations Office before 2 PM for same-day tour. For Calbiga Langun-Gobingob, do not walk in. Contact Trexplore or Calbiga Tourism 5 to 7 days ahead for DENR/SINP permits — they need names for insurance and clearance. Solo joiners are possible during announced caving festivals.

### Hire the accredited guide, no shortcuts

Basey requires a local cave guide and lighting man inside — it is mandatory and checked at entrance. In Calbiga, the Trexplore cave master guide plus local guide from Brgy. Panayuran are mandatory. This funds community and conservation.

### Pack for wet and dark

Both caves involve wading. Use aqua shoes or trek shoes that drain, not hiking boots. Dry bag for phone, power bank, camera. Quick-dry shirt and pants, not cotton. For Calbiga overnight, bring hamak, sleeping mat, extra headlamp batteries — there is no electricity past Calbiga town.

### Bring cash and leave early

No ATMs in Basey jump-off or Panayuran. No card payments. Bring PHP 3,000 to PHP 5,000 cash for Sohoton group expenses, PHP 5,000 to PHP 8,000 for Calbiga day tour personal expenses. Start before 8 AM to avoid midday heat on habal-habal and river.

### Respect cave rules

Sohoton caves are Stone to Iron Age archaeological sites. No writing on walls, no collecting. Calbiga is protected under RA 9072 Cave Act — any damage is punishable. Follow designated trail, no flash photography on bat colonies when guide says no, and carry out trash.

## So, Sohoton or Calbiga — or Both?

If you have one day from Tacloban, do Sohoton Natural Bridge and Cave — it is affordable, organized, and you can be back in Tacloban for dinner after kayaking through the arch. Budget PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,500 per person all-in sharing, plus transport from Cebu.

If you have two to three days and want a real expedition, go for Calbiga's Langun-Gobingob with Trexplore. Fly Cebu to Tacloban or Calbayog, van to Calbiga, and camp inside one of the biggest cave chambers in Southeast Asia. Budget PHP 3,500 for day tour, PHP 8,000 to PHP 12,000 for 3D/2N.

Combined itinerary works: Day 1 Cebu to Tacloban, van to Basey for Sohoton PM tour, overnight Basey or Tacloban. Day 2 van to Calbiga, start Langun-Gobingob discovery tour, overnight camping or back to Catbalogan. Best months November to May for river levels and dry cave floors.

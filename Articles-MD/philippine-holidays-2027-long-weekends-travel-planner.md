# Philippine Holidays 2027 and Long Weekends: A Travel Planner

January is the best time to map out your entire travel year, and 2027 gives you a clean, predictable calendar to do it. Malacañang has already released the official list through Proclamation No. 1427, signed September 8, 2026, and it follows the familiar structure: 10 regular holidays, 4 special non-working days, 4 additional special non-working days, 1 special working day, plus Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha to be announced later.

If you are trying to stretch weekends, book cheaper flights, or avoid the holiday rush in Cebu, Manila, and popular island routes, here is the practical breakdown of official dates, real long weekends, and what to do with each one.

## Official Philippine holidays 2027

These dates come directly from Proclamation No. 1427. Eid dates will be declared separately by the National Commission on Muslim Filipinos based on moon sighting.

### Regular holidays

* **January 1, Friday:** New Year's Day
* **March 25, Thursday:** Maundy Thursday
* **March 26, Friday:** Good Friday
* **April 9, Friday:** Araw ng Kagitingan (Day of Valor)
* **May 1, Saturday:** Labor Day
* **June 12, Saturday:** Independence Day
* **August 30, Monday:** National Heroes Day (last Monday of August)
* **November 30, Tuesday:** Bonifacio Day
* **December 25, Saturday:** Christmas Day
* **December 30, Thursday:** Rizal Day

### Special non-working days

* **August 21, Saturday:** Ninoy Aquino Day
* **November 1, Monday:** All Saints' Day
* **December 8, Wednesday:** Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary
* **December 31, Friday:** Last Day of the Year

### Additional special non-working days

* **February 6, Saturday:** Chinese New Year
* **March 27, Saturday:** Black Saturday
* **November 2, Tuesday:** All Souls' Day
* **December 24, Friday:** Christmas Eve

### Special working day

* **February 25, Thursday:** EDSA People Power Revolution Anniversary

Proclamation 1427 notes that proclamations for Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha will follow the Islamic calendar. For travel planning, expect both to create at least one extra long weekend once confirmed, usually with a day off near March and May based on lunar calculations.

## 2027 long weekends at a glance

Not every holiday creates a long weekend, especially those that fall on Saturday. Here are the ones that actually count if you work Monday to Friday:

**1. January 1 to 3: New Year weekend.** January 1 is Friday. This is your first 3-day window. Good for a reset trip rather than a big flight, since many resorts are still on peak rates until January 5.

**2. March 25 to 28: Holy Week super long weekend.** Maundy Thursday to Black Saturday runs Thursday to Saturday, plus Easter Sunday on March 28. This is the longest uninterrupted break in the first half of the year. No need to file leave for four straight days.

**3. April 9 to 11: Day of Valor.** Friday to Sunday. April is dry season in most of the country, ideal for beach crossings.

**4. August 28 to 30: National Heroes Day.** Saturday to Monday. End of habagat season in many areas, but usually manageable for city breaks and mountain towns.

**5. October 30 to November 2: Undas quadruple weekend.** This is the big one for the second semester. November 1 is Monday and November 2 is Tuesday. If you take leave on Monday November 29? Wait, that's different. For Undas, if you take no leave, you get Saturday October 30 to Tuesday November 2 because of the Monday and Tuesday holidays. That is four days using zero leave, or five if you include Wednesday.

**6. November 27 to 30: Bonifacio Day stretch.** Bonifacio Day is Tuesday November 30. File leave on Monday November 29 and you get Saturday to Tuesday, four days.

**7. December 24 to 26: Christmas weekend.** Christmas Eve is Friday and Christmas Day is Saturday. Many offices go on skeleton until December 27, so even without leaves you get Friday to Sunday, with more flexibility if your company declares December 27 as off.

**8. December 30 to January 2, 2028: Year-end finale.** Rizal Day Thursday December 30 and Last Day of the Year Friday December 31 create a Thursday-to-Sunday block, extending into New Year. This is the most expensive window for flights and ferries, so book early.

Tricky Saturdays: Labor Day May 1, Independence Day June 12, Chinese New Year February 6, Ninoy Aquino Day August 21, and Christmas Day December 25 all fall on Saturdays in 2027. They do not automatically extend your weekend, but some private companies give a Monday off in lieu, and LGUs often declare local activities. Confirm with HR before planning around them.

## Travel strategy per long weekend

### January 1 to 3: Start close, travel light

Airports are chaotic from December 28 to 30. Instead of flying out on the 1st, use this weekend for places reachable by bus or short ferry from Cebu. Think Carcar heritage houses, Aloguinsan's Bojo River, or a stayed-in day trip to the highlands of Busay to avoid traffic. Hotels in Cebu City drop rates by January 2. If you are in Negros Oriental, Dauin to Zamboanguita coastal drives are quiet that weekend.

### March 25 to 28: Book Holy Week now, not in March

This four-day break is sacred for family travel, and transport sells out first. Visita Iglesia routes in Cebu City, Siquijor's Lenten traditions, and Bohol's countryside are popular and often fully booked for buses. If you want beaches, go farther: Bantayan and Camotes see peak crowds, while south Cebu towns like Moalboal and Alegria spread people out better. Reserve ferries at least two weeks ahead, and expect liquor bans in some municipalities on Maundy Thursday and Good Friday.

### April 9 to 11: Dry season sweet spot

April remains dry in Visayas and Palawan, with calmer seas. Since it is only three days, avoid itineraries with two flights. A good formula: one base, two activities. For example, base in Dumaguete, day trip to Apo Island on Saturday, then Casaroro Falls on Sunday. From Cebu, Malapascua is doable for a full Saturday if you take the first Maya bus at 4 AM. April 9 traffic is lighter than Holy Week, so mountain roads to Osmeña Peak are more pleasant.

### August 28 to 30: Rainy season city breaks

Late August is not ideal for long boat rides, but cities and higher elevations work. Consider a food-focused weekend in Dumaguete, Bacolod's early MassKara build-up events, or a heritage walk in Silay. In Cebu, museums and indoor food markets are comfortable, and hotels run low-season promos. If you chase waterfalls, Aguinid in Samboan and Pulangbato Falls in Valencia are stronger in rainy months, but bring grip shoes and leave early to avoid late-afternoon downpours.

### October 30 to November 2: Undas, plan for cemeteries and traffic

November 1 and 2 will crowd terminals and cemeteries. Many families travel October 29. If you are not observing Undas in your hometown, this is a poor weekend for Manila flights and a great weekend for off-radar nature spots that do not rely on inter-island ferries. Book accommodations near but not inside major memorial parks. For Cebu-based travelers, the north such as Bogo and Daanbantayan remains less busy than Moalboal during Undas.

### November 27 to 30: Use one leave, gain four days

File Monday November 29. This is one of the most efficient leave uses in 2027. It falls at the tail end of amihan transition, so Siargao, Baler, and eastern Negros start getting surf-friendly winds, while west-facing beaches in Cebu stay calmer. Travel demand is lower than December, so flight sales often appear for this exact weekend. It is also a good window for budget trips because December prices have not kicked in.

### December 24 to 31: Two weekends, one strategy

The Christmas and year-end breaks are actually two separate long weekends divided by only three working days, December 27 to 29. Many offices run half-days. If you can file leave for December 27, 28, and 29, you turn December 24 to January 2 into a 10-day holiday using only three leaves. That covers Christmas with family and a short escape after. Ferry and flight prices peak, so set price alerts by October and consider bus routes as backup. For Cebu to Negros routes, overnight ferries have more availability than morning fast crafts.

## Budget and booking tips that work every year

* **Confirm local declarations.** LGUs sometimes add work suspensions for festivals such as Sinulog in Cebu (January) or Buglasan in Negros Oriental (October). Those are not part of Proclamation 1427, but they affect traffic more than national holidays.
* **Reverse the flow.** Travel out on the holiday itself, not the day before. October 30 will be busier than November 1 for outbound Manila traffic. December 24 morning flights from Cebu to Dumaguete or Bacolod are emptier than December 23.
* **Save two leave days for Eid announcements.** Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha are often declared close to a weekend once the NCMF confirms dates. Keeping a flexible leave balance lets you extend those weekends without rebooking.
* **Book refundable where you can for March.** Holy Week weather is generally dry, but sea conditions can still change. Accommodations with free cancellation until March 20 give you flexibility if the coast guard restricts small boats.

## Quick planner checklist

* January 3: Decide if you need that November 29 leave, file early.
* February 6: Chinese New Year falls on Saturday, expect Binondo and Cebu's old downtown areas to be crowded for food crawls, but no extra day off.
* March 1 to 10: Finalize Holy Week transport and accommodations.
* August 1: Start checking December flights, especially Cebu to Clark and Cebu to Siargao for the year-end window.
* October 15: Secure Undas accommodations, avoid last-minute cemetery-area bookings.
* November 1: Watch for official Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha proclamations, adjust November and December leaves if needed.

2027 is not a year packed with mid-week holidays that automatically become super long weekends, but it rewards people who file leaves strategically: one leave for November 30, three leaves for December 24 to January 2, and zero leaves for the March and October-November blocks. Map your trips around those anchors, keep the Saturday holidays as optional perks rather than certainties, and you will get eight usable long weekends without burning your entire leave balance.

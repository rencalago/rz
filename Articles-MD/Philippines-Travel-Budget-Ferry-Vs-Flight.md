# Philippines Travel Budget: Daily Costs, and Ferry vs Domestic Flight

The Philippines looks cheap on paper, but island geography makes transport the biggest variable. A daily budget can be as low as PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,400 for a backpacker staying in dorms and eating carinderia food, or PHP 5,000 to PHP 8,000 for a mid-range traveler in private rooms and private island-hopping boats. The jump between those levels is not food — it is flights and boats.

You have two main ways to move between major islands: domestic flights or ferries. Flights save a day. Ferries save money and let you bring luggage without strict weight limits. Knowing when to fly and when to ferry is how you control both cost and time.

Here is a realistic daily cost breakdown right now and a direct ferry vs flight comparison for the routes most travelers use from Cebu and Manila.

## 1. Daily Costs — What You Actually Spend Per Day

**Shoestring / Backpacker — PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,850 per day (USD 25–50):**
- Dorm bed or basic fan room: PHP 500–1,000
- Food: Pan de sal and coffee breakfast, carinderia lunch PHP 80–150, street BBQ dinner PHP 150–250, snacks and water PHP 100
- Transport: Jeepney PHP 15–25, tricycle PHP 50–150, bus between towns PHP 100–300
- Activities: One shared island-hopping tour every 2–3 days at PHP 800–1,500, split over days
- This is classic backpacking — dorms, local food, jeepneys, occasional paid activity.

**Mid-Range — PHP 3,500 to PHP 6,000 per day (USD 60–110):**
- Private aircon room with bathroom: PHP 1,500–3,500
- Food: Café breakfast PHP 200–350, restaurant lunch PHP 250–400, restaurant dinner PHP 300–500
- Transport: Grab or private van PHP 300–700 per ride, private island-hopping boat PHP 3,000–6,000 per group per day (PHP 1,000–2,000 per person if shared among 3)
- Activities: Diving PHP 1,800–2,500 per dive, guided tours PHP 1,200–2,000

**Family / Comfort — PHP 6,000 to PHP 10,000+ per day:**
- Family room or two rooms: PHP 3,500–7,000
- Food: Higher-end restaurants, western options, more bottled water
- Transport: Private vans, airport transfers, private boats for safety and schedule control
- Activities: Private tours, resort-based island hopping, kids' gear

**What pushes costs up:** Domestic flights, especially last-minute or during Holy Week and Christmas. Batanes and Siargao flights are often pricier than Cebu–Manila. Environmental fees, terminal fees, and boat tickets add PHP 200–500 per transfer day.

**What keeps costs down:** Staying longer in one island (weekly discounts on guesthouses), eating at carinderias, taking shared vans, booking flights 4–6 weeks ahead, and avoiding peak holidays.

## 2. Ferry vs Domestic Flight — Direct Comparison

**Flights — Fastest, Higher Base Fare:**
- **Manila to Cebu:** 1h20m–1h35m, Cebu Pacific/PAL/AirAsia hourly, promo PHP 1,200–2,000, regular PHP 2,500–5,000 one-way. Add baggage PHP 500–1,200 for 20 kg, seat selection extra, fuel surcharge Level 17 currently PHP 559–1,635 domestic depending on distance.
- **Cebu to Bohol (Tagbilaran):** 25–30 mins, PHP 1,000–2,500.
- **Cebu to El Nido (Lio):** 1h–1h15m direct, PHP 2,500–6,000.
- **Cebu to Siargao (IAO):** 55m–1h05m direct, PHP 1,500–5,500.
- **Cebu to Caticlan (Boracay gateway):** 1h–1h15m, PHP 1,200–4,500.
- **Pros:** Saves a full day, less seasickness risk, more frequent.
- **Cons:** Strict baggage limits (7 kg hand carry, 20 kg check-in typical), extra fees for sports equipment, weather cancellations still happen, and you need to be at airport 2–3 hours early.

**Ferries — Cheapest, Overnight, Carry More:**
- **Manila to Cebu (2GO):** 21–23 hours overnight, PHP 2,859–8,615 depending on class — Mega Value (open bunk area) cheapest, Tourist cabin mid, Private suite most expensive. Leaves Manila or Cebu evening, arrives next morning. Meals included in some classes.
- **Cebu to Bohol:** Fast craft 2 hours, PHP 800–1,200 one-way.
- **Cebu to Surigao (for Siargao):** 8.5–10 hours overnight, economy PHP 800–1,200, cabin PHP 1,500–2,500, plus Surigao to Dapa fast craft PHP 400–800, 2–3.5 hours.
- **Cebu to Iloilo:** 11–13 hours overnight, PHP 800–2,500 depending on class.
- **Coron to El Nido ferry:** 4–5 hours, PHP 1,800–3,500.
- **Pros:** No baggage weight stress (you can bring large backpacks, even motorbikes for extra fee), you save a night's accommodation, scenic, and sometimes cheaper than flying when baggage fees added.
- **Cons:** Takes 10–23 hours, seasickness common, schedules less frequent, and trips can be cancelled by Coast Guard during gale warnings June–October and Amihan surges December–February.

**Quick math for common routes:**
- **Cebu to Manila:** Flight PHP 1,200–5,000 + PHP 300–600 to city vs Ferry PHP 2,859–8,615 with bed and meals. Flight wins on time, ferry wins if you want to avoid airport hassle and bring lots of luggage.
- **Cebu to Bohol:** Flight PHP 1,000–2,500 but airport transfers both ends add 2 hours — ferry PHP 800–1,200 fast craft is often faster door-to-door.
- **Cebu to Siargao:** Flight direct 1 hour PHP 1,500–5,500 vs Ferry total PHP 1,200–2,000 + 11–13 hours. Flight wins unless budget is very tight.
- **Manila to Palawan (Puerto Princesa):** Flight PHP 1,500–5,000, 1h35m vs No direct ferry — must fly or go via Coron ferry chain.

## 3. When to Fly vs When to Ferry

**Fly when:**
- You have less than 7 days and want 2+ islands.
- You travel December to May and want reliable timing.
- You have limited baggage and booked early promo fares.
- You go to Batanes, El Nido Lio Airport, or any small airport — no ferry alternative.

**Ferry when:**
- You have time and want to save on accommodation for a night.
- You carry large luggage, surfboard, or motorbike.
- You travel Cebu–Bohol, Cebu–Iloilo, Manila–Cebu, or El Nido–Coron — ferries are competitive.
- You want a slower, scenic experience and are not prone to severe seasickness.

**Mixed strategy that works:** Fly into one hub (Cebu or Manila), ferry between nearby islands (Cebu–Bohol–Siquijor–Dumaguete chain, or El Nido–Coron), then fly out from the last island. This balances cost and time — you avoid backtracking.

## 4. Money-Saving Tips That Actually Work

**Book flights 4–6 weeks ahead:** Promo fares appear then. Avoid Holy Week (March/April) and Christmas to New Year — prices double. Use Google Flights price tracking.

**Check total flight cost:** Add baggage (PHP 500–1,200), seat (PHP 150–400), and fuel surcharge (PHP 559–1,635) to base fare. A PHP 1,200 promo can become PHP 2,500 with bags.

**For ferries, book Tourist or Cabin class:** Mega Value open bunks are cheapest but noisy and less secure. Tourist cabin with bunk bed is worth PHP 500–1,000 extra for overnight.

**Withdraw cash before islands:** ATMs on Malapascua, Siargao, Balicasag have PHP 250 fee and 10k limit, and run out on weekends. Withdraw in Cebu City or Manila.

**Use GCash and Maya where possible:** Many mid-range hotels and tour desks accept GCash, reducing ATM trips, but small boats and tricycles are cash-only.

**Travel insurance with weather cover:** Especially for Batanes, Siargao, and ferry routes — cancellations due to gale warnings are common June–October. Insurance saves rebooking costs.

## Tips Before You Book

### Compare door-to-door, not just ticket price

A PHP 1,200 flight plus PHP 600 airport transfer plus PHP 500 baggage plus 3-hour airport wait may be slower and pricier than a PHP 1,200 fast ferry that leaves city pier.

### Take seasickness meds early

Take 30 minutes before ferry departure. Sit mid-ship, lower deck, eyes on horizon. Avoid heavy oily meals before overnight ferries.

### Bring small bills for fees

Environmental fees, terminal fees, and tricycle fares are cash-only. PHP 20, 50, 100 bills are most useful.

## So, Ferry or Flight for Philippines Travel?

If you are short on time and have light bags, fly — especially for long hops like Manila–Cebu, Cebu–Siargao, Cebu–Caticlan. If you are on a budget, have big luggage, or travel Cebu–Bohol or El Nido–Coron, ferry saves money and a night's stay.

For a 10-day trip from Cebu, a common budget mix is: ferry Cebu to Bohol (2 hrs, PHP 800–1,200), ferry Bohol to Siquijor, fly Siquijor to Cebu or Manila, then fly to El Nido or Coron. You keep daily costs at PHP 2,500–4,500 per person mid-range, with flights only for the longest legs.

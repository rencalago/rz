# Iloilo and Guimaras Food Trip

Iloilo City was named a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy, and it shows in everyday meals. The city keeps its signature soups and grilled chicken within a few kilometers of each other, while Guimaras, just a 15-minute boat ride away, adds a completely different flavor with its sweet mangoes.

This food trip is best done as a two-part day or overnight: base yourself in Iloilo City for batchoy, pancit molo, and inasal, then cross to Guimaras for mango pizza, mango bulalo, and island seafood. You do not need a car — jeepneys, Grab, and small boats connect everything.

Here is what to eat, where to find it, how to get to Iloilo from Cebu, and how to cross to Guimaras without hassle.

## 1. La Paz Batchoy — Iloilo's Most Famous Noodle Soup

La Paz Batchoy originated in the La Paz district of Iloilo City. It is a rich pork broth with miki noodles, pork slices, liver, chicharon crumbs, and sometimes bone marrow and egg. The flavor is deep and slightly savory-sweet from the stock, topped with crushed chicharon for texture.

**Where to try:** Netong's Original La Paz Batchoy in La Paz Market is the most referenced. Ted's Old Timer La Paz Batchoy has branches in Atria and SM City Iloilo. Small stalls inside La Paz Public Market serve their own versions from early morning.

**Best for:** Breakfast or late-night soup after a walk around La Paz.

**What to do:** Order a small bowl first — servings are filling. Ask if marrow is available; it is often an add-on. Pair with puto manapla, a local rice cake.

**Practical tip:** Go early. The best batchoy shops open around 7 AM and broth is freshest in the morning. Expect PHP 90 to PHP 180 per bowl depending on toppings.

## 2. Pancit Molo — Dumpling Soup from Molo District

Pancit Molo is not pancit at all. It is a clear chicken broth with wonton wrappers stuffed with ground pork, chicken, and shrimp. It is associated with Molo district and often served during family gatherings.

**Where to try:** Local eateries around Molo Plaza, and 1872 Panaderia de Molo's Molo Mansion branch which pairs it with heirloom biscuits. Many Iloilo restaurants list it as a starter, but the Molo area versions stay closest to the home-style recipe.

**Best for:** A light, comforting soup between heavier meals.

**What to do:** Eat it hot — the wrappers soften quickly. Add a dash of calamansi if offered, but avoid heavy chili sauce that masks the broth.

**Practical tip:** Pancit Molo travels well as pasalubong frozen, but fresh bowls in Molo are better.

## 3. Chicken Inasal and Seafood — Iloilo's Grill Culture

Chicken inasal — chicken marinated in calamansi, vinegar, garlic, lemongrass, and annatto, then grilled over charcoal — is an Ilonggo staple. The best pieces are the thigh (paa) and breast-wing (pecho) served with chicken oil and soy-calamansi dip.

Iloilo also leans on seafood. Baked oysters (talaba), diwal (angel wing clams) in season, and fresh fish from the market are common. KBL — Kadyos, Baboy, Langka — is a sour soup made with pigeon peas, pork, and jackfruit, found in traditional restaurants.

**Where to try:** Villa Arevalo and Atria area for inasal clusters, Breakthrough or Tatoy's in Villa for seafood. For KBL, look for Ilonggo heritage restaurants in City Proper.

**Best for:** Lunch and dinner, especially for groups who want to share several dishes.

**What to do:** Order inasal with unlimited rice if you are hungry, and add a side of KBL to balance the smokiness with sourness.

## 4. Sweets, Coffee, and Pasalubong — Biscocho and Tablea

Iloilo's sweets are tied to old bakeries. Biscocho Haus sells biscocho — twice-baked bread with butter and sugar — and butterscotch. Deocampo Barquillos makes thin, crisp barquillos in Iloilo City proper. In Jaro, Agatona 1927 Museum Cafe serves thick tablea chocolate with heirloom cookies and a view of Jaro Cathedral.

**Best for:** Afternoon snacks and take-home pasalubong.

**What to do:** Buy biscocho in small packs first to test sweetness before buying large boxes. Tablea is best paired with puto or simple biscuits.

## 5. Guimaras Mango Pizza and Mango Twists — Crossing from Iloilo

Guimaras is famous for its mangoes, which are known for being sweet even when not fully ripe. The island's signature dish is mango pizza.

**How to get to Guimaras from Iloilo:** Take a tricycle or Grab to Ortiz Wharf (also called Parola Wharf) in City Proper, Iloilo City. Pump boats to Jordan Port, Guimaras run from around 5:30 AM to 6:00 PM every 15 to 30 minutes. Travel time 15 to 20 minutes. Fare PHP 80 to PHP 150 per person plus small terminal fee PHP 20 to PHP 30. From Jordan Port, tricycles and multicabs wait for island tours.

**Where to eat mango pizza:** The Pitstop Restaurant in Barangay San Miguel, Jordan, is the pioneer. It uses mango sauce instead of tomato sauce, topped with mango strips, cheese, and cashew nuts. A whole pizza starts around PHP 300 to PHP 500 depending on size. The same menu offers mango beef bulalo, mango adobo, mango spaghetti, mango sisig, and mango shake. Another branch in Buenavista is often cited.

Other Guimaras food stops: Trappist Monastery in Jordan for mango cookies, mango bars, and mango jam; local resorts for grilled seafood with mango salsa; and Alubijod Cove area for fresh buko.

**Best for:** A half-day food extension from Iloilo. The island itself is worth a full day if you add island hopping to Ave Maria Islet and windmills in San Lorenzo.

**What to do:** Eat the mango pizza while hot — the cheese and mango sauce separate when cold. If you do a tricycle island tour, fix the price before leaving Jordan Port — PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,800 for a half-day covering Pitstop, Trappist, and a beach.

## Getting to Iloilo from Cebu

**Flights:** Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, and AirAsia fly Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB) to Iloilo International Airport (ILO) daily. Flight time about 1 hour. One-way fares promo PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,000, regular PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,500. Iloilo Airport is in Cabatuan, about 30 to 45 minutes by van to City Proper — shared van PHP 100 to PHP 150 per person, Grab/taxi PHP 400 to PHP 700.

**Ferry:** Cokaliong Shipping Lines and Trans-Asia run overnight ferries Cebu Pier 1 to Iloilo City. Departure around 7 PM to 9 PM, arrival 7 AM to 8 AM next day, 11 to 13 hours. Fares economy PHP 800 to PHP 1,200, cabin PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500. Useful if you want to bring a vehicle or save on a night's accommodation.

**Total trip time Cebu to Guimaras:** If you fly Cebu to Iloilo, you can be eating mango pizza in Guimaras the same day — fly in the morning, boat to Jordan early afternoon. If you ferry overnight from Cebu, arrive Iloilo morning, then cross to Guimaras before lunch.

## How to Plan the Food Trip

**One-day Iloilo food crawl:** Start with batchoy in La Paz for breakfast (7:30 AM), jeepney or Grab to Molo for pancit molo (10:30 AM), coffee and tablea in Jaro (2 PM), inasal and seafood in Villa or Atria for dinner (6 PM). This covers 4 districts in one day without rushing.

**Overnight with Guimaras:** Day 1 as above, stay in Iloilo City Proper. Day 2: early boat from Ortiz to Jordan (6:30 AM), tricycle tour to Pitstop for mango pizza lunch, Trappist Monastery for pasalubong, quick swim at Alubijod or Raymen Beach, boat back to Iloilo by 4 PM, fly or ferry back to Cebu next morning.

**Budget guide per person excluding Cebu flights:** Batchoy PHP 100 to PHP 180, pancit molo PHP 120 to PHP 200, inasal with rice PHP 150 to PHP 250, KBL PHP 180 to PHP 300, mango pizza PHP 300 to PHP 500 shared, Guimaras boat roundtrip PHP 200 to PHP 300, tricycle tour half-day PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,800 split among 2 to 4.

**Practical tips:** Bring cash — small eateries and Guimaras tricycles are cash-only. Dress light — Iloilo is hot and humid. If you have a sensitive stomach, stick to cooked soups and grilled items first, and drink bottled water.

## So, Iloilo or Guimaras First?

Start in Iloilo City for the classic soups and grilled dishes, then cross to Guimaras for the sweet twist. The contrast is the point — savory, marrow-rich batchoy and sour KBL in Iloilo, then sweet-savory mango pizza and mango bulalo in Guimaras, just 15 minutes apart by boat.

If you only have one day from Cebu, fly early to Iloilo, do batchoy and pancit molo, cross to Guimaras for mango pizza, and return to Iloilo for inasal dinner. If you have two days, stay overnight in Iloilo and give Guimaras a full half-day without rushing the boat schedule.

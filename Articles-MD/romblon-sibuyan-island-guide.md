# Romblon and Sibuyan Island Guide: Marble Capital, Guiting-Guiting and Cresta de Gallo

Romblon Province is three main islands that feel completely different from each other. Romblon Island itself is the marble capital of the Philippines — small town, Spanish forts, and workshops where artisans carve everything from tiny horses to full-size saints. Sibuyan Island is the wild one — dense forest, rivers you can drink from, and Mount Guiting-Guiting, one of the hardest climbs in the country at 2,058 MASL. Between them sits Cresta de Gallo, a tiny uninhabited island with a long white sandbar that looks edited.

If you are coming from Cebu, there is no direct ferry to Romblon anymore. You go via Manila and Batangas Port, or you fly to Tablas Island and connect by van and boat. It takes longer than Boracay or Palawan, which is exactly why it is still quiet.

Here is a practical breakdown of the three highlights, how to get there from Cebu, what things actually cost right now, when to go, and where to base yourself.

## 1. Romblon Island — The Marble Capital

Romblon town is the provincial capital, set around a sheltered harbor between Fort San Andres and Fort Santiago. The Spanish built those forts in 1640 and 1579 as lookouts against Moro raids. The main town is walkable, with the 17th-century St. Joseph Cathedral, a small public market, and a coastline of workshops selling marble.

**What to do:**
- Visit the marble quarries in Barangay Mapayapa, northeast of town. Entry is free, you are watching actual cutting work. Ask permission from the workers. Blocks come in white, grey, beige, and the rarer black and green.
- Stop at marble factories along the road — you can see carvers using hand tools for small mortars, tiles, nameplates, ashtrays, animal figurines. A small marble mortar and pestle costs PHP 250 to PHP 600, a 12x18 inch tile around PHP 800 to PHP 1,200, sculptures higher.
- Walk up to Fort San Andres for sunset. No entrance fee, 10-minute uphill walk. There is also Bonbon Beach, connected to mainland at low tide by a long sandbar, and Tiamban Beach, better for swimming.
- If you dive, Romblon is known as a macro capital — similar critters to Anilao. Dive shops in town charge PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,500 per dive with gear.

**Best for:** Travelers who like small-town culture, souvenir shopping that supports artisans, and quiet beaches without bars.

**Costs on Romblon Island:** Tricycle around town PHP 20 to PHP 50 per person. Motorbike rental PHP 500 to PHP 700 per day. Tour to Bonbon + Tiamban + marble shops with tricycle PHP 600 to PHP 1,000 for half-day.

## 2. Sibuyan Island — Mount Guiting-Guiting

Sibuyan is often called the Galapagos of Asia because a third of its forest is primary growth and many species are endemic. The centerpiece is Mount Guiting-Guiting (G2), famous for its jagged ridge, knife-edge rocks called Kiss the Wall and 90 Degrees, and rapid weather changes.

**What you need to know:**
- Jump-off is Barangay Tampayan, Magdiwang municipality. You must register at the DENR office in Magdiwang before the climb.
- Guide is mandatory. Standard regulation is 1 guide per 5 climbers. Current rates: Guide fee PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,000 per guide, porter PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,000 (same ratio). For a Traverse (Tampayan-Olango) you usually need 2 to 3 days, so guides stay with you.
- DENR registration and permits: Environmental/registration fee PHP 500 per person for Filipino hikers, PHP 1,000 for foreigners, plus barangay tourism fee PHP 100 to PHP 200. Port fees PHP 30 to PHP 50. Climb certificate around PHP 50 to PHP 100.
- Difficulty: 9/9 for most rating groups. Not for first-time hikers. You need to rappel and scramble on exposed rock. Bring your own tent, cook set, 3 liters water per day, gloves, and proper hiking shoes with grip.
- DENR closure: Mountain is usually closed for rehabilitation from November to January (sometimes extended to February). Best months to climb are March to May dry season, with February to October as open window depending on yearly advisory. Check DENR Sibuyan page before booking transport.

**Best for:** Experienced mountaineers training for big peaks, or hikers who have done Pulag, Halcon, or Apo and want the next level.

**What to do if you are not climbing:** Sibuyan has Cawa-cawa Falls, Cantingas River (one of the cleanest rivers), Lambingan Falls jump-off, Cresta de Gallo access via San Fernando town, and river swimming in Magdiwang. Most falls have no entrance fee or PHP 20 to PHP 50.

## 3. Cresta de Gallo — The Sandbar Island

Cresta de Gallo is a tiny islet off San Fernando, Romblon, about 1 to 2 hours by small outrigger from Barangay Azagra Port. There are no resorts, no electricity, only a caretaker hut and coconut trees. The draw is a long white sandbar on one side and snorkeling on the other.

**How to get there:** From Tablas Airport or Odiongan Port, go to San Fernando town on southern Tablas (2 to 2.5 hours by van, PHP 300 to PHP 500), then tricycle to Azagra Port (PHP 50 to PHP 100, 10 minutes), then boat.

**Current boat and fees:**
- Boat rental from Azagra to Cresta de Gallo: PHP 2,500 to PHP 3,500 per boat good for 4 to 6 pax, day trip. Overnight slightly higher PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,500 because boat stays. Travel time 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on sea condition.
- Entrance/environmental fee: PHP 20 to PHP 100 per person (varies by barangay contact, day vs overnight).
- Bring food and water. There is a small grilling fee PHP 100 if you cook. Overnight camping allowed — bring tent, PHP 100 to PHP 200 camping fee in some reports. Best to arrange via local contact (homestay in Azagra can connect you).

**Best for:** Day trip after a Sibuyan climb or a quiet beach day when you want total isolation.

**What to do:** Swim the sandbar at low tide, snorkel the reef edge, walk the entire island in 20 minutes. No trash bins — bring trash back to Azagra.

## 4. How to Get There from Cebu — Two Practical Routes

### Route A: Cebu → Manila → Batangas → Ferry (Most Common)

This is how most travelers from Visayas and Mindanao go because there is no Cebu-Romblon direct sea link now.

1. **Fly Cebu to Manila:** Daily flights CEB-MNL by Cebu Pacific, PAL, AirAsia. 1 hr 30 mins. Promo one-way PHP 1,200 to PHP 2,000, regular PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,500. Or take 2GO ferry Cebu-Manila (20 hours, PHP 1,200 to PHP 3,000) if you like slow travel.
2. **Manila to Batangas Port:** From NAIA, taxi/Grab to Buendia or PITX Bus Terminal (45 mins to 1.5 hours depending on traffic) then bus to Batangas Pier: Ceres, Jam, DLTBCo buses run every 30 mins, 2 to 3 hours, PHP 250 to PHP 350. Bus leaves from Buendia, Cubao, or PITX.
3. **Batangas to Romblon/Sibuyan:** Starlite Ferries is the workhorse. Daily departures around 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM from Batangas Port to Romblon, Romblon (ETA 1:00 AM to 2:00 AM next day, ~10 hours) then continues to Magdiwang Port, Sibuyan (ETA 4:00 AM to 6:00 AM). Also Montenegro Lines has a Thursday or mixed schedule departure 5:00 PM arriving next morning. 
- Fares: Tourist/reclining PHP 850 to PHP 1,000, economy bed PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,300, cabin for 4 PHP 2,500 to PHP 3,500. Terminal fee at Batangas PHP 30 to PHP 100.
- Book at Starlite website or 12Go, and at the port counter day-of. During peak (Holy Week, December) tickets sell out.
- To get to Tablas Island (Odiongan), Montenegro runs RoRo Batangas-Odiongan daily ~9 to 10 hours, PHP 816 to PHP 1,632 depending on class.

### Route B: Fly to Tablas (Tugdan Airport) — Faster but Pricier

Tugdan Airport (TBH) in Alcantara, Tablas is the provincial air gateway.

- **Flights:** Cebu Pacific flies Manila (MNL) to Tablas (TBH) daily ~1 hour. PAL previously reopened MNL-TBH. Sunlight Air flies Clark (CRK) to Tablas every Tuesday and Saturday from August 2024/2025 season with base promo PHP 1,899 one-way. No direct Cebu to Tablas flight, so you connect in Manila or Clark.
- **Cebu to Manila to Tablas timing:** If you land NAIA before lunch, you can catch an afternoon MNL-TBH flight same day. Total flying day ~5 to 7 hours with layover.
- **Tablas to Romblon/Sibuyan:** From Tugdan Airport, van/jeep to Odiongan Port (1 hour, PHP 100 to PHP 150), then ferry or fastcraft to Romblon Island (RORO 3 to 4 hours, PHP 500 to PHP 800; Montenegro or Santa Fe Shipping) or to Sibuyan (Magdiwang via Odiongan or direct RoRo). There are also daily small passenger boats Romblon-Romblon to Sibuyan.

**Which route to choose:** If you value time and can book early, fly to Tablas. If you value cost and can sleep on a ferry, do Batangas night ferry from Manila — you save a hotel night and wake up in Romblon.

## 5. Best Months, Weather and Costs

**Best months overall:** December to May is dry season. March and April are the driest and sunniest, best for beach hopping and Cresta de Gallo. December to February is cooler and windy — Amihan northeasterly winds bring rough seas between islands, especially crossing Tablas Strait, so expect delays. For Guiting-Guiting climbs, February to May gives the most stable weather; July to September is wet and slippery.

**Habagat vs Amihan:** Habagat (southwest monsoon) runs June to October with heavier rain, but Romblon diving is actually good March to October because plankton brings macro life. If you are island hopping between Romblon, Tablas, and Sibuyan, avoid September to November when rain peaks.

**Budget snapshot per person from Cebu (excluding Manila stopover):**

- Cebu-Manila flight return: PHP 2,500 to PHP 7,000 total if booked 3 to 4 weeks ahead.
- Manila-Batangas bus return: PHP 500 to PHP 700.
- Batangas-Romblon-Sibuyan ferry return: PHP 1,700 to PHP 3,000 for tourist class.
- Or Manila-Tablas flight return: PHP 3,500 to PHP 6,500.
- Tablas internal van/ferry transfers: PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,000 total loop.
- Accommodation: PHP 800 to PHP 1,500 per night basic fan/AC guesthouse in Romblon town or Magdiwang; PHP 1,500 to PHP 3,500 mid-range beachfront like The Three P Resort or Sanctuary Garden Resort; PHP 3,500 to PHP 6,500 boutique.
- Food: PHP 150 to PHP 350 per meal local carinderia, PHP 300 to PHP 600 restaurant. Seafood is fresh and cheaper than Cebu.
- Cresta de Gallo boat shared among 4 pax: PHP 625 to PHP 875 each.
- Guiting-Guiting all-in organized climb (DIY with contacts): PHP 4,500 to PHP 6,500 plus transport; with tour operator including Batangas ferry, guides, food: PHP 6,500 to PHP 9,000.

For a 4-day trip covering Romblon Island + Cresta de Gallo day trip, budget PHP 12,000 to PHP 18,000 from Cebu excluding flights if using ferry, PHP 18,000 to PHP 28,000 if flying to Tablas. Add PHP 3,000 to PHP 4,000 if adding Guiting-Guiting.

## 6. Where to Stay

**On Romblon Island (Romblon town area):** Most first-timers stay near town for marble shops and port access. Options like The Three P Holiday Homes, Lolos Beach Resort near Bonbon, or smaller guesthouses like Haliwood Inn. Book early for Biniray Festival second week of January.

**On Tablas Island:** If flying in, stay overnight in Odiongan or San Agustin before ferry next morning. Looc and San Fernando have homestays close to Cresta de Gallo jump-off.

**On Sibuyan Island:** Magdiwang is best for Guiting-Guiting (near DENR and Cantingas River), San Fernando town for Cresta de Gallo. Sanctuary Garden Resort in San Fernando, Magdiwang homestays PHP 800 to PHP 1,200, some with kitchen. Electricity can be intermittent outside town, bring powerbank.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book ferries in advance for weekends
Starlite Batangas-Romblon at 4 PM daily sells out on Fridays and before long holidays. Buy online 2 to 3 days ahead, then re-confirm at port. If you miss it, sleep in Batangas City — hotels near pier cost PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,800.

### Cash is king
ATMs in Romblon town and Odiongan exist but often offline. Magdiwang has one. Withdraw in Manila or Cebu. Marble shops often prefer cash, some take GCash. Cresta de Gallo has no signal.

### Respect climbing rules on G2
You need a medical certificate for Guiting-Guiting (valid within 6 months in some years). DENR requires you to attend briefing. No solo climbs. Pack out trash, no speaker boxes. The trail has leeches after rain — bring salt and leggings.

### Protect marble purchases
If buying large tiles or sculptures, shops can ship via RoRo to Manila or Cebu. Wrap in clothes or request wooden crate (PHP 300 to PHP 600). Small items fit in hand-carry but check airline weight.

### Get a local SIM and offline map
Globe and Smart have signal in towns but patchy between. Download Maps.me or Google offline map for Romblon Province. Trike drivers know marble shops by owner name, not street.

## So, Is Romblon and Sibuyan Worth the Extra Leg from Cebu?

For a Cebu-based traveler who has done Boracay, Palawan, and Siargao, Romblon is a reset. Romblon Island gives you culture and slow-town life where you can actually talk to craftsmen, Sibuyan gives you raw jungle and one of the toughest summits in the Philippines, and Cresta de Gallo gives you that postcard sandbar without a beach club playing loud music.

Go in March to May if you want the best mix of calm seas and good hiking. Come via Batangas ferry if you want to keep costs down and enjoy the overnight boat experience, or fly to Tablas if you are short on leave. Either way, allow buffer days — sea conditions dictate schedules more than timetables here.

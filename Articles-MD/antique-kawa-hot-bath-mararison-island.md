# Antique Kawa Hot Bath and Mararison Island

Antique is Panay's quiet western side — rivers, rolling hills, and islands that feel a world away from Boracay crowds. Two spots put it on most Cebu-based itineraries: Tibiao for the Kawa hot bath and Bugtong Bato Falls, and Culasi for Mararison Island, locally called Malalison Island.

Tibiao is a river town about 3.5 to 4.5 hours north of Iloilo City. The Kawa bath is its signature: a huge metal caldron (kawa) heated over open fire, filled with warm water and herbs, milk, beer, coffee, or wine, set overlooking the Tibiao River. Thirty minutes away on foot is Bugtong Bato Falls, a multi-tiered waterfall you reach after a short trek through forest and river crossings.

Further north in Culasi is Mararison Island. It's a small island barangay 15 to 20 minutes by outrigger from Culasi port, known for its long white beach on one side, turquoise water, and grassy rolling hills perfect for sunset hikes. You can do both Tibiao and Mararison on one 2-day or 3-day trip from Cebu if you base via Iloilo.

Here are the practical routes from Cebu, current costs, and when to go.

## 1. How to Get to Antique from Cebu via Iloilo

There are two ways from Cebu to Iloilo, then one shared land leg north to Tibiao and Culasi.

**Cebu to Iloilo by air:** Cebu Pacific, Philippine Airlines, and Cebgo fly Mactan-Cebu (CEB) to Iloilo International Airport (ILO) 4 to 5 times daily. Flight time is 50 minutes to 1 hour 10 minutes. Regular one-way fares are PHP 1,200 to PHP 3,500, cheaper on promos. From Iloilo Airport, take a van or taxi to Iloilo City — 30 to 45 minutes, PHP 100 to PHP 150 shared.

**Cebu to Iloilo by ferry:** Cokaliong and Trans-Asia Shipping run overnight ferries from Cebu Pier to Iloilo Fort San Pedro or Lapuz. Travel time 11 to 14 hours, usually departing around 7 PM. Tourist and economy fares start at PHP 1,050 to PHP 2,200. This is a good option if you bring a motorbike or want to save on baggage.

**Iloilo City to Tibiao / Culasi by land:** Go to Molo Terminal or Mohon Terminal in Arevalo/Iloilo City. Look for Ceres buses bound for Culasi, Pandan, or Caticlan that pass Tibiao, or UV Express vans bound for Culasi/Tibiao.

- Bus: PHP 250 to PHP 350 per person. Travel time 4.5 to 6 hours to Tibiao, 5.5 to 7 hours to Culasi depending on stops. Buses leave from early morning, last trip around 5 PM.
- Van (V-hire/UV Express): PHP 200 to PHP 300 per person to Tibiao, PHP 300 to PHP 400 to Culasi. Travel time 3.5 to 4.5 hours to Tibiao, 4 to 5 hours to Culasi. Faster and fewer stops.

Tell the driver you are getting off at Tibiao town proper, Barangay Malabor, or Kayak Inn / Tibiao Fish Spa area for Kawa. For Mararison, stay on until Culasi town proper, then tricycle to Culasi Port or Culasi Tourism Terminal.

**From drop-off to resort:** From the national highway in Tibiao, take a habal-habal (motorcycle) to your Kawa resort — PHP 70 to PHP 100 one-way, 5 to 10 minutes. Most resorts like Tibiao Fish Spa and Kawa Bath, Kayak Inn, and Calawag Mountain Resort are along Tibiao-Barbaza Road beside the river.

**Best for:** Cebu travelers who want a quick Panay loop without flying into Caticlan or Kalibo. Flight + van lets you do Cebu to Tibiao in about 6 to 7 hours total.

**What to do:** If you land in Iloilo late, stay overnight in Iloilo City and take the first Ceres bus at 4 AM to 5 AM. Vans fill fast on weekends — book a seat the day before or arrive before 7 AM.

## 2. Tibiao Kawa Hot Bath — What It Costs and How It Works

The Kawa bath is not a natural hot spring. Staff boil water in a separate vat, pour it into a large kawa over a low wood fire, add leaves, flowers, and your chosen flavor, and keep the fire low enough to keep it warm, not scalding. You sit inside on a wooden plank.

**Fees right now:**
- Basic Kawa hot bath: Starts at PHP 250 to PHP 350 per pax for 30 to 45 minutes, good for 1 to 2 persons per kawa depending on resort. Kayak Inn and Tibiao Fish Spa list PHP 299 to PHP 350 per person.
- Flavored baths: Milk bath PHP 399, beer bath PHP 399, coffee bath PHP 399, wine bath PHP 500 to PHP 599 per kawa. Prices include use of kawa, firewood, and attendant to adjust temperature.
- Tibiao environmental fee: PHP 50 per person collected at some jump-off points or by the resort.
- Fish spa (optional add-on): PHP 119 to PHP 150 for 30-minute fish spa + foot massage.

Most resorts let you stay in the kawa as long as you want within your booked slot as long as no one is waiting. Best taken late afternoon after the falls hike.

**Best for:** Couples, friends, and first-timers wanting the classic Antique photo. Good after Bugtong Bato Falls or whitewater tubing.

**What to do:** Ask for a river-facing kawa. Bring swimwear, quick-dry towel, and slippers. The metal rim gets hot — use the wooden ledge. If you have sensitive skin, start with plain herbal or milk, not coffee or beer.

## 3. Bugtong Bato Falls — Entrance, Guide, and Trek

Bugtong Bato is a 7-tier waterfall northwest of Tibiao town. You usually visit Tier 1 and 2 on a day hike; higher tiers need more time and a stronger guide.

**Fees:**
- Entrance fee: PHP 50 to PHP 100 per person.
- Guide fee: PHP 150 to PHP 250 per group (up to 5 pax). One guide per group is required by the barangay. Cost is often PHP 150 for the first tier, PHP 250 if going to higher tiers.
- Habal-habal from Tibiao Fish Spa area to Brgy. Importante jump-off: PHP 70 to PHP 90 per person one-way, plus another habal to Tuno Barangay Hall if you choose the longer trail.

**Trek details:** From the jump-off at Brgy. Importante or Tuno, it's a 30 to 60-minute walk depending on water level, with several river crossings on bamboo bridges and rocks. Trail can be slippery after rain. Wear trekking sandals or shoes you can get wet, not sneakers.

Water flow is strongest June to November. In summer (March-May), water is clearer and lower, easier to swim, but falls are less dramatic.

**Best for:** Day hikers and families who want a short adventure without overnight camping.

**What to do:** Start before 9 AM to avoid midday heat. Bring PHP 100 to PHP 200 for snacks and water at the sari-sari store near the registration. Leave no trace — the community maintains the trail.

## 4. Mararison Island (Malalison Island) — Boats, Fees, Hiking, and Beaches

Mararison is officially Malalison Island, Barangay Mararison in Culasi. It's an island barangay with about 700 families, a long sandbar on the south, white sand beach on the east, and rolling hills that look like Batanes.

**How to get there:** From Culasi town, tricycle to Culasi Tourism Terminal / Port — PHP 30 to PHP 50. Register at the tourism desk.

**Current island fees:**
- Registration / terminal fee: PHP 10 per person.
- Environmental fee: PHP 20 to PHP 40 per person (usually listed as PHP 30 total for both fees).
- Boat rental roundtrip Culasi Port to Mararison: PHP 750 per boat good for 5 pax (PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,500 for overnight or for 6 to 10 pax). If joining other travelers, PHP 150 to PHP 200 per person roundtrip. Travel time 15 to 20 minutes, can be 25 to 30 minutes with waves.
- Island trekking guide to the rolling hills (Lantawan / Nablag viewpoint): PHP 250 to PHP 300 per group of up to 5 pax. Required for hill entry.
- Boat transfer east to west side of island or boat for snorkeling: PHP 200 per trip.

**Accommodation:** Homestays PHP 300 to PHP 500 per person per night, usually with fan room, mat, and shared bathroom. Some offer cooking or full-board meals PHP 150 to PHP 250 per meal. You can also bring a tent and camp on the designated beach area with barangay permission.

**Beaches and hikes:**
- East side beach (facing Culasi): Calmer for swimming at high tide, lots of boats.
- South sandbar and Nablag Islet: Exposed at low tide, best for photos in late afternoon.
- Rolling hills hike: 30 to 45 minutes uphill to the first viewpoint, another 15 minutes to the highest. Grassy, no shade. Do sunset hike — start at 4 PM to 4:30 PM. Sunrise hike starts around 5 AM from the beach.

**Best for:** Overnight campers, beach lovers who want quiet, and hikers who like open hills over forest.

**What to do:** Bring enough cash — no ATMs on the island. Bring drinking water, reef-safe sunscreen, and power bank. Signal is weak on some parts (Smart usually better than Globe). Check tide chart: low tide makes boat landing harder and beach walk longer.

## 6. Best Months to Visit Antique

Antique faces the northwest monsoon, so seasons matter.

**Best overall — November to May (dry season):** Calmer seas for Mararison boats, clearer water for swimming, and safer river crossings to Bugtong Bato Falls. December to February is coolest, with average temps 24 to 31°C and less humidity. March to May is hottest and sunniest, good for beach photos, but falls have less water.

**Shoulder — Late May to June:** Good balance. Waterfalls start to swell after early rains, sea still mostly calm, fewer visitors than summer.

**Wettest — July to October (Habagat):** Sea can be rough, boats to Mararison and Seco Island get cancelled. Bugtong Bato trek becomes slippery and river crossings deeper. Tibiao River tubing may be suspended after heavy rain. If you go in these months, check weather and allow a buffer day. Typhoon peak is August to September.

For Cebu travelers: Target November to April for the highest chance of calm boat rides. If your priority is Bugtong Bato with strong flow, late November to early July works.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book the van early if you fly in

The last Ceres bus to North Antique leaves Iloilo around 5 PM, last van around 3 PM to 4 PM. If your flight lands after 2 PM, expect to stay in Iloilo City overnight. Molo Terminal and Mohon Terminal are different — confirm which terminal your van to Tibiao/Culasi leaves from.

### Bring cash in small bills

Tibiao and Culasi accept GCash in some resorts, but guides, habal drivers, boats, and environmental fees are cash-only. No ATMs in Tibiao river area and none on Mararison. Withdraw in Iloilo City or Culasi town.

### Pack for both river and island

Quick-dry clothes, trekking sandals with strap, dry bag for phone, rash guard for island, and a headlamp if staying overnight. Kawa resorts provide towels sometimes, but not always.

### Respect community rules

Mararison is a living barangay, not a resort island. Ask before taking photos of houses, keep noise low after 9 PM, and don't take corals or sand. Guides are locals — hiring them directly helps.

### Don't pair with Seco on the same day if seas are rough

Seco Island is further and needs a bigger boat and very calm conditions. If the coast guard cancels trips due to waves, Mararison may still run, but Seco won't.

## So, Is Antique Worth It From Cebu?

If you want a quick change from Cebu's crowded beaches, Antique makes sense for a long weekend. Fly to Iloilo in the morning, van to Tibiao by lunch, Kawa bath by sunset, Bugtong Bato Falls the next morning, then Mararison for sunset hills and overnight beach stay. Total extra travel beyond the flight is 4 to 6 hours each way, and costs stay low compared to Boracay or Palawan.

Go November to May for the easiest logistics, budget PHP 2,500 to PHP 3,500 for the Antique leg on top of Cebu-Iloilo flights or ferry, and keep your itinerary flexible for boat cancellations in Habagat season.

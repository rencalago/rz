# Bukidnon and Dahilayan Adventure Park Guide

Bukidnon sits 1,200 to 4,700 feet above sea level on the Mindanao highlands, which is why people from Cebu go there for the opposite of beach weather — cool air, pine trees, and wide-open ranch land. The center of that highland loop for most first-timers is Manolo Fortich town, where two parks share one hillside in Barangay Dahilayan: Dahilayan Adventure Park and Dahilayan Forest Park.

Adventure Park is the thrill side, home to Asia's longest dual zipline at 840 meters. Forest Park next door is the resort side, with a luge, canopy walk, go-karts, and hotel rooms. A few minutes away, Pine Grove and the pineapple plantation roads give you that Baguio-like forest backdrop without leaving Mindanao. They are close enough to do in one day from Cagayan de Oro, but far enough apart that you should know which entrance you are paying for.

Here is how to get to Bukidnon and Dahilayan from Cebu via CDO, what each park actually charges right now, how the famous zipline works, and when to go for clear mountain views.

## 1. How to Get to Bukidnon and Dahilayan from Cebu via CDO

There is no direct public transport from Cebu to Bukidnon — you transit through Cagayan de Oro City, then take mountain transport north to Manolo Fortich.

**Step 1: Cebu to Cagayan de Oro**

**By ferry:** 2GO Travel sails Pier 4, Cebu City to CDO Port 2x weekly, usually Monday 11:00 AM and Thursday, taking 8 to 9 hours. Tourist accommodation starts around PHP 1,414 one-way, stateroom PHP 2,300 to PHP 4,700. Cokaliong Shipping Lines also runs Cebu-CDO with fares PHP 1,405 to PHP 2,000 for economy to tourist. Lite Ferries runs via Balingoan or Jagna with connections. Book online a week ahead — CDO sailings fill on weekends.

**By plane:** Cebu Pacific, PAL and AirAsia fly CEB to Laguindingan Airport (CGY) daily, 50 to 70 minutes one-way. Promo fares PHP 900 to PHP 2,000, regular PHP 2,400 to PHP 4,200. From Laguindingan to CDO city, take the LAX-type shuttle or Magnum Express van, 90 to 120 minutes, PHP 250 to PHP 350.

**Step 2: CDO to Manolo Fortich / Dahilayan**

Head to Agora Integrated Bus Terminal in Lapasan, CDO. Look for Rural Transit Mindanao Inc. (RTMI) or Super 5 buses bound for Malaybalay, Valencia, Maramag, Wao, or Davao — all pass Camp Phillips, Manolo Fortich junction.

**Bus fare:** Agora to Manolo Fortich proper is about PHP 86 to PHP 150 depending on aircon and bus type. Agora to Malaybalay is PHP 150 to PHP 250. Buses leave 24 hours, every 20 to 30 minutes, travel time Agora to Manolo Fortich junction is 45 to 75 minutes.

**Last mile to Dahilayan:** Get off at Camp Phillips / Manolo Fortich junction or Damilag crossing. From there, hire a habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) to Dahilayan. Negotiated fare is PHP 150 one-way, PHP 300 to PHP 400 roundtrip with waiting time, 20 to 30 minutes over pineapple plantation roads. If you ask drivers at Agora, some offer direct CDO-to-Dahilayan habal-habal for PHP 400 to PHP 600 one-way, but bus + habal-habal is cheaper and safer.

**Direct shuttle option:** ZIPZONE / Magnum Express runs an air-conditioned shuttle CDO to Dahilayan Adventure Park straight, PHP 249 one-way, PHP 498 roundtrip. Departs from Limketkai area or Agora pickup by booking. Travel time 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes. This is the easiest if you are solo and do not want to transfer to habal-habal.

**Best for:** Day trippers doing Dahilayan in one day from CDO.

**What to do:** Leave CDO by 6:00 to 7:00 AM. You reach Dahilayan by 8:30 AM, dodge midday fog, and catch return buses before 4:30 PM from the highway.

## 2. Dahilayan Adventure Park — Asia's Longest Dual Zipline

This is why most people make the trip. Sitting at about 4,700 feet above sea level, the park strings cables across a forested valley with a 100-meter vertical drop. You ride prone, Superman-style, side by side with a partner.

**The 840-meter dual zipline:** Two parallel lines, 840 meters long, reaching up to 90 kph on windy days. Rate is PHP 500 per person per ride. No age cap, but weight limits usually 30 kg to 120 kg — staff weigh you at the platform. The ride lasts 40 to 60 seconds but feels longer because of the view over pine and farm ridges.

**The 320m + 150m combo zipline:** Shorter, lower, good for first-timers or kids. Starts at the upper platform and connects two segments, PHP 250 per person. Often bundled with the 840m if you want both.

**Other rides that matter:**
- Razorback Mountain Coaster (Alpine coaster) — Single rider PHP 400, Double PHP 600
- Dropzone (free fall drop) — Single PHP 500, Tandem PHP 750 per pair
- Flying Lizard canopy glider, Zorb ball, Bungee Bounce — PHP 150 to PHP 350 each

**Entrance fee:** PHP 100 per person to enter Dahilayan Adventure Park itself, separate from ride fees. You pay at the gate even if you only watch.

**Best for:** Thrill seekers who want the longest zipline run and photo-worthy views over Bukidnon's highlands.

**What to do:** Ride the short 320m first to get comfortable, then do the 840m. Wear closed shoes, secure phones with a lanyard, and tie long hair. Morning rides have steadier wind.

## 3. Dahilayan Forest Park Resort — The Other Side of the Hill

Despite similar names, Dahilayan Forest Park is a separate development beside Adventure Park, not inside it. It runs like a mountain resort with accommodations, a restaurant, and gentler rides.

**Entrance:** Regular day tour PHP 150 per person. Some listings still post weekday PHP 50 / weekend PHP 100, but the more current resort rate is PHP 130 adult / PHP 80 child 10 and below. Hotel guests get free entrance plus 10% off rides and 50% off jacuzzi use. The park is open 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily.

**Rides and package:** The signature PHP 900 ride package usually includes Forest Luge, Go-Kart, Bumper Boat, Mini Golf, Forest Canopy Walk, and Hanging Bridge. Individual rides run PHP 100 to PHP 600 — the Forest Luge solo is around PHP 600.

**Accommodation:** Twin rooms PHP 2,800, Premium PHP 3,200, Park View good for 3 PHP 3,700, Forest Annex good for 4 PHP 4,500. Good if you want to stay overnight and start early at Communal Ranch.

**Best for:** Families and groups who want photos, easy rides, meals with a mountain view, and less waiting than the zipline queues.

**What to do:** If you are staying one night in Manolo Fortich, do Forest Park late afternoon for luge and cafe, then Adventure Park zipline the next morning when lines are short.

## 4. Pine Grove, Communal Ranch and the Scenic Loop

**Pine Grove / Dahilayan Pine Forest:** A 10-hectare stand of Caribbean pine planted as a visual break between pineapple fields. No separate entrance fee — you see it from the road and trail edges between the two parks. Bring a light jacket, it is often foggy by 10 AM and breezy all afternoon. Good for quick drone shots and portraits.

**Impasug-ong Communal Ranch (Communal Ranch):** 642 hectares of government ranchland in Impasug-ong, about 20 to 40 minutes past Dahilayan toward Bukidnon's interior. Entrance PHP 100 per person, corkage for tents PHP 300 to PHP 500 if you camp. Open 6 AM to 6 PM. Best in early morning 6 to 9 AM when horses are out and fog still hugs the ridges. Wear a jacket — mornings average 18 to 22°C. This is where you get New Zealand-like rolling hills.

**Del Monte Pineapple Plantation drive:** The highway from Manolo Fortich to Malaybalay cuts through endless rows of pineapples with Mt. Kitanglad in the back. No fee, just roadside stops.

**Best for:** Travelers who want Baguio-like scenery plus a clear photo itinerary beyond just the zipline.

## 5. Costs to Budget Right Now from Cebu

For one person doing a 2D1N from Cebu:

- **Cebu-CDO-Cebu transport:** Ferry budget PHP 2,828 to PHP 4,000 roundtrip economy/tourist, or flight PHP 1,800 to PHP 6,000 roundtrip if you catch promos. Add Laguindingan to CDO shuttle PHP 500 to PHP 700 roundtrip if flying.
- **CDO to Dahilayan transport:** Bus Agora to Manolo Fortich PHP 86 to PHP 150 one-way, habal-habal PHP 150 to PHP 200 one-way / PHP 300 to PHP 400 roundtrip. Or ZIPZONE Magnum shuttle PHP 498 roundtrip.
- **Entrance fees:** Adventure Park PHP 100, Forest Park PHP 130 to PHP 150, Communal Ranch PHP 100 if added.
- **Rides:** 840m zipline PHP 500, 320m+150m PHP 250, bundled rides PHP 2,550 for 5 rides (Forest Luge, Rainbow Slide, Drop Zone, Razorback, Python Zipline in some promos). Forest Park package PHP 900.
- **Food:** Forest Park Forest Cafe meals PHP 150 to PHP 350 per dish, local carinderias along highway PHP 80 to PHP 150.
- **Stay:** CDO budget hotel PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,800 per night, Dahilayan Forest Park Resort PHP 2,800 to PHP 4,500 per room per night.

For a lean day trip from CDO (no Cebu transport), budget PHP 900 to PHP 1,600 per person with one big zipline, entrance fees, and shared transport. From Cebu with cheapest ferry + bus, budget PHP 4,000 to PHP 5,500 per person all-in for 2 days without hotel upgrades.

## 6. Best Months to Visit Bukidnon and Dahilayan

Bukidnon is a mountain province, not coastal, so its weather lags differently from Cebu or CDO.

**Dry and clearest — December to May:** Northeast monsoon is over, skies clear, roads dry. Temperatures are coolest December to February at 18 to 24°C daytime, 16 to 20°C dawn. March to April is the sweet spot — generally dry, less fog before 10 AM, best for Communal Ranch and zipline photos where clouds otherwise block the valley. Peak crowds are Holy Week and December holidays.

**Shoulder — November:** Transition month, usually good but with afternoon showers. Still better than lowland Mindanao.

**Wet — June to October:** Southwest monsoon brings cloudier mornings and thick fog by mid-morning. Bukidnon rarely gets direct typhoons, but trails get muddy and zipline operations pause during strong winds or heavy rain. Visibility at Communal Ranch drops. If you go in wet months, start early — 6 AM at Communal Ranch, 8 AM at Dahilayan — and plan indoor cafe time after lunch.

**Pro tip:** Check wind at the platform. Even in dry months, staff may pause the 840m zipline if gusts exceed safe limits. If you have only one day, ask the park at opening if the long line is running.

## Tips Before You Go

### Understand you are visiting two parks, not one
Adventure Park and Forest Park have separate gates, separate fees, and separate tickets. Do not assume a receipt from one works in the other. Confirm at the gate which rides you want before paying.

### Book the CDO-Dahilayan shuttle by 6 PM the day before
Magnum Express / ZIPZONE shuttle seats run out on weekends. Message their page early. If you miss it, Rural Transit to Manolo Fortich junction plus habal-habal still works.

### Carry cash in small bills
Gates, habal-habal drivers, and ranch entrances prefer cash. There is no ATM at Dahilayan itself. The nearest working ATMs are in Manolo Fortich town and CDO Agora terminal.

### Wear the right clothes for highland weather
Morning is 18 to 22°C, afternoon can still be 26 to 28°C with sudden fog and drizzle. Bring a light windbreaker, closed shoes with grip for the canopy walk, and a dry bag for phones. Sunscreen still matters — UV is strong at altitude even when foggy.

### If coming from Cebu by ferry, buffer a night in CDO
Cebu-CDO ferries arrive morning or late evening depending on line. Overnight in CDO lets you catch the 6 AM bus to Bukidnon fresh. Trying ferry + bus + habal-habal + zipline all same day is possible but tiring.

### Secure your phone on the zipline
Guards require lanyards or zip pockets. Phones dropped from 840m are not recovered. The official photo from staff on the landing is better than risking your own while flying Superman-style.

## So, Is Bukidnon and Dahilayan Worth It from Cebu?

If you live in Cebu and want pine trees instead of palms for a weekend, yes — the logistics via CDO are well-trodden, the zipline is legitimately the longest dual system in Asia for that Superman ride, and you can pair it with Forest Park luge and Communal Ranch without adding another day if you start early. The trade-off is travel time: you spend 8 hours on a ferry or a day flying and busing for what is essentially 2 to 4 hours of rides and views.

Make it efficient: fly or ferry to CDO, night in CDO, early bus to Manolo Fortich, 840m zipline first, Forest Park lunch and luge, quick Pine Grove stop, bus back to CDO. Add Communal Ranch if you can stay one more night. That sequence gives you the thrill, the family-friendly park, and the highland landscape without backtracking.

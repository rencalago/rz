# Philippines Itinerary for 7, 10 or 14 Days: Cebu Hub Routes to Palawan, Boracay, Bohol & Siargao

The Philippines has 7,600 islands, but you don't need 7,600 decisions. If you base yourself around Cebu, you can link the best of the Visayas and Palawan without constant backtracking to Manila. Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB) has direct flights to El Nido (Lio), Coron (Busuanga), Caticlan (Boracay), Siargao (IAO), and Bohol-Panglao (TAG), plus fast ferries to Bohol in 2 hours.

This guide gives you three ready-to-copy itineraries — 7 days for first-timers, 10 days for the classic mix, and 14 days for the grand circuit — with real PHP costs, how to get there from Cebu or Manila, ferry vs flight trade-offs, and the fees no one puts on the booking page.

Whether you want white sand, lagoons, Chocolate Hills, or Cloud 9 surf, start in Cebu and build out from there.

## 1. Best Months & Quick Logistics from Cebu

**Best for:** Timing your trip correctly before booking flights
**What to do:** Pick your window and pre-book the long hops

- **Overall dry season:** November to May is the safest window for island-hopping anywhere. Seas are calmest, lagoons are clearest.
- **Palawan (El Nido, Coron, Puerto Princesa):** December to May is peak. February to April has the flattest water for El Nido Tours A/C.
- **Boracay & Visayas (Cebu, Bohol):** November to May, with December-February cooler and driest. Boracay is best December to April.
- **Siargao (surf):** Opposite calendar. March to October has better surf, with August to November as peak swell. If you want sun + small surf, go December to February. For serious surf, go September to November.
- **Typhoon risk:** June to October is wettest in Luzon and western Visayas. Eastern Visayas and Mindanao (Siargao) get rainier November to February during Amihan.

**From Cebu Hub — How to get there:**
- **Cebu to Bohol (Tagbilaran):** OceanJet or SuperCat fast ferry, Pier 1, 2 hours, PHP 800-1,200 one-way, hourly departures. Often faster door-to-door than flying.
- **Cebu to Boracay:** Flight Cebu to Caticlan (MPH) 1h-1h15m, PHP 1,200-4,500. Then tricycle + boat to White Beach (terminal + boat PHP 150 + environmental fees). No direct ferry that makes sense.
- **Cebu to El Nido:** Direct flight Cebu-ENI (Lio) 1h-1h15m, PHP 2,500-6,000 via AirSWIFT/CebGo. Budget option: fly to Puerto Princesa (PPS) PHP 1,500-3,500 then van 5-6 hours PHP 600-800 to El Nido. Flight saves a full day.
- **Cebu to Coron:** Direct flight Cebu-USU (Busuanga) 1h, PHP 2,000-5,000, or ferry El Nido to Coron 4-5h PHP 1,800-3,500 if combining Palawan.
- **Cebu to Siargao (IAO):** Direct flight 55m-1h05m, PHP 1,500-5,500. Ferry alternative: Cebu to Surigao overnight 8.5-10h PHP 800-2,500 + Surigao to Dapa (Siargao) 2-3.5h PHP 400-800. Flight wins unless ultra-budget.

## 2. 7-Day Itinerary — Cebu + Bohol + Boracay (Easy First Trip)

**Best for:** First-timers, families, travelers with limited leave who want beach + culture without 5 flights
**What to do:** Stay central Visayas, minimize transfers

- **Day 1-2: Cebu (Mactan + City)** — Arrive CEB, Mactan beach, Cebu City (Magellan's Cross, lechon dinner). Overnight Cebu.
- **Day 3-4: Bohol** — Early OceanJet Cebu to Tagbilaran (2h). Day tour: Chocolate Hills, Philippine Tarsier Sanctuary, Loboc River lunch cruise. Stay Panglao/Alona Beach for snorkeling with turtles at Balicasag. Ferry cost PHP 800-1,200 + day tour PHP 2,500-3,500 private or PHP 1,200 shared.
- **Day 5-7: Boracay** — Fly TAG or CEB to Caticlan (connect via Cebu if from Bohol). White Beach, sunset sail, island-hopping. Fly back Caticlan to Cebu for international connection. 

**Permits/fees 7-day:** Bohol nothing major except terminal fees PHP 25 each port. Boracay Environmental Fee PHP 300-400 + terminal fee PHP 150 + boat PHP 50-100 each way, now collected at Caticlan Jetty. Keep receipts.

## 3. 10-Day Itinerary — Cebu + Bohol + Palawan (Classic Lagon)

**Best for:** Most travelers doing one longer trip; best balance of famous lagoons + Visayas
**What to do:** Combine central Visayas ferries with one Palawan flight

- **Day 1: Arrive Cebu** — Settle in, buy SIM (Globe/Smart PHP 299 for 7-day data), withdraw cash.
- **Day 2-3: Moalboal** — 3h bus/van from Cebu City (PHP 200-300). Sardine run snorkeling (free from shore), Kawasan Falls canyoneering PHP 1,500-2,200 with guide.
- **Day 4-5: Bohol/Panglao** — Ferry Cebu to Tagbilaran 2h. Chocolate Hills, tarsiers, Panglao Island Hopping (Balicasag + Virgin Island) PHP 1,500-2,500 per boat shared.
- **Day 6-9: El Nido, Palawan** — Fly Cebu to El Nido direct or Bohol (TAG) to El Nido via Cebu connection. Budget PHP 4,000-8,000 one-way if booked 3-4 weeks ahead. Days: Tour A (Big Lagoon, Shimizu, Secret Lagoon) PHP 1,200-1,600, Tour C (Hidden Beach, Helicopter Island) PHP 1,400-1,800. Nacpan Beach for chill.
- **Day 10: Return via Cebu** — Flight El Nido to Cebu (1h) then international out. 

**Getting smart:** Fly to Puerto Princesa (PPS) if El Nido direct is PHP 6k+, then van to El Nido. Saves PHP 2,000 but costs 6 hours.

## 4. 14-Day Itinerary — Grand Circuit: Palawan, Boracay, Bohol, Siargao

**Best for:** Second visit, backpackers, couples wanting surf + lagoons + white sand
**What to do:** Loop without backtracking: Cebu > Siargao > Palawan > Boracay > Bohol > Cebu

- **Day 1-2: Cebu City + Mactan** — Arrival, rest.
- **Day 3-6: Siargao** — Fly Cebu to Siargao (IAO) direct morning flight. Base General Luna. Day 3 Cloud 9, Day 4 Tri-island (Naked, Daku, Guyam) PHP 1,500 per boat, Day 5 Magpupungko Rock Pools + afternoon surf lesson PHP 800-1,200/hr, Day 6 scooter around Pacifico. 
- **Day 7-10: El Nido + Coron split** — Fly Siargao to Cebu to El Nido (typically Sayak-CEB-ENI, PHP 4,000-8,000 total, 1 travel day). 2 days El Nido Tours A & C, ferry El Nido to Coron (4.5h, PHP 1,800-3,500, morning departures). 1 day Coron island-hopping (Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon) PHP 1,500-2,000.
- **Day 11-12: Boracay** — Fly Coron (USU) to Caticlan via Manila or Cebu (often USU-MNL-MPH). Two nights White Beach, Willy's Rock, Diniwid. 
- **Day 13-14: Bohol + Depart** — Fly Caticlan to Cebu to Bohol-Panglao (TAG) or ferry to Bohol if time allows. Final night in Panglao, fly TAG to Cebu for exit.

**Alternate 14-day less flying:** Skip Coron, do 4 days El Nido, 3 days Boracay, 3 days Bohol, 2 days Moalboal. Reduces flights from 5 to 3.

## 5. Budget Breakdown — Real PHP Costs 2026

**Backpacker: PHP 2,200-3,500/day (USD 38-62)**
- Dorm/hostel or basic fan room: PHP 600-1,200
- Food: carinderia + market: PHP 300-500/day
- Transport between islands: ferry hops PHP 600-1,500 + 1-2 flights PHP 1,500-3,000 each if booked early
- Tours: shared island-hopping PHP 1,200-1,800/day
- 7-day total: PHP 18,000-28,000 + flights. 14-day: PHP 30,000-45,000.

**Mid-range/Comfort: PHP 5,000-8,500/day (USD 88-150)**
- Private AC room/boutique hotel: PHP 1,800-3,800
- Food: cafe + restaurant: PHP 800-1,400/day
- Private/nice shared tours: PHP 2,500-4,500/day when split 2-3 pax
- Domestic flights: PHP 2,500-5,000 one-way, luggage PHP 500-1,200 for 20kg
- 7-day: PHP 40,000-65,000. 10-day: PHP 55,000-85,000. 14-day: PHP 80,000-120,000 per person excluding international flight.

**Environmental & terminal fees (budget these separately):**
- Boracay: PHP 300-400 environmental + PHP 150 terminal + PHP 150 boat round trip.
- El Nido Eco-Tourism Development Fee (ETDF): PHP 400 valid 10 days (standardized as of 2025).
- Coron environmental PHP 200 at airport + port fees PHP 20.
- Siargao environmental PHP 20-100 at arrival + departure.
- Puerto Princesa environmental PHP 150 if stopping there.
- El Nido Big Lagoon fee: PHP 200 per person (on top of tour price).
- Total for 14-day grand circuit: ~PHP 1,200-1,800 in fees alone.

## 6. Ferry vs Flight Tips — When to Save Time vs Money

**Fly when:**
- Route is Cebu to Siargao, El Nido, Coron, Caticlan — no competitive ferry chain, you save 10-23 hours.
- You have <10 days and want 3+ islands.
- Promo fare appears 4-6 weeks ahead: Cebu Pacific/AirAsia PHP 700-2,500 one-way base. Check total with baggage — a PHP 1,200 fare becomes PHP 2,400 with 20kg.

**Ferry when:**
- Cebu-Bohol (PHP 800-1,200 fast ferry 2h) is almost always faster door-to-door than flying with airport transfers.
- El Nido-Coron ferry (PHP 1,800-3,500) replaces a flight via Manila and lets you carry surfboard/large backpack without weight penalties.
- You want to save one night hotel: 2GO Manila-Cebu overnight 21-23h PHP 2,859-8,615 with bed and meal.
- 2GO, OceanJet, Montenegro are most reliable; book Tourist or Cabin class for overnight.

**Smart mixed strategy:**
Fly into Cebu, ferry Cebu-Bohol-Siquijor-Dumaguete chain if adding Siquijor, fly to Palawan, ferry within Palawan, fly out from last island. Avoid backtracking to Cebu after every island — open-jaw tickets (into Cebu, out from Boracay or Manila) often cost same as round-trip.

## 7. Permits, Documents & Practical Tips

- **Visa:** 157+ countries visa-free 30 days, extendable to 59 days at Bureau of Immigration (~PHP 3,030). 
- **eTravel & cash:** Fill eTravel QR 72h before entry, free. ATMs charge PHP 250 per withdrawal, limit PHP 10,000. Withdraw in Cebu City or Manila — island ATMs run out weekends. GCash/Maya accepted in hotels but tricycle/boat drivers cash-only.
- **Terminal fees & baggage:** Small port terminal fees PHP 25-100, airport terminal fee mostly included in ticket now. Fuel surcharge Level domestic PHP 500-1,700 depending on distance, often baked into fare.
- **Island-hopping safety:** Check DOT-accredited boats, wear life vest, bring dry bag, avoid tours if gale warning. Bring small bills for fees.
- **Seasickness:** Take meds 30 min before ferry, sit mid-ship lower deck.
- **Booking order:** Longest flights first (Cebu-Siargao, Cebu-El Nido), then ferries, then day tours. Holy Week and December 20-Jan 5 prices double — book 6-8 weeks ahead.

## Tips Before You Go

### Don't overpack islands for your days
Rule: 7 days = 2 islands max, 10 days = 3 islands, 14 days = 4 islands. More than that you spend trip in airports. Remove one destination for a slower trip.

### Always compare door-to-door
A PHP 1,200 ferry from Pier 1 arriving at Tagbilaran Port in city center is faster than a PHP 1,000 flight with 1h to airport + 2h wait + 30 min flight + 45 min to Panglao.

### Bring cash and keep fee receipts
Keep El Nido ETDF receipt for 10 days — rangers check it on lagoon stops. Keep Boracay QR code until exit.

## So, Which 7, 10 or 14-Day Route Should You Take?

If it is your first time and you have a week, do Cebu + Bohol + Boracay — easiest logistics, best beaches, no heavy flying. If you have 10 days and want iconic lagoons, do Cebu + Bohol + El Nido — that is the postcard Philippines. If you have 14 days and want surf plus lagoons, do Cebu > Siargao > El Nido > Coron > Boracay or the easier loop Cebu > Bohol > Siargao > El Nido > Cebu.

The common thread is Cebu hub. Fly long legs, ferry short legs. Book 4 weeks ahead, travel November to May for calm seas (except Siargao surf August-November), and budget PHP 1,200-1,800 just for environmental fees.

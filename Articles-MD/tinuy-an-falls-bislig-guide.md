# Tinuy-an Falls Bislig Guide: The Little Niagara of the Philippines

Tinuy-an Falls in Borboanan, Bislig City, Surigao del Sur is called the little Niagara of the Philippines for good reason. It is 55 meters high and 95 meters wide, making it the widest waterfall in the country, with three tiers you can see from the main viewing area and a fourth hidden in the forest. On a strong flow day, the curtain of white water stretches across the whole cliff and throws mist far enough for afternoon rainbows.

The falls sit about 18 kilometers inland from Mangagoy, the commercial center of Bislig. The road cuts through coconut groves and secondary forest before opening to the Bislig River gorge where Tinuy-an drops in three levels. Below the main drop are shallow pools where you can wade, plus a larger basin where bamboo rafts bring you right under the spray.

Here is a practical, current-cost guide to visiting Tinuy-an Falls — fees, raft rental, habal-habal fares, best months, and how to get there from Cebu using the Butuan or Davao jump-off.

<br>
## 1. Entrance Fees and Raft Costs in 2025

Tinuy-an is managed by the Bislig City LGU, so fees are low and paid at the gate.

**Entrance fee:** PHP 50 per adult, often PHP 55 per head on recent visitor logs when local environmental fees are included. Kids 7 years old and below are usually free. The site is open daily, roughly 6 AM to 5 PM, but swimming under the main catch basin can be restricted when water volume is very strong.

**Bamboo raft rental:** PHP 300 to PHP 350 per raft per ride is the common rate in 2024-2025, with a local guide paddling you close enough for a full shower. The older PHP 150 rate is still quoted on some blogs but most recent visits show PHP 300. A raft fits 6 to 8 people, so it comes to PHP 40 to PHP 60 per person if you share. Add PHP 50 to PHP 100 tip for the boatman if he takes photos.

**Cottages and tables:** Small cottage PHP 100 to PHP 300, bigger open huts PHP 500 to PHP 1,000 depending on size. You do not need one if you are just swimming and leaving.

**Life vest:** Often included with the raft, or PHP 20 to PHP 50 rental if you want to swim deeper.

**Best for:** Budget travelers and families — even with raft and cottage, you spend less than PHP 500 per person inside the park.

**What to do:** Pay entrance at the ticket booth, leave your big bag at a rented table, rent a life vest if you are not a strong swimmer, then take the raft to the base of the falls for the classic photo. Go in the morning for softer light and fewer day-trippers from Davao.

## 2. How to Get to Tinuy-an Falls from Bislig and How Much Habal-Habal Costs

Once you are in Bislig City, Tinuy-an is 45 minutes to 1 hour away.

**Mangagoy to Barangay Borboanan:** Most Cebu travelers arrive at the Mangagoy Integrated Terminal in Bislig. From there, you have three options.

**Habal-habal:** The most common. A single motorcycle takes 1 to 2 passengers. Rates vary by negotiation and fuel price:
- One-way: PHP 100 to PHP 150 per motorcycle
- Roundtrip: PHP 350 to PHP 400 per motorcycle when you keep the driver waiting, up to PHP 500 to PHP 600 if you also ask for a side trip
- Day package habal-habal (hotel – Tinuy-an – Enchanted River – hotel): PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,200 per motorcycle for 2 pax, or PHP 250 to PHP 300 per person

**Tricycle / bao-bao:** Some drivers offer PHP 600 to PHP 800 per tricycle roundtrip, fits 3 to 4. Slower and bumpier but more comfortable for bags.

**Van or private car:** If you have a group, charter a van in Mangagoy for PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,000 for Bislig–Tinuy-an–Hinatuan loop. You can also rent from the habal-habal association near the terminal.

The last stretch from the parking area to the ticket gate is a short walk; some parking attendants ask PHP 20 per person for the final habal connection from the souvenir shops to the falls.

**Best for:** Solo and duo travelers should take habal-habal for flexibility. Groups of 4+ should charter a van to split costs and store dry clothes.

**What to do:** Agree on roundtrip price, waiting time, and whether Enchanted River is included before leaving Mangagoy. Take a photo of your driver's plate and tell your hotel.

## 3. How to Get There from Cebu via Butuan or via Davao

There is no direct flight from Cebu to Bislig. You fly to Butuan or Davao, then take a bus or van to Mangagoy (Bislig).

**Option A: Cebu to Tinuy-an via Butuan (Faster if you land early)**

**Cebu (CEB) to Butuan (BXU):** 5 to 7 daily flights by Cebu Pacific, CebGo, and PAL. Flight time 1 hour to 1 hour 10 minutes. One-way promo PHP 1,200 to PHP 2,500, regular PHP 2,700 to PHP 4,500. Mactan Airport is the departure.

**Butuan Airport to Butuan Integrated Terminal:** Taxi or multicab 15 to 20 minutes, PHP 150 to PHP 250.

**Butuan to Mangagoy (Bislig):** Bachelor Express and Davao Metro Shuttle aircon and non-aircon buses run daily. Travel time 4 to 5 hours via San Francisco, Agusan del Sur. Fare PHP 400 to PHP 650 for aircon. Vans are faster, 3.5 to 4 hours, PHP 500 to PHP 700, but less luggage space. First trips start around 4 AM, last around 6 PM.

**Arrival in Mangagoy:** Check in, then take habal-habal next morning or same afternoon if you land before 2 PM.

Total Cebu to Mangagoy via Butuan: 7 to 9 hours including airport time.

**Option B: Cebu to Tinuy-an via Davao (More trips, easy for afternoon arrivals)**

**Cebu (CEB) to Davao (DVO):** 8 to 12 daily flights, 1 hour 25 minutes to 1 hour 40 minutes. Promo PHP 1,300 to PHP 2,800, regular PHP 3,000 to PHP 5,000.

**Davao Airport to Ecoland Bus Terminal:** Taxi 20 to 30 minutes, PHP 200 to PHP 350.

**Davao to Mangagoy:** Bachelor Express buses to Mangagoy depart Ecoland as early as 2 AM. Travel time 5 to 6.5 hours. Fare PHP 550 to PHP 800 aircon. Vans at Gaisano Mall area to Mangagoy, PHP 600 to PHP 800, 5 hours.

**Why pick Davao?** Davao has more late flights and night buses. If you miss Butuan connections, Davao is the fallback.

**Best for:** Travelers who find cheaper Cebu-Davao promo fares or arrive in Mindanao after 3 PM. Butuan route is generally shorter overall from landing to Bislig.

**What to do:** Book the Cebu flight that lands before lunch so you can catch a 1 PM to 3 PM bus to Bislig and arrive before dark. If you land in Davao after 6 PM, stay overnight in Davao and take the 4 AM to 6 AM bus.

## 4. Best Months to Visit and What the Water Looks Like

Tinuy-an has water all year because it is on the Bislig River system, but flow changes a lot.

**Peak flow, dramatic curtain — November to January:** This is the northeast monsoon period for Surigao del Sur. Recent rains from October and November feed the river, so the falls are at their widest and loudest. Great for photos, but swimming near the second tier may be closed and water can be brownish after a hard downpour. Rainbows often appear around 9 AM to 11 AM and again around 2 PM to 3 PM when sun hits mist.

**Clear water and swimming — February to May:** Dry season in Surigao del Sur runs December to May, but February to May is typically clearer. Water is still strong, turquoise-green in the basins, and raft access is easiest. This is the most comfortable window for families.

**Shoulder low flow — June to early October:** Southwest monsoon brings less consistent rain to this side of Mindanao. The falls still run, but the curtain can split into sections during dry weeks. If there was a recent LPA or typhoon trough, it can surge back to full width within a day.

**Avoiding crowds:** Weekends and holidays, especially after the Enchanted River crowd, bring vans from Davao and Butuan. Arrive by 8 AM to get empty raft shots. Weekdays are noticeably quieter.

**Best for:** Photographers chasing the Niagara look should aim for December to February. Swimmers who want calmer pools should aim for March to May.

## 5. Itinerary from Cebu and Other Practical Tips

**2 days / 1 night sample via Butuan:**

Day 0: Fly Cebu to Butuan morning, bus to Mangagoy, check in at a Bislig Bay hotel (PHP 800 to PHP 2,000 per night for fan to aircon).

Day 1: 7 AM habal-habal to Tinuy-an (8 AM arrival). Swim, raft, early lunch at carinderia stalls near entrance (rice + grilled fish PHP 120 to PHP 180). 11 AM ride to Hinatuan Enchanted River (1 to 1.5 hrs by van/habal, entrance PHP 30 to PHP 50). Return to Mangagoy by 4 PM. Evening bus back to Butuan or overnight.

**Budget estimate per person from Cebu (excluding flights):** Bus Butuan-Mangagoy roundtrip PHP 800 to PHP 1,300, habal-habal roundtrip PHP 200 to PHP 300 share, entrance PHP 50 to PHP 55, raft share PHP 50 to PHP 80, food PHP 400 to PHP 600, hotel split PHP 400 to PHP 1,000. Total PHP 1,700 to PHP 3,000 plus flights.

**What to bring:** Aqua shoes or sandals with heel straps, dry bag, rash guard, waterproof pouch for phone, quick-dry towel. The path to the upper tiers is concrete with steps but slippery when wet.

**Safety:** Do not attempt the second tier without a guide when river current is strong. Listen to the blue-shirted tourism guides. Do not fly a drone without LGU clearance — Tinuy-an is in a protected landscape.

## Tips Before You Go

### Confirm fees at the gate, especially for raft
Signs still show PHP 50 entrance, but tourism staff may collect PHP 55 including barangay environmental fee, and raft rates have moved to PHP 300 since 2023. Ask the ticket booth for the current raft chart.

### Book your Butuan or Davao bus before Holy Week and Christmas
Bachelor Express buses to Mangagoy fill fast during long weekends. Vans also sell out by noon. Reserve or be at the terminal early.

### Bring cash in small bills
Bislig to Borboanan has no ATMs. Habal drivers, cottage owners, and carinderias prefer cash. GCash signal is spotty at the falls.

### Protect your camera on the raft
The raft goes under heavy spray. A cheap rain sleeve or ziplock works. The boatmen offer waterproof camera rental for PHP 300 per use, but your own pouch is safer.

### Combine with Enchanted River smartly
Enchanted River closes at 5 PM and has a lunch swimming break from 12 NN to 2 PM when swimming is paused for fish feeding. If you do both in one day, do Tinuy-an early morning, then Enchanted River after 2 PM for swimming.

## So, Is Tinuy-an Worth the Long Trip from Cebu?

If you like big, wide waterfalls that you can actually swim near, Tinuy-an is worth the flight-plus-bus effort. It is not a hidden trickle you climb for 30 minutes — it is a 95-meter-wide, 55-meter-high curtain you hear before you see, with bamboo rafts that take you into the mist for that classic little Niagara photo.

From Cebu, the Butuan jump-off keeps travel time shortest, while Davao gives you more flight and bus options if you arrive late. For best conditions, target December to May, with December to February for power and March to May for clear swimming. Budget PHP 50 to PHP 55 entrance, PHP 300 per raft shared by your group, and PHP 350 to PHP 400 per habal-habal roundtrip, and you have already covered the core costs.

Pack light, go early, and stay a night in Bislig so you are not rushing back for a night bus.

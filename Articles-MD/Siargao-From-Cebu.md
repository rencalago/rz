# Siargao from Cebu

Siargao is best known for Cloud 9, but even if you do not surf, the island works as a beach and island-hopping base with mangroves, tide pools, and sandbars. From Cebu, you have two realistic options: a direct flight to Siargao Airport (IAO) in Del Carmen, or a longer ferry route via Surigao City.

Flying takes an hour. The ferry route takes 11 to 12 hours door-to-door. Most Cebu-based travelers fly one way and consider the ferry only if they want to bring a motorbike or travel on a tight budget.

Here is how the routes compare, what they cost right now, and where to stay once you land.

## 1. Flights: Cebu to Siargao — The Fastest Option

**Route:** Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB) to Siargao Airport (IAO) in Del Carmen. Flight time is about 55 minutes to 1 hour 5 minutes direct.

**Airlines:** Cebu Pacific/Cebgo, Philippine Airlines, and Sunlight Air operate direct Cebu to Siargao flights. There are typically 1 to 2 direct flights per day, more during peak months November to May. Some aggregators list connecting flights via Manila or Clark, but direct flights exist and are the practical choice from Cebu.

**Fares:** Promo one-way fares start around PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500 when booked early, regular fares PHP 3,000 to PHP 5,500. Add baggage if you carry surfboards — boards usually have a separate sports equipment fee.

**Airport to General Luna:** Siargao Airport is not in General Luna. It is in Del Carmen, about 45 to 60 minutes by van to General Luna and Cloud 9. Shared van transfers cost PHP 300 to PHP 500 per person, private van PHP 2,000 to PHP 3,500 per vehicle. Most hotels can arrange pickup if you message ahead. Road is paved but narrow in parts — expect slow traffic after landing.

**Best for:** Anyone who values time, surfers with limited days, and travelers who want to avoid overnight ferries.

**What to do:** Book an early morning Cebu departure — you can be surfing Cloud 9 by late morning. For return, afternoon flights give you a morning surf window.

## 2. Ferry: Cebu to Siargao via Surigao City — The Budget, Long Route

There is no direct ferry from Cebu to Siargao Island. You take a ferry from Cebu to Surigao City, then a fast craft from Surigao City to Siargao.

**Leg 1 — Cebu City to Surigao City:** Cokaliong Shipping, Starlite Ferries, and Medallion Transport run overnight ferries from Cebu Pier 1 or Pier 3 to Surigao Base Port. Schedule is typically 9:00 PM to 10:00 PM departure, arriving 7:00 to 8:00 AM next day — about 8.5 to 10 hours. No sailing on some Saturdays, so check the day before. Fares: economy PHP 800 to PHP 1,200, tourist/AC cabin PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,500.

**Leg 2 — Surigao City to Siargao (Dapa Port):** Fast crafts leave Surigao City to Dapa, Siargao multiple times a day, usually 5:30 AM, 7:00 AM, 11:00 AM, and 1:00 PM depending on operator. Travel time 2 to 3.5 hours depending on sea conditions and whether it is a fast craft or RORO. Fare PHP 400 to PHP 800 per person, plus terminal fee PHP 25 to PHP 50.

**Total time:** About 11 to 13 hours from Cebu Pier to Dapa Port if connections line up. If you miss the morning fast craft in Surigao, you wait several hours.

**Dapa Port to General Luna:** Van or habal-habal from Dapa to General Luna takes 30 to 45 minutes, PHP 100 to PHP 300 per person shared, PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,500 private.

**Best for:** Budget travelers, travelers bringing a motorbike, or those who want an overnight boat experience.

**Practical tip:** If you take the ferry, book Cebu to Surigao in a cabin — you get a bunk and aircon for the 10-hour crossing. Bring your own water and snacks; food on board is limited.

## 3. Where to Stay in Siargao

Siargao's tourism is concentrated, but neighborhoods feel different.

**General Luna — The Main Hub:** About 90 percent of first-time visitors stay here. This is where you find most restaurants, tour desks, motorbike rentals, coworking cafes, and nightlife. Walking distance to shops, 5 to 10 minutes by habal-habal to Cloud 9. Best for first-timers, solo travelers, and anyone who wants options within walking distance.

- Budget: Dorms PHP 500 to PHP 1,000 per bed, basic private rooms PHP 1,200 to PHP 2,500.
- Mid-range: Boutique guesthouses and small resorts PHP 3,000 to PHP 6,000.
- Higher: Design resorts and villas PHP 7,000 to PHP 15,000.

**Cloud 9 and Tourism Road — For Surfers:** The area around the Cloud 9 boardwalk, 2 to 3 km from General Luna proper. Closest to the main break, with surf schools and board rentals on every corner. Quieter at night than central General Luna, but still has cafes and resorts. You will need a motorbike or tricycle to get to town for dinner if you stay here without your own transport.

- Good choice if surfing is your priority and you want dawn patrol without a long ride.
- Tricycle from General Luna to Cloud 9 is around PHP 25 per person each way.

**Pacifico and North — Quieter and More Spread Out:** North of General Luna, toward Burgos and Pacifico. More empty beaches, fewer crowds, better for travelers who want to surf uncrowded breaks or just have space. Requires your own motorbike — distances between resorts are longer, and restaurants are sparse.

**Dapa and Del Carmen — Local and Practical:** Dapa is the port town, not a tourist area, but it has cheaper guesthouses and markets. Del Carmen is near the airport and the mangrove reserve. Useful if you have an early flight, but not where you want to base for beach and surf.

**Booking tips:** November to May is peak, especially December, Holy Week, and the WSL Cloud 9 surfing competition window (usually September). Book at least 2 to 3 weeks ahead for peak. Many guesthouses are cash-preferred — bring small bills. Power and water interruptions still happen; mid-range and higher resorts usually have generators.

## 4. How Many Days and What to Do Beyond Surfing

**Ideal stay:** 4 to 6 days from Cebu if you fly. That gives you one day for travel, two to three days for surf or island hopping, and one day for land tours.

**Non-surf highlights:**
- **Tri-island hopping:** Naked Island, Daku Island, and Guyam Island — half-day, PHP 1,500 to PHP 3,000 per boat shared.
- **Magpupungko Rock Pools:** Natural tide pools in Pilar, best at low tide, about 45 minutes from General Luna. Environmental fee around PHP 50 to PHP 100.
- **Maasin River and Coconut Road:** Popular for the bent palm tree swing and drone shots, accessible by motorbike.
- **Sohoton Cove and Bucas Grande:** Full-day trip to jellyfish sanctuary and caves, better as an organized tour.

## Tips Before You Go

### Bring a dry bag and reef shoes

Siargao roads can flood briefly during rain, and island hopping involves wading. A dry bag keeps electronics safe. Reef shoes help on rocky entries.

### Rent a motorbike only if you are confident

Rental is PHP 350 to PHP 500 per day. Roads are mostly paved but have sand patches and dogs crossing. Helmets are mandatory — ask for one that actually fits.

### Book your airport van ahead

Shared vans fill up quickly when a flight lands. Message your hotel a day before with your flight number.

### Cash is still king

ATMs in General Luna run out during long weekends. Withdraw in Cebu City before flying. Most restaurants accept GCash, but small tour operators do not.

## So, Flight or Ferry from Cebu?

Fly if you have 4 days or fewer and want to maximize time on the island. The direct Cebu to Siargao flight gets you from Mactan to Cloud 9 in about 3 hours including transfers.

Take the ferry via Surigao if you are on a budget, have plenty of time, or want to bring a motorbike. It is an overnight adventure rather than a quick transfer, and you trade 10 extra hours for lower cost.

For most Cebu-based travelers, the best value is a promo direct flight out and a flexible return — book General Luna near Tourism Road for your first Siargao trip, close enough to Cloud 9 to walk or take a short tricycle, but still near restaurants and tour desks.

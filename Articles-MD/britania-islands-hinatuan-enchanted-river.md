# Britania Islands and the Hinatuan Enchanted River

If you are looking for white sand without the Boracay crowd and a river that looks filtered blue for real, Surigao del Sur delivers both in one trip. The Britania Group of Islands in San Agustin is a cluster of 24 low islets off Lianga Bay, with powdery sandbars and coconut-shaded strips. Less than two hours south, the Hinatuan Enchanted River is a saltwater river that turns deep sapphire and emerald depending on the light and depth, with a daily fish feeding ritual that draws everyone to the main pool.

From Cebu, the most practical gateway is Butuan. Cebu Pacific flies CEB to Butuan (BXU) daily in about 1 hour 10 minutes. From Butuan, you take a van or Bachelor bus south to San Agustin and Hinatuan. There is also an older route via Surigao City, but Butuan saves you a ferry transfer and keeps total land time to 3.5 to 5 hours.

This guide puts Britania Islands, Hagonoy Island, and the Enchanted River together as a 2-day to 3-day loop from Cebu, with current fees, transport options, how to move between the two spots, and the months when the water looks its best.

## 1. Britania Islands, San Agustin, Surigao del Sur

**Location:** Barangay Britania, San Agustin, Surigao del Sur, about 6 km from the highway junction at Barangay Salvacion and 3 km from Britania Port. The islands face the Pacific but sit inside Lianga Bay, so waters are usually calm in the dry months.

The tourism name covers 24 islets and sandbars, but most island-hopping tours visit four main ones: Naked Island (the white sandbar that disappears partly at high tide), Hagonoy Island, Boslon Island, and Hiyor-Hiyoran Island. Some tours swap in Panlanggan or the smaller limestone outcrops depending on tide and wind.

**Fees and boat costs (current as of 2025):**
- Environmental fee collected at Britania Port: PHP 25 per adult, PHP 10 per child. Some barangay posts add a PHP 25 per vehicle toll if you arrive by car or van.
- Island hopping boat rental: PHP 1,500 per boat for up to 10 to 15 persons, PHP 100 per extra head beyond 15. Capacity 15 to 20 pax for small pump boats, up to 35 for larger bangka. No official joiner system — if you are solo, you pay the whole boat or wait for other travelers at the port to share. From 6 AM, boats are at the port.
- Cottage rental on the mainland: PHP 300 to PHP 500 per cottage if you want to leave your bags. On-island, no cottages — you bring your own shade.
- No island entrance fees are collected on the islets themselves.

**What it’s like on the tour:** Trips start early, around 6:00 to 7:00 AM, to catch low tide and soft light. Boats are open bangka with outrigger. Travel between islets is 10 to 20 minutes. Expect strong sun even with clouds — the sand reflects. Most boats provide life vests but no dry bags.

**Best for:** Cebu travelers who want a chill sandbar day, photographers who want empty white stretches, and families who prefer short boat rides over open-sea crossings. Not ideal if you want beach bars or resorts on the sand — the islets are kept undeveloped.

**What to do:**
- Do the 4-islet loop: start at Hagonoy for shade, then Boslon for exploring at low tide, then Hiyor-Hiyoran for snorkeling on the reef edge, end at Naked Island when the tide is lowest and the bar is fully exposed.
- Snorkel near Boslon and Hiyor-Hiyoran — bring your own mask. Coral is close to shore, so wear reef shoes.
- Bring your own food and water. There are sari-sari stores in Britania proper, but nothing on the islets. Several resorts in Barangay Britania offer island hopping with lunch packages at PHP 680 per head minimum 10 pax for day tour, PHP 1,390 per head with overnight and breakfast for 2D1N.

## 2. Hagonoy Island — The Most Photogenic Stop

**Why it gets a separate mention:** Among the four regular stops, Hagonoy is the balanced one — biggest usable area, long stretch of creamy white sand, clear water on both sides, and a cluster of coconut and talisay trees in the center that give real shade. Locals call it the lover’s island, others call it tadpole-shaped because of the narrow sand tail visible from the boat.

It sits about 10 minutes by boat from Naked Island and is usually the first or second stop in most boatmen’s itineraries because it’s the farthest from the port. The island is uninhabited. A small ruined nipa structure remains from a fisherman who once tried to settle there, but overnight camping and cooking are not allowed now to protect the area.

The sand is fine, not gravelly, and the water drop stays shallow for several meters — good for wading and for kids. At the rocky side, the water turns a deeper turquoise and visibility is best mid-morning when the sun is high. If you visit with a small group, ask your boatman to stay at least 45 minutes here; most package tours rush it to 20 minutes.

**Best for:** Those who want shade without renting anything, couples and families who want a base island for lunch (boodle fight on a mat), and anyone who wants classic Britania photos without people in the background — early arrival helps.

**What to do:**
- Walk the full sandbar loop at low tide. The tail is narrow but walkable when water is knee-deep.
- Swim on the leeward side where the sand is softest. Keep an eye on sea urchins near the rocks on the far end.
- Take photos from the boat approach — Hagonoy photographs best from slightly offshore where the palm cluster and white tail are both visible.

## 3. Hinatuan Enchanted River, Barangay Cambatong, Hinatuan

**What it is:** A short, spring-fed saltwater river that flows to the Pacific, famous for its 80-feet deep natural pool that looks electric blue at midday. The color comes from depth and mineral content, not chemicals. The site is managed by the LGU of Hinatuan — swimming is allowed only in the designated main pool area, and the deeper blue hole is off-limits to swimmers for safety and conservation.

**Fees (very affordable and managed):**
- Entrance fee: PHP 30 per head for adults, PHP 20 for seniors and locals of Hinatuan. This rate has stayed consistent across multiple tourism advisories.
- Cottage rental: PHP 100 to PHP 300 depending on size. Tables PHP 100. 
- Life jacket rental: PHP 15 to PHP 30 per hour, required if you cannot swim and recommended for everyone due to depth beyond the swimming line.
- Parking/habal-habal from highway junction to river: PHP 50 to PHP 150 per person each way if coming by public transport; direct habal-habal round trip from Hinatuan town to river about PHP 300.

**Hours and rituals:** Site is open daily 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM. The fish feeding is scheduled at 12:00 NN every day, and sometimes an additional one at 3:00 PM when there are many visitors. The caretakers play the Hinatuan Hymn, the fish — mostly jacks and a variety of saltwater species — come in from the deeper cave, and swimming is paused until feeding finishes. Best time to be in the water is 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM when sunlight hits the pool directly, or right after feeding around 12:30 PM when the fish clear and the water settles again.

**Best for:** Day trippers pairing the river with Tinuy-an Falls in Bislig, travelers who want a quick, cheap natural attraction that does not need a boat, and photographers chasing the blue-green gradient.

**What to do:**
- Swim in the marked area — the water is cold even on hot days. Bring a rash guard if you get cold easily.
- Watch the feeding from the deck, not from the water. Do not throw food.
- Rent a boat at the river mouth for PHP 500 per boat for 3 hours to explore the surrounding islands and Sibadan Fish Cage if you want to extend.
- Eat at stalls near the entrance — fresh seafood and rice meals PHP 80 to PHP 150 per order.

## 4. How to Get There from Cebu: Via Butuan or Via Surigao City

**Option A — Via Butuan (Recommended from Cebu):**

1. **Fly Cebu to Butuan:** Cebu Pacific operates daily direct flights CEB to BXU, flight time 1 hour 5 minutes to 1 hour 20 minutes. Regular one-way fares PHP 1,500 to PHP 4,000 depending on how early you book. Cebu Pacific lists several flights a day in peak months. Butuan Bancasi Airport is about 10 to 15 minutes from Butuan city by tricycle PHP 100 to PHP 150 or airport van.

2. **Butuan to San Agustin / Britania:** From Butuan Langihan Bus Terminal or from direct vans at the airport, take a van or Bachelor Express bus bound for Tandag, Mangagoy, or Bislig. Tell the conductor to drop you at Barangay Salvacion junction for Britania. 
   - Van: PHP 350 per person, 3 to 4 hours, more comfortable and faster.
   - Bus non-aircon: PHP 200 to PHP 250 per person, 4 to 5 hours.
   - Once at Salvacion junction, habal-habal or tricycle to Britania Port: PHP 20 to PHP 40 per person, 10 to 20 minutes. 
   - Total Butuan to Britania Port door-to-door is 4 to 5 hours if you include waiting.

3. **Britania to Hinatuan (Enchanted River):**
   - The fastest is to stay overnight in Britania and take a van the next morning to Hinatuan. Britania to Hinatuan town is about 1 to 1.5 hours by van, PHP 60 to PHP 100 per person. From Hinatuan terminal to Enchanted River jump-off, habal-habal PHP 50 per person or PHP 150 to PHP 200 per bike if solo.
   - Alternative if coming from Bislig: Bislig to Hinatuan is about 1 hour via van PHP 60. Hinatuan terminal to river is another 40 minutes.
   - If you are in a group, renting a private van for the San Agustin–Hinatuan–Bislig loop costs about PHP 4,000 to PHP 6,000 per day including driver, fuel, and meals, split among passengers.

**Option B — Via Surigao City:**

1. Fly Cebu to Surigao (SUG) if there is a direct flight on the day you travel, or take an overnight ferry from Cebu to Surigao City (Cokaliong, Starlite). Cebu to Surigao by ferry is 9 to 11 hours, PHP 800 to PHP 2,500 depending on accommodation.

2. From Surigao City to San Agustin: Take a bus bound for Butuan and transfer at Butuan to a Tandag-bound bus, or take a direct bus to Tandag and transfer to a Butuan-bound bus that stops at Salvacion. Total Surigao City to Britania is 6 to 8 hours with one transfer. Fare PHP 450 to PHP 600 total.

For most Cebu-based travelers, Butuan is faster and cheaper than the Surigao City ferry connection.

- Butuan to Britania van roundtrip: PHP 700

- 1 night basic resort in Britania: PHP 1,200 to PHP 2,500 per room fan/aircon

## 5. Best Months, Weather, and Safety Notes

**Best months to visit both spots:** December to May is the dry season window for Surigao del Sur. For the clearest water at Enchanted River and calm seas for Britania island hopping, February to April is peak — skies are clear, river color is most vivid blue around 10 AM to 12 NN, and Pacific swells are lower. 

November to January can still work but expect more rain and occasional shear line. June to September is the Habagat and more frequent low-pressure areas in eastern Mindanao — sea trips can be cancelled. The river still runs clear after rain, but water level rises and visibility drops.

**Day timing:** Do Britania before 10 AM or after 2 PM for softer light and lower heat. Do Enchanted River between 9:00 AM and 11:00 AM for best color, and be there before 12:00 NN for the fish feeding. Avoid weekends and holidays if you want fewer crowds — Enchanted River can get pool-party crowded on long weekends.

**Safety:** No lifeguard towers on Britania islets. Wear life vests when snorkeling off the boat. In Enchanted River, stay within the swimming buoy line; the deep hole beyond is 80 feet and has strong currents. Both sites have spotty mobile signal — Globe usually works better than Smart in Britania port, but almost no signal at Enchanted River pool.

**Packing:** Dry bag, reef shoes, rash guard, power bank, cash (ATMs are in Bislig, Tandag, and Butuan — not in Britania), reusable water bottle. Most resorts and boatmen now enforce no single-use plastic on the islets.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book the Butuan van early and ask for a Britania drop

Vans from Butuan fill by late morning. Message your resort in Britania — many have partner van drivers who will pick you up at Bancasi Airport for PHP 350 and drop directly at your resort, saving you one habal-habal ride.

### Pair the spots in the right order

Do Britania first, stay overnight in San Agustin, then do Enchanted River and Tinuy-an Falls next day toward Bislig. This avoids backtracking — Britania is north of Hinatuan, Bislig is south. If you fly out of Butuan again, loop back via Barobo to Butuan.

### Bring exact cash and split wisely

Boat rental has no card payment. If you are solo, arrive at Britania Port at 7:00 AM and ask the tourism desk if there are other solo travelers to share a boat. Environmental fee and Enchanted River fee have official receipts — ask for them.

### Respect local rules

Cooking and overnight camping are not allowed on Hagonoy and Naked Island. In Enchanted River, no diving, no fishing, no soap or shampoo in the pool, and life jackets if required by staff. Fish feeding is managed — do not throw bread or chips.

### Plan for power and signal

Bring a 20,000 mAh power bank. Many homestays in Britania have generator hours only. Download offline maps for the Butuan–San Agustin–Hinatuan route before you leave Butuan.

## So, Should You Do Britania Islands and Enchanted River Together?

Yes — if you have 2 to 3 full days from Cebu, this pairing works better than doing either alone. Britania gives you the classic white-sand-and-coconut island day with easy 10-minute boat hops. Hagonoy Island alone is worth the trip if you want that empty tropical photo. Enchanted River gives you a completely different kind of water — cold, deep blue, riverine, and cultural with the feeding ritual — for just PHP 30 entrance.

Fly Cebu to Butuan, ride a van to San Agustin for your first night, do the island hopping early next morning, move to Hinatuan by lunch, catch the 12 NN feeding and a swim, and either stay in Bislig or head back to Butuan. February to May gives you the best chance of calm seas and that bright sapphire color, weekdays give you space to enjoy it.

It is not a luxury circuit — roads are partly bumpy, boats are basic bangka, and you will carry cash and your own snacks. But for low cost, easy logistics from Cebu, and two very different faces of Surigao del Sur in one loop, it is one of the most efficient southern Mindanao side trips you can do.

# Cagayan de Oro White-Water Rafting Guide: Cagayan River Routes, Costs, and Cebu Travel

Cagayan de Oro is called the white-water rafting capital of the Philippines for a reason. The Cagayan River runs 90 kilometers from Bukidnon highlands to Macajalar Bay, cutting through 20-plus rapids that are paddled almost every day of the year. You do not need to be an athlete to try it, but you do need to pick the right course.

Rafting in CDO is commercial, regulated, and run by DOT-accredited outfitters with certified guides. Trips include transport from the city, safety gear, briefing, and a river guide in every raft. You just need to choose between beginner and advanced, book a slot, and show up.

This guide covers the actual routes along Cagayan River, current rates from PHP 1,000 to PHP 2,500, where to book, best months for water level, how to get to CDO from Cebu by direct flight, and safety details first-timers often miss.

## 1. The River and the Three Levels

**Route:** All commercial trips happen on the Cagayan River, which acts as the natural border between Bukidnon and Cagayan de Oro. Put-in points are along the Bukidnon-CDO highway boundary, take-out is near the city.

The operators use three standard levels. Names vary slightly between shops — Beginner/Basic, Advance 1, Advance 2, Extreme — but the structure is consistent.

**Beginner / Basic Course — Best for first-timers, families, barkada:**
- Distance: 12 km, 14 rapids
- Rapids: Class II to III, mostly wash and waves, one or two bigger drops
- River time: 2.5 to 3.5 hours actual paddling
- Total time including briefing and transport: 4 to 5 hours
- What to expect: You will get wet, you will paddle, there are calm sections for swimming. Guides call instructions the whole way. This is the course most Cebu travelers take if they have a flight the same afternoon.

**Advance Course — For fit beginners who want more:**
There are two flavors. Advance 1 starts higher upstream than Advance 2.
- Distance: 16 to 18 km, 18 to 21 rapids
- Rapids: Class III to IV, including named rapids like Gotla, Rodeo, and The Washing Machine
- River time: 4 to 5 hours
- Total time: 5.5 to 7 hours
- What to expect: Longer, more continuous rapids, stronger current, more time in the water if you fall. You should be comfortable swimming with a life vest. Some operators set a minimum age of 15 for Advance.

**Extreme Course — By arrangement only:**
- Distance: 20+ km, 24+ rapids, includes uppermost sections
- Rapids: Class IV during high water, can be canceled if river is too high
- River time: 5+ hours
- Requirements: Previous rafting experience, strong swimming ability, medical clearance for some operators. Not offered daily; booked when river conditions are right and with minimum 6 to 8 pax.

**Best for:** Beginner if it is your first time, you travel with non-swimmers, or you want a half-day. Advance if you have done rafting before in Chico River, Davao, or Bali and want a real workout.

**What to do:** Pick morning trip 7:30 AM for cooler air and fewer rafts on the river. Afternoon trip 12:00 NN to 1:00 PM works if you land from Cebu on the morning flight.

## 2. How Much It Costs in 2025-2026 and What Is Included

Rates are per head and change slightly by season and fuel prices. Current published ranges from licensed operators:

- **Beginner / Basic:** PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,500 per person. Promo rates go down to PHP 900 on weekdays with large groups. Klook and local desks often list PHP 1,349 to PHP 1,400 for this course.
- **Advance 1:** PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,500 per person. This is the most popular for adrenaline seekers.
- **Advance 2:** PHP 1,500 to PHP 2,100 per person. Slightly shorter than Advance 1 but still Class III-IV.
- **Extreme:** PHP 2,000 to PHP 2,500 per person, minimum 6 pax, by reservation.
- **Combo packages:** Basic + 3 zipline rides PHP 1,500 per person, Advance + zipline PHP 2,100 per person offered by some outfitters near the take-out.

**Inclusions you should expect:** Roundtrip transport within CDO city proper (usually jeepney or van to put-in), DOT-certified guide, helmet, PFD/life vest, paddle, safety briefing and insurance, light snacks or lunch after take-out for Advance. Towel, waterproof bag, and photos are sometimes extra.

**Permits and fees:** There is no separate environmental fee you pay at the riverbank like in El Nido. The operator's fee covers local tourism registration and barangay access. Bring valid ID. Some outfitters ask for a small mandatory insurance form signing.

**How payments work:** Most accept GCash, bank transfer, or cash on site. Online booking platforms take card. Downpayment of PHP 300 to PHP 500 per person is common for private groups.

**Best for:** Budget travelers — take weekday Beginner. Groups of 6 can often negotiate pickup included from Limketkai or Centrio.

**What to do:** Confirm group minimum. Most require 6 persons to run. If you are 2 or 3 from Cebu, ask to be joined with another group instead of paying for a private raft.

## 3. Where to Book — Accredited Operators

Book with a licensed outfitter, not a random van driver at the airport. Certified operators follow Department of Tourism safety standards and have rescue kayakers.

**Top options:**

1. **Kagay Whitewater Rafting (Kagay Outdoors)** — The longest-running and DOT-accredited operator. Booking office at #1 Rivera Building, Corrales St cor Rivera St, CDO. Daily trips 7:30 AM and 12:00 NN. Online booking at kagaywhitewaterrafting.veent.co. Contact 0917-712-2442 / 0917-712-2323. The most referenced by returning visitors.

2. **Great White Water Tours** — Location near Xavier Estates. Beginner to Advance covered, lunch after the run. Often listed on Klook and Traveloka.

3. **Bugsay Rafting / CDO White Water Rafting & Adventure Tours / Red Rafts** — Second wave of DOT-accredited operators with similar pricing. Good for last-minute slots.

Avoid booking a trip that does not include helmet and PFD, or that cannot name its river guide certification.

## 4. How to Get to Cagayan de Oro from Cebu

**Fastest: Direct Flight Cebu to CDO**

- **Airports:** Mactan-Cebu International Airport (CEB) to Laguindingan International Airport (CGY). Laguindingan is the airport for Cagayan de Oro and Iligan, located 46 km west of CDO city.
- **Airlines:** Cebu Pacific (5J), Philippine Airlines / PAL Express (PR/2P), daily direct flights. Typically 3 to 5 direct flights a day.
- **Flight time:** 50 minutes to 1 hour 5 minutes direct, no stopover.
- **Fares:** Promo one-way starts around PHP 1,200 to PHP 1,800, regular PHP 2,500 to PHP 4,500. Book 2 weeks ahead for better rates. Morning departures 5:00 AM to 10:00 AM are most useful for rafting.
- **Laguindingan to CDO city:** Travel time 45 minutes to 1.5 hours depending on traffic. Options: Magnum Express van PHP 200 per person to Limketkai Mall, LAX Shuttle van PHP 249 to Centrio Mall, taxi PHP 800 to PHP 1,200, Grab may be available but limited. Most rafting operators include city pickup for the tour itself, so you only need to get from airport to your hotel.

**Sample same-day plan from Cebu:** Take 5:00-6:00 AM Cebu to CGY flight, land by 6:50 AM, van to city by 8:00 AM, join 7:30-8:00 AM hotel pickup that waits for flight arrivals if you pre-arrange, finish Beginner by 12:30 PM, back in city for late lunch.

**Alternative: Ferry via Bohol or direct sea** is possible but takes 12+ hours via Cokaliong or Trans-Asia from Cebu Pier 1 to CDO port. Not practical if you are only going for rafting.

**Best for:** Weekend warriors from Cebu — fly out Friday morning or Saturday morning, raft Saturday, return Sunday.

**What to do:** Book your rafting trip after you have a flight confirmation. Give the operator your flight number so they adjust pickup.

## 5. Best Months and Weather for Rafting

CDO is outside the main typhoon belt and rafting runs year-round because Cagayan River is fed by 26 tributaries. But water character changes.

- **June to December / July to December:** Highest water, strongest rapids. Locals and guides say this is ideal for Advance and Extreme. Water is brownish but rapids are more technical. July to October has most consistent high flow.
- **December to May:** Dry season, clearer water, manageable Class II-III for Beginner. Best for first-timers, non-swimmers, and families. March to May has warmest weather and lower water, making swims calmer.
- **February to May:** For general CDO sightseeing and side trips to Camiguin or Bukidnon, drier months are more reliable even if rafting is milder.

There is no closed season. If heavy rain or a low-pressure area hits Bukidnon overnight, operators may delay, downgrade Advance to Beginner, or cancel for safety. They will refund or rebook.

**Best for:** Adrenaline — go August to November. First time or photos where water looks bluer — go March to May.

**What to do:** Message your operator the night before for river level. During rainy months, bring a dry change of clothes in a real dry bag, not a plastic labo.

## 6. Safety, What to Wear, and What to Bring

CDO rafting is safe when you follow instructions, but it is still a moving river.

**Safety checklist:**
- Guide always at the back with paddle commands — forward, back paddle, stop, get down. Listen before you worry about filming.
- Everyone wears helmet and Class V PFD, even strong swimmers. Check your vest straps before launch.
- Not allowed if you have certain heart conditions, recent surgery, or are heavily intoxicated. Pregnant travelers are not allowed.
- Minimum age 7 to 8 years for Beginner in low water, 12 to 15 for Advance depending on operator. Operators will ask age.
- Non-swimmers can join Beginner with a guide noting it — you stay in the middle of the raft. You must be willing to float on your back in the PFD if you fall out.
- Rescue kayak usually follows the group. Know the whitewater swim position: feet up, downstream, hands steering.

**What to wear:**
- Quick-dry shirt, board shorts, river sandals with straps, booties, or old sneakers. No slippers/flip-flops and no barefoot.
- No cotton jeans. They get heavy when wet.
- Leave jewelry, long necklaces, and cotton caps.

**What to bring:**
- Waterproof action camera with floater or tether. Phones in waterproof case only if case is proven — river claims many phones.
- Sunblock, lip balm, 1-liter water bottle, light snacks.
- Towel, dry clothes, toiletries in a bag you leave on the van.
- PHP 200 to PHP 300 extra for photos/video if you want operator's GoPro shots.
- ID and small cash for zipline if you add it.

**After the trip:** Most operators serve lunch or heavy snacks at their headquarters — pork BBQ, rice, fresh fish. Take a shower before eating. Tip your guide if service was good, PHP 100 to PHP 200 per guide is appreciated.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book morning on your first day

You get calmer winds, less sunburn, and extra buffer if your Cebu flight is delayed. Advance courses can run long — morning avoids night driving back.

### Confirm pickup and minimum

If you are 1 to 3 people, ask to join a shared raft. If you are private group of 6+, you get your own raft and can request transport from your hotel in Limketkai, Centrio, or Downtown.

### Train a bit the night before

Advance is a full-body workout. Hydrate, sleep early, avoid heavy drinking. Shoulder pain the next day is common.

### Cash, eTravel, and ID

Bring a valid government ID for insurance form. eTravel registration is for international arrival, not needed for domestic Cebu-CDO flight, but keep your airline boarding pass handy for hotel check-in.

### Do not combine with Camiguin on the same day

Ferry to Camiguin is from Balingoan, 1.5 hours from CDO city plus boat. Do rafting on a separate day from island day trips.

## So, Should You Go Rafting from Cebu for the Weekend?

Yes, if you want something different from island hopping. From Cebu, you can fly to Laguindingan in under an hour, ride PHP 200 to PHP 249 van to the city, and be on the water before noon. Beginner costs PHP 1,000 to PHP 1,500 for 12 km and 14 rapids, Advance PHP 1,800 to PHP 2,500 for 18 to 21 rapids, inclusive of gear and guide. July to December gives you the wild ride, December to May gives you the friendlier, photo-ready ride.

Book directly with Kagay or Great White Water Tours, lock in a morning slot, wear shoes with straps, and listen to your guide when he shouts "get down." That one instruction saves most swim stories from becoming rescue stories.

For Cebu-based rafters, CDO rafting works best as a 2-day, 1-night trip: fly in Day 1 morning, Beginner afternoon, city dinner. Day 2 Advance or side trip to Dahilayan or Camiguin, then fly back to Cebu evening.

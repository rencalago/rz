# The Ruins and the Silay Heritage Houses, Negros Occidental

Negros Occidental holds two of the most photogenic heritage stops in the country within 15 kilometers of each other — The Ruins in Talisay City and the ancestral house cluster of Silay City. The Ruins is the skeletal mansion of Don Mariano Ledesma Lacson built in the early 1900s for his late wife Maria Braga, burned during World War II to prevent its use by Japanese forces, and left as a concrete-and-steel shell that now looks like it belongs in Italy. Silay, called the Paris of Negros, has 30-plus declared heritage houses from the sugar boom era, most walkable along Cinco de Noviembre Street.

You can do both in one day from Bacolod City, or slower in two days with food stops. The route is flat, tricycles and taxis are easy, and all three main museums are cash-friendly and do not require advance booking except for small groups at Hofileña. Think of it as Vigan of the south, but quieter and with better cake.

Here is how to visit The Ruins and the Silay heritage houses, what each stop costs, where to eat, and how to get there from Cebu via Bacolod, with practical numbers for 2026.

## 1. The Ruins, Talisay City — The Taj Mahal of Negros

**What it is:** The Lacson family mansion built in the early 1920s, mixing Italianate and American colonial design. Locals call it Taj Mahal of Negros because Lacson built it in memory of his wife. The structure is 903 square meters, all concrete with baroque columns, a long veranda, and a garden that lights up at dusk.

**Where:** Don Mariano L. Lacson Highway, Brgy. Zone 15, Talisay City, about 14 km north of Bacolod downtown, 20 to 30 minutes by taxi. You will see signs after the Talisay boundary.

**Hours:** Daily 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM, last entry around 7:30 PM. Best light is 4:00 PM to 6:30 PM — sunset through the columns is why most people come.

**Entrance fees (2025-2026 rates):**
- Adults: PHP 100 to PHP 150
- Seniors/PWD/Students with ID: PHP 60 to PHP 100
- Children: PHP 20 to PHP 50
Fees shift slightly year to year and some ticket stubs include a small drink. Bring cash; cards are usually not accepted. If you eat at the on-site restaurant, there is sometimes a consumable option around PHP 300.

**Best for:** Sunset photos, heritage fans, and first-timers to Negros who want one iconic shot.

**What to do:**
- Walk the full perimeter — front facade for the classic shot, back for the fountain ruins.
- Stay until lights turn on around 6:00 PM. The structure is lit and reflects on the garden.
- Order a drink at the garden cafe and sit on the side benches, less crowded than the front.
- Taxi from Bacolod costs PHP 200 to PHP 400 one-way. Jeepney + tricycle is cheaper: jeepney from Bacolod North Terminal to Talisay PHP 15-25, then tricycle PHP 30-50 to the gate.

## 2. Silay City Heritage Houses — Walkable Sugar Baron Block

Silay City is 15 minutes north of The Ruins and 30 minutes north of Bacolod. The heritage zone is centered on Cinco de Noviembre Street and Rizal Street. You can walk three major houses in 2 to 3 hours.

### Balay Negrense Museum (Victor Gaston Ancestral House)

The house of Victor Gaston, son of Frenchman Yves Leopold Germain Gaston who pioneered sugar cane in Negros. Built in 1897-1898 using balayong hardwood, coral stone, and metal sheets. Inside: period furniture, kitchen tools, old sugar labor photos, and a second-floor lifestyle museum.

- **Address:** Barangay III, Cinco de Noviembre St., Silay City
- **Hours:** Monday to Sunday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, sometimes closed noon on holidays
- **Entrance:** PHP 60 adults, PHP 30 children/students, some reports PHP 50-100 depending on maintenance fee updates
- **Best for:** First heritage house if you have never been to Silay — most curated.
- **What to do:** Look for the sliding capiz windows and the ventanillas below. Ask the guide about the azotea and the old kitchen.

### Manuel Severino Hofileña Heritage House (Hofileña Museum)

A 1934 sugar-era house that is also an art museum and lived in by the Hofileña family. Opened as a museum in 1962, it is famous because owner Ramon Hofileña personally gives the tour when he is home — art, antiques, Japanese occupation memorabilia, and works by masters like Juan Luna, Felix Resurreccion Hidalgo, Amorsolo, plus world memorabilia. You cannot photograph inside.

- **Address:** Cinco de Noviembre St., a few blocks from Balay Negrense
- **Hours:** By appointment is ideal, but walk-ins are allowed daily except Monday, 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (034) 495-5093 or message the Facebook page before going.
- **Entrance:** PHP 60 to PHP 150 regular adult, depending on season and donation request. Students PHP 30-60. Recent travel guides list PHP 150 as standard.
- **Best for:** Art lovers and travelers who like stories — the guided talk lasts 1 to 2 hours and is worth it.
- **What to do:** Listen to the full tour, then ask about the collection of vintage toys and the letters from World War II. No tripod, no photos inside.

### Bernardino Jalandoni Museum (Pink House)

A 1908 stilt house painted pink, owned by the family of Gen. Bernardino Lopez Jalandoni. It shows early 20th-century life and original furniture, dolls, and a carriage.

- **Address:** Rizal St., Silay City Heritage Zone
- **Hours:** Daily except Monday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- **Entrance:** PHP 50 to PHP 150 adult (guides list PHP 50 recently, some list PHP 150). Confirm at gate.
- **Best for:** A quick third stop — smaller than Balay Negrense, very photogenic outside.

Silay also has Golez House, Ledesma Mansion, and Silay City Public Market if you have extra time. San Diego Pro-Cathedral, a white dome church from 1927, is 5 minutes walk from the museums.

## 3. Food Around Talisay and Silay

Negros is sugar country, so desserts are the headline.

- **Napoleones:** Layers of puff with custard and sugar glaze. Original from Roli's and Pendy's. Buy boxes for PHP 180-300 per box of 6-10. Best eaten same day, but it keeps 2 days chilled.
- **Piaya:** Flat, filled with muscovado sugar. BongBong's and Merci pasalubong stores at Bacolod North Terminal sell fresh packs PHP 80-140.
- **Chicken Inasal:** Bacolod's icon. Manokan Country at SM Bacolod or old Manokan Country near Rizal St. — inasal paa PHP 120-180, pecho PHP 140-200 with garlic rice.
- **El Ideal Bakery in Silay:** Since 1920, heritage bakery near the heritage houses. Try fresh guapple pie and torta.
- **Cafes in Silay:** Casa Gamboa, Cafe 1925, and heritage cafes along Cinco de Noviembre serve lunch sets PHP 180-300.

**Best for:** Bringing pasalubong back to Cebu — piaya and napoleones travel well if you keep them flat.

**What to do:** Eat inasal in Bacolod before or after Silay, pick up napoleones after The Ruins, and buy piaya last day before airport.

## 4. Getting There from Cebu to Bacolod, Talisay and Silay

Bacolod-Silay Airport (BCD) is in Silay City itself — convenient, you land right where the heritage houses are.

**Fastest — Flight Cebu to Bacolod:**
- **CEB to BCD:** Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines fly 4-6 times daily, 50 minutes to 1 hour direct.
- One-way fares: PHP 1,500 to PHP 5,500 standard, promo as low as PHP 188 base fare during festivals. Roundtrip often PHP 3,000-7,000 if booked 2-3 weeks out.
- Airport to Bacolod city: van or taxi PHP 150-200 per person shared, PHP 600-900 private, 45-60 minutes.
- Airport to Silay heritage houses: tricycle or taxi 10-15 minutes, PHP 50-150. You can drop luggage at Silay and start tour same day.

**Budget — Bus + Ferry Cebu to Bacolod:**
Two main RoRo routes:
- **Via Toledo-San Carlos:** From Cebu South Bus Terminal, bus to Toledo Port 2-2.5 hours, FastCat/RoRo ferry Toledo to San Carlos 1.5-2 hours PHP 240 plus terminal fee PHP 20, then Ceres bus San Carlos to Bacolod 2.5-3 hours PHP 200-250. Total time 7-8 hours, total cost around PHP 700-900 aircon.
- **Via Bato-Mabinay (south route):** Bus Cebu to Bato Port via Carcar/Barili 3-3.5 hours, small ferry Bato to Tampi PHP 120 plus fee PHP 20, then bus Tampi to Bacolod via Mabinay/Kabankalan 5-6 hours. Total 9-10 hours, around PHP 780 bus plus ferry.
- **Ceres bus direct Cebu to Bacolod:** PHP 600 aircon regular via Canlaon. The bus includes ferry ticket. Search Ceres Bacolod South schedule — usually 4 AM and 3 PM daily.

**From Bacolod City to The Ruins and Silay:**
- Bacolod to The Ruins: taxi/Grab PHP 200-400, jeepney PHP 15-25 + tricycle PHP 30-45.
- Bacolod to Silay heritage houses: Ceres bus from North Terminal PHP 25-35, 30 minutes, or taxi PHP 400-600.
- Silay to The Ruins: tricycle PHP 80-150.
- Day hire: car rental PHP 2,000-3,500 per day with driver, ideal if you combine Bacolod city tour.

## 5. Best Time to Visit and Sample Costs

**Best months:** October to May is dry season. October has MassKara Festival in Bacolod — crowded but most fun, book flights early. November to February is cooler for walking Silay. March to May is hot but clear for sunset at The Ruins. June to September is rainy — gardens are greener but photos are cloudier, and some heritage houses close Monday.

**Sample day tour budget per person from Bacolod (excluding Cebu flights):**
- Bacolod to Silay roundtrip bus: PHP 70
- Tricycles in Silay + to The Ruins: PHP 150-250
- Balay Negrense: PHP 60
- Hofileña Museum: PHP 60-150
- Jalandoni Museum: PHP 50-150
- The Ruins entrance: PHP 100-150
- Lunch and snacks: PHP 300-500
- Pasalubong piaya + napoleones: PHP 300-500
- Total day: PHP 1,000-1,800 if you commute, PHP 2,500-3,500 if you hire a car with 2-3 pax splitting.

**Two-day stay:** Add hotel in Bacolod PHP 1,500-3,500 per night (Go Hotels, Seda, boutique houses in Silay like Casa Gamboa). Bacolod-Silay Airport is close so last-day flight is easy.

## Tips Before You Go

### Book Bacolod-Silay flights early for October

MassKara is second to third week of October. Cebu to Bacolod fares triple close to festival. If you go then, book 3 weeks ahead and Silay hotels 1 month ahead.

### Do Hofileña first and call ahead

Ramon Hofileña is in his 80s and tours are personal. Call (034) 495-5093 morning of visit. If he is not available, his brother or caretaker still tours but it is shorter. Weekdays are quieter.

### Sunset is non-negotiable at The Ruins

Go 4:00 PM onward. Before 3 PM it is harsh and hot, garden has little shade. After 5:30 PM crowd thins, lights turn on around 6 PM. Wear light colors for photos against gray concrete.

### Bring cash, small bills

Heritage houses and tricycles are cash-only. Silay ATMs are near plaza; Bacolod has more. Keep coins for jeepney.

### You can base in Silay, not Bacolod

If heritage is your focus, stay in Silay (Casa Gamboa Boutique Hotel or Balay Florentina). Walking distance to museums, airport 10 minutes. Bacolod is better for food and nightlife.

## So, Is The Ruins and Silay Worth a Trip from Cebu?

Yes if you want heritage that you can finish in a day without a long trek. It pairs well with a Bacolod food weekend — chicken inasal, piaya, napoleones — and flights from Cebu are under an hour. From Mactan you land in Silay, tricycle to the heritage houses, taxi 15 minutes to The Ruins for sunset, eat in Bacolod at night, and fly back next day with pasalubong that still fits carry-on.

Work With Me

Ren Calago 26 October 2026

I build websites, fix SEO and run Facebook ads for small tourism businesses: resorts, cafés, restaurants, homestays and tour operators in Cebu, Dumaguete and Negros Oriental. If travellers can't find you online, or find you and then book somewhere else, this is the kind of problem I work on.


You already know this blog. It is where I research and write travel guides about these same towns, so I know what visitors search for before they go, and what they need to see on a page before they message you.


What I can do for your business


A website that gets bookings


A fast, mobile-friendly website built in WordPress with Elementor, with clear prices or packages, photos that load quickly, directions, and one obvious way to book or message you. I can build a new site, rework an old one, or set up a simple WooCommerce shop if you sell products or vouchers online. I have more than five years of hands-on WordPress experience.


SEO so travellers find you


An honest review of how your business shows up on Google, followed by fixes in order of what matters most: page titles and descriptions, page speed, your Google Business Profile, and pages that answer the questions travellers actually type. SEO takes months, not days, and nobody can promise a first-page ranking. What I can promise is clear work, a written plan and regular updates on what has moved.


Facebook ads that are worth the money


An audit of the ads you are already running, with a plain list of what is wasting budget and what to change, or full setup and management for a new campaign. Facebook is still where many travellers in the Philippines discover resorts and cafés, so a small, well-targeted budget can go further than you might expect.


A feature on Ren Calago Blog


If you run a place that fits this blog, it can be featured in a story. Paid features are always labelled as sponsored, and I only write what I can verify, so readers keep trusting what they read here. That trust is what makes a feature worth having.


How it works


  1. Send a message. Tell me about your business, where it is and what you want to fix.
  2. Free first look. I check your current website, Google listing or ads and reply with what I would do first.
  3. A written quote. You get the scope, the price and the timeline in writing before any work starts.
  4. The work, with updates. You see progress along the way, and nothing goes live without your approval.

Who this is for


Small, independent businesses that depend on visitors: a beach resort in Moalboal, a café in Dumaguete, a homestay in Siquijor, a dive shop, a tour operator or a new restaurant that wants to be found. If you are not sure whether your project fits, ask anyway. I would rather say no early than waste your time.


Common questions


How much does it cost?


It depends on the size of the job, so every project gets its own written quote after the free first look. There are no hidden fees, and you know the full price before you agree to anything.


Do I need to be in Cebu or Dumaguete?


No. Most of the work is done online, and I work flexible hours across time zones. Businesses in these areas are simply the ones I know best.


Can you guarantee more bookings?


No one honestly can. Bookings depend on your place, your prices and your season as much as your website. I will tell you plainly what I think will help most, and show you the numbers so you can judge the results yourself.


Let's talk


Email me at rencalago@gmail.com or send a message on Facebook. A short note is enough: your business name, where you are, and what you would like help with. If you only want to suggest a place for the blog, the contact page is the right spot.

Michelin Guide Cebu 2027 Results: Full List and New Entries

Ren Calago 27 October 2026 Updated 29 October 20260

The Michelin Guide Manila and Environs & Cebu 2027 was revealed on the evening of 29 October 2026, and Cebu still has no starred restaurant. The island's count did grow, though: 20 Cebu restaurants made the second edition, up from 18, with seven Bib Gourmands and thirteen Michelin Selected places.


The biggest Cebu story is in the Bib Gourmand list. Ato-ah in IT Park moved up from Selected, and two new names joined it: Kalipay in Banawa and Tatang's Lechon in Lahug. Three restaurants were added to the Selected list, and three from last year's Cebu list are missing this time.


Here is the full 2027 Cebu list, what changed from 2026, and how to plan meals around it.




Cebu in the 2027 guide at a glance


  • Michelin Stars: none in Cebu. All ten starred restaurants in the 2027 guide are in Metro Manila and nearby provinces.
  • Bib Gourmand: 7 in Cebu (6 in 2026)
  • Michelin Selected: 13 in Cebu (12 in 2026)
  • New Bib Gourmands: Ato-ah (up from Selected), Kalipay, Tatang's Lechon
  • New Selected: Kokeek's Manokan, Kumedor, Playa
  • Not in the 2027 list: CUR8, Pares Batchoy Food House, Pares Pares

Across both regions, the second edition lists 127 establishments: one two-star restaurant, nine one-star restaurants, 31 Bib Gourmands and 86 Selected. For the starred side of the guide, see our Michelin Guide Manila 2027 results.



Cebu's Bib Gourmand restaurants for 2027


A Bib Gourmand marks very good cooking at a reasonable price. For most visitors this is the part of the list worth planning around, since none of these places requires a tasting-menu budget.


  • Esmen – F. Rallos Street, Pasil. Kept its Bib. Known for larang, the spicy, sour fish soup that Cebuanos eat at any hour.
  • The Pig & Palm – MSY Tower, Cebu Business Park. Kept its Bib.
  • Abaseria Deli and Cafe – Kasambagan, Cebu City. Kept its Bib.
  • Lasa – Busay, Cebu City. Kept its Bib.
  • Ato-ah – 38 Park Avenue, Cebu IT Park. New Bib Gourmand, moved up from Selected.
  • Kalipay – R. Duterte Street, Banawa, Cebu City. New this year.
  • Tatang's Lechon – Wilson Street, Apas, Lahug, Cebu City. New this year.

Tatang's Lechon is the first lechon house in Cebu to earn a Bib. If you're building a lechon day around it, our guide on where to eat lechon in Cebu lists more places to compare, including House of Lechon, which stays on the Selected list.



Cebu's Michelin Selected restaurants for 2027


Selected means the inspectors recommend a restaurant without giving it a star or a Bib. Cebu's 13 are:


  • House of Lechon – Acacia and Tojong Streets, Cebu City
  • Abli – A. Climaco Street, Cebu City
  • Maya – Crossroads, Banilad Road, Cebu City
  • Socarrat – Bonifacio District, Cebu City
  • Sialo – Pres. Laurel Street, Cebu City
  • Kamakura – Greenhills Road, Mandaue City
  • Enye by Chele Gonzalez – Crimson Resort and Spa Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City
  • DIP – Sheraton Cebu Mactan Resort, Punta Engaño Road, Lapu-Lapu City
  • COCO – Punta Engaño Road, Lapu-Lapu City
  • Lantaw – Guiwanon, Compostela
  • Kokeek's Manokan – new this year
  • Kumedor – Red Coco Town, Lapu-Lapu City, new this year
  • Playa – new this year

Kokeek's Manokan, a chicken place as the name suggests, puts another everyday Cebuano meal on the list next to Esmen's larang and the island's lechon. Mactan now has four Selected restaurants, which makes it easier to eat well near the airport and the resorts.



Who left the list


CUR8 and Pares Batchoy Food House, both Bib Gourmands last year, and Pares Pares, a Selected restaurant in 2026, are not in the 2027 guide. Michelin reviews every restaurant fresh each year, so a missing listing doesn't mean a place has closed or that the food has gone downhill.



Planning a Michelin food trip in Cebu


Group the restaurants by area


The 20 restaurants are spread across Cebu City, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu and Compostela. Grouping them by area saves you from spending your trip in traffic:


  • Downtown and uptown: Esmen in Pasil, House of Lechon and Abli. Good for lunch while you're seeing the old city.
  • Lahug, IT Park and Banilad: Tatang's Lechon, Ato-ah, Maya, Sialo and Abaseria are within a short ride of each other. Kalipay in Banawa is a short ride away.
  • Mactan: Enye, DIP, COCO and Kumedor, which suit an island stay or the night before a flight.
  • Day trips: Lasa is up in Busay, so pair it with the hill viewpoints. Lantaw is in Compostela, north of Mandaue, and Kamakura in Mandaue is on the way.

Book ahead, especially in December


New and upgraded places tend to fill up right after an announcement, and the holiday season makes it worse. For Ato-ah, Kalipay and Tatang's Lechon, call ahead or book a table if they take reservations.


Check hours and prices directly


Menus, opening hours and prices often change once a restaurant gets more attention. Check each place's official page or message them before making a long trip across the city for one meal.


Cebu is still waiting for its first star, but the 2027 list is a better eating guide than last year's. It has more Bibs, more local cooking and more choice on Mactan, from a bowl of larang in Pasil to a plate of Tatang's lechon in Lahug. If you're new to Cebuano food, our list of Cebu's best local food beyond lechon covers what else to order.

Lalimar Beach Resort in La Libertad: What to Expect

Ren Calago 14 Feb 2023 Updated 22 October 20260

Lalimar Beach Resort is a beachfront resort in San Jose, La Libertad, on the Tañon Strait coast of northern Negros Oriental. It has rooms and cottages for overnight guests, day-use cottages, an infinity pool facing the water, a restaurant and function space for events. This guide covers what to expect, what to confirm before you book and how to get there.


It works best as a quiet weekend stay or a stopover on the way up the coast. The resort is the main reason to be in this part of town, and options outside it are limited, so plan to spend your time on its grounds.


The infinity pool is the centrepiece. It faces the Tañon Strait to the east, which makes early morning the time to be there for sunrise over the water.



Rooms, Cottages and Day Use


Overnight guests stay in cottage-style rooms. When you book, ask which ones have air-conditioning and which face the water, since the units are not all the same.


Day guests can use the day-use cottages. Ask for the day-use and pool fees before you go, and whether renting a cottage or an umbrella changes the price. Rates and availability change, so confirm them directly by phone or by messaging the resort on Facebook rather than trusting an old listing.


What to Eat


The restaurant serves Filipino and Italian dishes, including pizza. Our personal favourites are the shrimp gambas, fried chicken, carbonara and margherita pizza. Because dining options outside the resort are limited, plan your meals around its kitchen and ask about kitchen hours if you arrive late.


Weddings, Birthdays and Big Events


Lalimar is a favourite venue for weddings and birthdays, with function space that can handle large groups. On event weekends the grounds are busier and the music louder. Ask whether anything is booked on your dates, and request a room away from the function area if you want a quiet night.



Before You Book


Ask whether the pool is open on your dates. Bring insect repellent for the evenings, and pack water shoes if you plan to go into the sea. Ask staff about the shoreline and the tide before you do.


A Simple Plan for a Day or a Night


For a day visit, arrive before lunch, eat at the restaurant, spend the afternoon at the pool and head home in good light. Settle how you will get back before you relax, since a long ride is still ahead of you.


For a night, check in by the afternoon, swim, have dinner on the grounds and set an early alarm for the sunrise. Families with young children should ask about the depth of the pool and whether it is supervised.


When to Go


The driest month is February, and the wet season runs from about June to September. Daytime highs sit around 29 to 32°C for most of the year. Drier months suit a pool and beach stay and the road trip north, while wet-season visitors should keep a spare day in the plan in case rain disrupts the journey.


The town fiesta, Pandanyag, falls on 28 April. If you are visiting around then, ask about room availability early.


What Else to Do Nearby


La Libertad's caves are the other draw, and they are best arranged through the municipal tourism office or the barangay rather than on the day. A night at the resort followed by a morning cave visit makes an easy two-day trip, and the details are in the La Libertad guide linked below.


How to Get There


La Libertad is about 105 kilometres north of Dumaguete. Buses heading north take roughly three hours, so leave early if you want the afternoon at the pool, and tell the conductor you are getting off at Lalimar in San Jose. From Cebu, the Tangil to Bulado ferry lands you in Guihulngan, from where you continue south along the national highway. Our La Libertad guide has the sailing times, fares and a two-day plan.


If you are building a longer trip, our posts on Negros Oriental weekend getaways and the towns beyond Dumaguete show what else fits around a stay here.


A Valentine's Dinner at Lalimar


On Valentine's night in 2023, the resort ran a special dinner for couples with a set price for two. We had wine on the table, a band serenading the guests and a platform decorated for photos. We arrived before the music started and left early, because our two-year-old son was already testing the limits of the table. It rained, but the food was good and it made for a lovely night. Ask whether a similar package is offered near Valentine's Day.



Is Lalimar Worth a Stay?


It makes sense if you want a pool, a sunrise view over the strait and a restaurant on the same grounds, all within reach of a weekend. It makes less sense if you want shops and cafes outside your door, or a guaranteed quiet night on an event weekend. Call ahead, ask the questions above and you will know which kind of stay to expect.

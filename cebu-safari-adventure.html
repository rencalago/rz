Cebu Safari and Adventure Park in Carmen: Fees, Hours and Visit Tips

Ren Calago 18 Feb 2023 Updated 22 October 2026

Cebu Safari and Adventure Park is a hillside wildlife park in Barangay Corte, Carmen, in northern Cebu. The short version for planning: go between Wednesday and Sunday, arrive in the morning, and budget for more than the entrance ticket. The bird show and the African Savanna tour do not run on Mondays and Tuesdays, and the rides are charged separately.


Carmen is a long way from the city, so this is a full-day trip rather than a quick stop. This guide covers what tickets cost, which days the shows run, how to reach the park by car or by bus, and how our own day went with a toddler in tow.


We visited in June 2022, and the fees and schedules below have been updated for 2026. Prices at parks like this change often, so confirm them before you set out.





How Much Are the Tickets at Cebu Safari?


Regular entrance is priced by height, not age. Children under 2 feet get in free, children between 2 and 3 feet pay half, and anyone 3 feet or taller pays the full adult rate. Senior citizens and persons with disabilities get 20% off with a valid ID, and that discount is usually applied at the park's ticket counter rather than on booking apps.


Adult prices have been shifting. Expect roughly ₱900 to ₱1,300 on a weekday and about ₱200 more on weekends and holidays, with booking platforms adding a fee on top. When we went in 2022, a weekday ticket was ₱900 and weekends were ₱1,100. Call the park at (032) 520 4800 or email reservations@cebusafari.ph for the current ticket board before you travel.


Your entrance ticket covers the animal areas, the shuttle between them, the bird show, the white lion safari, Michel's Garden and the Gardens of the World. Rides are a separate expense: an ATV ride, sky bike, giant swing, zip coaster and spider web. Rides start from about ₱300, and the ATV costs more, so families who want to ride should add that to the budget.


When Is Cebu Safari Open, and Which Days Are Best?


The gates open around 8:00 to 8:30 AM, last admission is about 1:30 PM, and the park closes at 5:00 PM. Arriving late morning leaves you a short day, so aim for the opening hour.


The day of the week matters more than the hour. The bird show runs Wednesday to Saturday at 1:45 PM, and on Sundays and holidays at 10:45 AM and 1:45 PM. The African Savanna tour runs Wednesday to Sunday between 10:00 AM and 3:30 PM. On a Monday or Tuesday you would still see the big cats, the giraffes and the gardens, but you would miss two of the park's headline attractions. Show times can change, so ask at the ticket counter when you buy your pass.

How to Get to Cebu Safari From Cebu City


By car, expect 1.5 to 2 hours heading north on the national highway. Watch for Carmen National High School and the park's signage, then follow the road uphill to the gate. The entrance sits well away from the main road, which is why most visitors come by private vehicle or hired van.


By public transport, take a north-bound bus from the Cebu North Bus Terminal to Carmen Plaza, and tell the conductor where you are headed. From the plaza, a van or habal-habal covers the last stretch for roughly ₱100 to ₱150 per person. Leave early, because the full journey by bus can run 2.5 to 3 hours each way. Whatever you use for the final leg, arrange your ride back before you go in, since the park is far from the highway.


A Route That Worked for Us


We began with the bird show, ate at a restaurant nearby, then took the park shuttle to see the white lion. The ride there was scenic and the distance was longer than we expected. From there we shuttled down to the African savanna, walked on to the crocodile area, and carried on to the giraffes.


We took the long trail from the giraffes back toward the bird show area, passing Michel's Garden and its children's playground. We skipped the playground because the afternoon was almost over. Near the end there is a collection of animal statues that made for good photos.


Show times should drive your route. Do the African Savanna tour in the morning and be back at the bird show area before 1:45 PM. The park is large, and the shuttle stops at every attraction, so use it rather than walking between areas.


Visiting Cebu Safari With a Toddler


Our son was 1.5 years old on our visit. We left the stroller at home because the paths slope, and loading a stroller on and off the shuttle looked like more trouble than it was worth. The result was carrying him for about 80% of the day. If your child is small, bring a carrier rather than relying on your arms, and ask at the gate whether strollers can ride the shuttle.


Outside food and drinks are not allowed, but an empty tumbler to refill with water is fine. Several restaurants operate inside, so plan to eat there. Comfortable closed shoes make the slopes much easier.


Is Cebu Safari Worth the Trip?


It is worth it if you can give it a full day, travel with a vehicle, and time the visit for a show day. It is a weaker fit for a half-day or a Monday visit, and for families with babies who cannot walk the slopes. We would go back when our son is about five years old, old enough to walk the trails and appreciate the animals.

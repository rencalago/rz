About Ren Calago

Ren Calago 22 May 2026

Welcome to Ren Calago Blog — a space built to celebrate the best of local travel, hidden food finds, and the everyday adventures around the Philippines.


Why this blog exists


I started this blog to share stories from places that inspire me: mountain cafes, seaside resorts, festivals, and small businesses with big heart. Whether you are planning your next trip or just looking for a fresh idea, this site is meant to spark curiosity and a sense of discovery.


What you will find here


Expect honest travel tips, local restaurant and cafe recommendations, and photos from spots I have visited in Cebu, Negros Oriental, and beyond. I also share thoughtful notes on life, inspiration, and the small moments that make travel meaningful.


Explore with me


If you have a new place, event, or business that deserves to be featured, I would love to hear from you. This blog grows through the people and communities I meet, and every story is an opportunity to discover something new.


Follow along on social media or visit the Contact page to send a message, share a recommendation, or inquire about collaborations.

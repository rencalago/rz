About Ren Calago

Ren Calago 22 May 2026

Ren Calago Blog is a travel blog about the Philippines, written and run by Ren Calago. It started with Cebu and Negros Oriental and has grown into a library of guides that now reaches from Batanes in the far north down to Mindanao.


The aim is simple: give you the information you need to plan a real trip, written plainly, with the costs, the crowds and the catches included.


What you will find here


Most of the blog is practical travel guides. There are Cebu guides for the city, the south coast and the islands up north, Dumaguete guides for the food, the budget and the day trips, and Negros Oriental guides for the towns, caves and beaches beyond the city. The Luzon guides and the stories on Bohol, Siquijor, Palawan, the rest of the Visayas and Mindanao cover the wider country.


Alongside the guides you will find festival coverage, from Sinulog and MassKara to smaller town fiestas, plus food trips, cafe and resort write-ups, and local news that affects travellers, such as transport strikes, weather warnings and new rules at popular sites.


How the guides are put together


Every guide is researched before it is written. Facts such as entrance fees, opening hours, boat and bus schedules, permits and festival dates are checked against official sources wherever possible: tourism offices, local government units, the operators themselves and established news outlets. When a detail cannot be pinned down, the guide tells you to confirm it on the day rather than guessing.


Prices and rules in the Philippines change often, sometimes from one month to the next. Each post shows the date it was published, and an "Updated" date when it has been revised, so you can judge how fresh the information is. If you spot something that has changed, a fee that went up or a ferry that no longer runs, please say so on the contact page. Corrections are welcome and get fixed.


Tools to help you plan


The site also has a few free planning tools: a trip budget calculator, a festival calendar, a packing checklist, a quiz to help you choose where to go and a travel map of every story by province. You can also download the free printable travel guides.


Get in touch


If you know a place, an event or a small business that deserves a feature, or you run one, I would love to hear about it. This blog grows through the people and communities it covers. Send a message through the contact page or find Ren Calago Blog on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and X.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026


This page explains what information Ren Calago Blog (www.rencalago.com) collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. The blog is written and run by Ren Calago in the Philippines. If anything here is unclear, write to rencalago@gmail.com.



Information you give us


You never need an account to read the blog. Some features only work if you choose to share details:


  • Free travel guides sign-up. Your first name (if you give it), your email address, the guides you picked, the date and the page you signed up on. These are kept in a private Google Sheet that only the blog owner can open.
  • Comments. The name you type, your comment and, if you choose to add it, your email address. Every comment is checked by hand before it appears. Only your name and comment are published; your email address is never shown and is only used to tell you when there is a reply.
  • Messages. If you email, call or message the blog through social media, your message and contact details are used to reply to you.


Information collected automatically


The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read, which device and browser are used, and roughly where readers are from (country or city level). Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. The data is used only to understand what readers find useful and is not used to identify you. You can read how Google handles this data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.



Advertising and cookies


Ren Calago Blog shows ads served by Google AdSense to help cover the cost of running the site.


  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at www.aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • More detail is in Google's page on how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. The blog still works without them, though ads may be less relevant to you.



Booking widgets and embedded content


Some articles include booking boxes from Klook (tours and activities) and Agoda (hotels), and some photos load from Google Drive. When these load, those companies may collect information such as your IP address and set their own cookies, under their own privacy policies. If you book through one of these boxes, the blog may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you; see the Disclaimer.



How information is used and shared


Your details are used to send the guides you asked for, occasional travel tips and new guides, to manage comments and to reply to messages. Ren Calago Blog does not sell, rent or trade your personal information. It is only shared with the services that make the site work (Google for analytics, ads, email and storage) or when the law requires it.



How long information is kept


Sign-up and comment details are kept for as long as you stay subscribed or your comment stays on the site. When you unsubscribe or ask for deletion, your details are removed. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics retention settings.



Your choices and rights


You can unsubscribe from emails at any time by replying "unsubscribe". You can also ask to see, correct or delete the personal information the blog holds about you by writing to rencalago@gmail.com. Readers in the Philippines have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), and readers elsewhere may have similar rights under their local laws.



Children


This blog is written for adults planning travel. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal details, write in and they will be deleted.



Changes to this policy


This policy may be updated when the site adds or removes a feature. The date at the top shows when it last changed.



Contact


Questions about privacy can go to rencalago@gmail.com or through the contact page.

Tops Cebu Lookout at Sunrise: Our Visit to Busay Before the Renovation

Ren Calago 10 Feb 2023 Updated 22 October 20260

We were lucky to reach Tops a few months before it closed to visitors. We drove up the Busay mountain road a few minutes before sunrise, and by the time we parked, the sun was slowly coming up from behind the clouds over the city. It was a Sunday, early enough that only a handful of people were around, and it was cold enough that we did not stay long.


Tops is the lookout on the hills of Busay, above Cebu City, and for decades it has been the place locals take visitors for the widest view of Metro Cebu. This post is our record of the old Tops at dawn, before the rebuild, plus what we would tell anyone planning their own sunrise trip now that it has reopened.


If you want current prices, the shuttle and opening hours, our Tops Cebu Lookout in 2026 guide has the practical details. This page is about the early-morning side of the place.




What the old Tops felt like at dawn


Apart from the view, there was not much to do at the old Tops, and early in the morning that was the appeal. No music, no queue, just a stone deck, the cool air, and the city below still half asleep. The original 1985 structure, with its stone arches and hexagonal floor tiles, gave the place an older, slightly worn charm.


The city skyline and the buildings below look small from up here, but you can pick out landmarks if you take your time. Binoculars help. On a clear morning the view runs across Cebu City toward the Mactan channel and Mactan island, with the mountains behind you.


Why Tops closed, and what changed


Tops first opened in 1985. It closed for a major renovation in late 2022, and the developer set out to give it a brand new, world-class look. It reopened in February 2024 as a much bigger site, with a rebuilt deck, a five-storey building with food outlets, and an open-air concert area. The view is still the reason to go, but the quiet hilltop park we saw is now a busier destination, especially in the evening.




Sunrise or sunset at Tops?


Most people go up for the city lights after dark, and the dusk crowd is the busiest part of the day. Sunrise is the opposite. You trade the glow of the city at night for soft light over the rooftops, cooler air and far fewer people.


Go at sunrise if you want a calm deck, cleaner photos without a crowd in the frame, and the chance to watch the city wake up. Go at dusk if you want food, a livelier atmosphere and the moment the lights come on. The site is open around the clock, but check the shuttle schedule in our 2026 guide before you plan an early start.


Tips for a sunrise visit


A sunrise trip means driving or riding up Busay in the dark. The road climbs steadily with tight bends, so take it slowly, use your headlights early, and watch for motorbikes coming down. If you are not driving yourself, book your ride the night before rather than hoping to find one at four in the morning. These are the things that made our early start easier:


  • Arrive before first light. Sunrise goes quickly, and the colour is best in the few minutes before the sun clears the clouds.
  • Bring a jacket. The hilltop sits at around 610 metres and early mornings are noticeably colder than the city.
  • Check the sky the night before. Low cloud can hide the city completely, especially in the rainy months.
  • Pack water and a snack, since food outlets may not all be open at dawn.
  • Wear proper shoes. Parts of the deck are dim, and some changes in level are easy to miss in low light.



Photography at Tops


If you are into photography, Tops is one of the easiest places in Cebu to get a wide city shot. At sunrise, shoot toward the light as the sky changes, then turn to the city once the sun is up and the buildings catch it. A small tripod helps with long exposures, whether that is the city lights before dawn or the light trails after dark. We also think it is a good spot to watch a downtown fireworks display. The bursts look tiny from this distance, but seeing a whole show over the city at once is a different experience.


Making a morning of it in Busay


A sunrise at Tops leaves the rest of the day free. Busay has other hilltop stops nearby, and our Busay hills day trip with Temple of Leah and Sirao Garden shows how to link them. Weather matters a lot for any viewpoint, so it is worth reading our best time to visit Cebu guide before picking a date.


We would happily go back for another sunrise now that the new Tops is open, just to see how the dawn view compares with what we saw from the old deck.

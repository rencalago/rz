Where Should You Go in the Philippines?

Ren Calago

Answer six quick questions about your trip and get three destination matches, each with a guide to help you plan. There are no wrong answers, and you can change any of them to see the results shift.



How the Matches Work


Each destination is scored on what it does best, how far it is from the main airports, how much a typical trip costs and how it handles the season you pick. The top three scores win. It is a starting point, not a final answer, so read the guide before you book.


Once you have a pick, see what it might cost with the trip budget calculator, check the festival calendar for events during your dates, and pack with the packing checklist.
